# 《半妖司藤》第三章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件按源文本中的 `第③章` 整理。
- 排版规则为“一句中文，一句英文”；遇到较长句子时，补充语法说明和常用短语。

安蔓脑子再乱，也知道开夜路危险，尤其是盘山道，当地人称“九十九道盘，鬼走也难”，具体有没有九十九道没数过，但是上一道盘陡过一道，整个呈螺旋锥样绕十几座山上去，最顶上那道说是万丈悬崖一点都不过分。  
No matter how chaotic（混乱的） An Man's mind was, she still knew that driving mountain roads at night was dangerous, especially on a winding mountain pass. Locals called it "ninety-nine bends, hard even for ghosts to cross." No one had counted whether there were truly ninety-nine, but each switchback was steeper than the last, spiraling up more than a dozen mountains like a giant cone, and calling the topmost bend a sheer ten-thousand-foot cliff was no exaggeration.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`让步状语从句`、`比较结构`、`动名词作主语`。<br>
句子主干：主句核心是 `she still knew that driving mountain roads at night was dangerous`。<br>
难点拆解：`No matter how chaotic ...` 是让步状语从句，表示“脑子再乱也知道”；`each switchback was steeper than the last` 是比较结构；后半段把原文连续地形描写拆成几个短层次，让英文更清楚但仍保留压迫感。<br>
常用短语：
- `winding mountain pass`：盘山道
- `switchback`：盘山急弯
- `spiral up`：盘旋向上
- `no exaggeration`：一点都不夸张

上到第三十来道时，安蔓把所有的车窗都打开，寒风在车里头嗖呦嗖呦的，冻的人困意全无，有山壁上斜出的稀拉的树，陡一看都像是隐在暗处不怀好意的人，安蔓好几次心惊肉跳，后背上一层冷汗叠一层热汗的。  
By the time she reached roughly the thirtieth bend, An Man had rolled down every window. The freezing wind hissed through the car, driving every trace of drowsiness from her. Sparse trees jutted from the mountainside, and at a glance they looked like ill-intentioned figures lurking in the dark. More than once her heart lurched, cold sweat layering over hot sweat across her back.  
深夜的山里极其安静，偶尔有磔啦一声，不知道是蜷巢在哪处夜惊的鸟，已经是12月下旬，月相开始由满转半，疏淡地挂在天上，像是睁开的冷冷的眼睛，不管拐几个弯，行多少路，抬头一看，它的视线还在你身上，叫人无所遁形。  
The mountains were deathly quiet in the middle of the night. Now and then some sharp cry would split the dark, perhaps a bird startled awake in its nest. It was already late December, and the moon had begun to wane from full to half. Thin and pale in the sky, it looked like a cold eye held open. No matter how many bends you took or how far you drove, one glance upward told you its gaze was still fixed on you, leaving nowhere to hide.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`让步状语从句`、`定语后置描写`、`结果表达`。<br>
句子主干：主句落点是 `it looked like a cold eye held open` 和 `its gaze was still fixed on you`。<br>
难点拆解：英文先把山里静与偶发声响分开，再把月亮单独拉出来；`No matter how many bends you took ...` 保留原文“拐几个弯、行多少路”的追迫感；最后用 `leaving nowhere to hide` 把“无所遁形”自然收住。<br>
常用短语：
- `deathly quiet`：安静得可怕
- `split the dark`：划破黑暗
- `wane from full to half`：从满月转向半月
- `be fixed on`：盯在……身上
- `nowhere to hide`：无所遁形

这别样的仿佛置身世界尽头的安静，终于让安蔓的脑子从混沌里一点点抽离出来。  
That strange silence, as if she were driving at the end of the world, finally let An Man's mind pull itself little by little out of the fog.  
车轮胶皮摩擦着粗糙山道，她开始仔细回忆这个晚上的一切，一帧一格，像是缓缓拉出的古老胶片……  
As the rubber tires scraped along the rough mountain road, she began to replay everything that had happened that night, frame by frame, like an old reel of film being slowly drawn out.  
——喝下放了安定的茶水之后，秦放慢慢阖上眼睛……  
After drinking the tea laced with sedatives（镇静剂）, Qin Fang had slowly closed his eyes...  
——犹豫了再犹豫，伸手去敲188号的房门……  
After hesitating again and again, she had finally lifted her hand to knock on Room 188...  
——赵江龙拿着卷起的书，一下下抽她的头脸，说：“你赵哥错哪了啊，你给解释解释，解释解释……”  
Zhao Jianglong had held a rolled-up magazine and smacked her head and face again and again, saying, "What did your Brother Zhao do wrong? Explain it to me. Explain it..."  
——被赵江龙打得全无还手之力，她蜷缩着护住头脸任他拳打脚踢，肋骨挨了两脚，现在还在疼，隐隐地疼……  
Beaten by Zhao Jianglong until she could no longer fight back, she had curled up to shield her head and face while he punched and kicked her. Two kicks had landed on her ribs, and even now they still ached, a dull persistent ache...  

陡然间，安蔓浑身一颤，重重踩下了刹车，车子惯性往前冲了好几米，车轮和地面发出难听的摩擦声，前方再有几米就是悬崖，黑魆魆的山石外头，就是大片的无边无际的稀薄空气。  
All at once, An Man shuddered violently and slammed on the brakes. The car lurched forward several more meters on momentum, the tires grinding harshly against the road. A few meters farther ahead lay the cliff edge. Beyond the black mountain rock was nothing but a vast, endless stretch of thin air.  
自始至终，她根本没有碰过刀子！  
From beginning to end, she had never touched the knife at all.  
被赵江龙往死里打的时候，她试过用牙咬，用指甲去狠狠挖，穷极的时候甚至抓住茶几的腿想把茶几抡起来砸赵江龙，但是真的没有刀子，真的没有！  
When Zhao Jianglong was beating her half to death, she had tried biting, clawing with her nails, and in sheer desperation（绝境之中） had even grabbed the leg of the coffee table to try to swing it at him, but there had been no knife, truly no knife.  
那时她是傻了，屋里只有她和赵江龙两个人，赵江龙中了刀，又是那样的表情，她就以为是自己混乱间失了手，接下来方寸大乱，她居然半夜开了车逃跑。  
At the time she had been stunned senseless. There had been only her and Zhao Jianglong in the room. He had been stabbed, and he had looked at her like that, so she had assumed she herself had done it in the chaos. After that she had completely lost her head and actually fled in the middle of the night.  
跑到哪去，这是跑得了的事吗？再说了，这一跑畏罪潜逃，不是更把罪是坐实了吗？  
Where could she possibly run? Was this the kind of thing anyone could run from? And besides, by fleeing like a guilty person, wasn't she only making the crime seem all the more certain?  
安蔓有一种不祥的预感。  
A sinister premonition（不祥的预感） rose in An Man's chest.  
不行，得回去。  
No. She had to go back.  
她强迫自己冷静，深深吸一口气，准备重新发动车子。  
She forced herself to calm down, drew a deep breath, and got ready to start the car again.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`反问句`、`宾语从句`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段由 `she had assumed ...` 推到 `She had to go back`。<br>
难点拆解：两个反问句连着出现，把她开始恢复理智后的自我追问做得很有力度；`making the crime seem all the more certain` 把“把罪坐实”转换成自然英语表达；最后用极短句 `No. She had to go back.` 收紧决心。<br>
常用短语：
- `lose one's head`：乱了方寸
- `flee in the middle of the night`：半夜逃跑
- `run from something`：从某事中逃开
- `all the more certain`：显得更加确定
- `go back`：回去面对

就在这个时候，车子的后视镜里忽然灯光大亮，安蔓还没反应过来，就听轰一声巨响，巨大的撞击力迫得车子往前进了四五米，车头前探走空，安蔓怕不是以为下一刻就要坠崖，吓得尖叫不止，就在这尖叫当口，车门被猛地拽开，一个高大的男人伸手粗暴拽住她头发将她整个人拖扔在地上，安蔓头皮火辣辣疼，挣扎着撑地想站起来，那人一脚踩在她后脑勺上，把她的脸重重踩进泥土里，怒吼了句：“臭□□，货呢？”  
At that very moment, the rearview mirror suddenly flared with headlights. Before An Man could react, a thunderous crash erupted behind her. The impact shoved the car forward another four or five meters, the front end jutting out into emptiness. She was certain the next second would send her plunging over the cliff, and her screams would not stop. In the middle of those screams, the door was yanked open. A tall man grabbed her by the hair, dragged her bodily out, and threw her to the ground. Her scalp burned with pain. She struggled to push herself up, but he planted a foot on the back of her head and ground her face into the dirt, roaring, "You filthy bitch, where's the merchandise?"  

***

秦放觉得特别冷。  
Qin Fang felt unbearably cold.  
感觉上，像是床头有人放了好几台风扇，开足了马力对着他猛吹，被子也不知道什么时候掀开了，但是摸索着总也摸不到，风扇的声音咯噔咯噔又嗖呦嗖呦的，在这声音的背后，似乎很远的地方，有安蔓的惨叫声……  
It felt as though someone had set several fans by his bed and turned them to full power, blasting him without mercy. Somehow the blanket had been thrown off, yet no matter how he groped for it, he could not find it. The fans clacked and hissed, and behind those sounds, somewhere very far away, there seemed to be An Man's scream...  
秦放一个激灵，眼睛陡然睁开，身处的环境让他完全懵了，脑子里一阵阵针刺样的疼，心跳得特别厉害，有些呼吸不顺，像是高反的征兆，他挣扎着从后座上坐起来，头靠着头枕缓了一下，然后几乎是下意识地偏头朝一边的窗外看。  
Qin Fang jolted and his eyes flew open. The surroundings left him completely stunned. Needle-sharp pain stabbed through his head in waves, his heart was hammering, and his breathing came unevenly, like the onset of altitude sickness（高原反应）. He struggled upright from the back seat, rested his head against the headrest for a moment, and then, almost by instinct, turned to look out the side window.  
不远处，安蔓蜷缩着身子在地上痉挛，有个男人脚踩在她身上，手撑着膝盖似乎打累了在休息，另一个戴鸭舌帽的狠狠踢着她肚子，大声吼着：“不是你是谁，货呢？”  
Not far away, An Man was curled on the ground, convulsing. One man had a foot planted on her and his hands braced on his knees, as if he were resting after getting tired from beating her. Another, wearing a baseball cap, was kicking her viciously in the stomach and shouting, "If not you, then who? Where's the merchandise?"  
秦放下意识觉得这是梦，但即便是在梦里，也容不得别人这么欺负安蔓，他怒吼了一声，叫了句“安蔓”，撑着椅座就要去开车门，刚有动作，车身突然嘎啦响了一下，以一种不祥的幅度缓慢倾斜。  
Qin Fang's first instinct was that this had to be a dream, but even in a dream he could not allow anyone to bully An Man like that. He roared her name and pushed himself up by the seat, reaching for the door. The instant he moved, the car gave a harsh metallic creak and began to tilt slowly at an ominous angle.  
秦放后背一凉，突然就不敢动了，僵了有一两秒之后，他慢慢地抬头看向另一侧的前方。  
An icy chill ran down Qin Fang's back, and suddenly he did not dare move. After freezing for a second or two, he slowly lifted his head and looked toward the front on the opposite side.  
那里不是实地，是深蓝色大海一样的空气，无边无际的尽头，甚至漂浮着低一些的星星，车头明显地开始下倾，幸运的是，又以一种颤巍巍的态势保持住了平衡。  
There was no solid ground there, only deep blue air like an endless sea, stretching without limit, with even the lower stars seeming to float inside it. The front of the car was clearly beginning to dip, and luckily, by some trembling miracle, it managed to hold its balance.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`宾语从句`、`条件感`、`结果补充`。<br>
句子主干：这一段的关键动作是 `He turned to look ...` 和 `the car ... began to tilt`。<br>
难点拆解：先用 `this had to be a dream` 写他刚醒时的错乱，再迅速切到现实危险；`only deep blue air like an endless sea` 把中文里强烈的视觉反差落成一个清楚的画面；最后的 `by some trembling miracle` 用来承接“颤巍巍地保持平衡”。<br>
常用短语：
- `by instinct`：下意识地
- `at an ominous angle`：以一种不祥的角度
- `run down one's back`：沿着后背窜下
- `hold its balance`：勉强保持平衡
- `like an endless sea`：像没有尽头的大海

那边的两个人显然也注意到这头的动静了，先前休息的那个冷笑了两声，拔腿就往这边走，才刚走了两步，腿上突然一紧，低头一看，安蔓死死抱住他的腿，虚弱地说了一句：“你别……跟他没关系的，真没关系。”  
The two men over there had clearly noticed the movement on this side. The one who had been resting let out two cold laughs and strode toward the car, but after only two steps he felt something clamp around his leg. Looking down, he saw An Man clutching him desperately and saying in a weak voice, "Don't... it has nothing to do with him, truly nothing."  
那人居然笑了，插科打诨一样向对面的鸭舌帽说了句：“呦，你看看这舍生忘死的，当演戏了都。”  
The man actually laughed and called out to the cap-wearing one on the other side in a joking tone, "Would you look at that? She's risking her life for him like they're putting on a play."  
老搭档了，处理这种事不是一次两次，听个音都知道要行左行右，鸭舌帽笑了笑，大踏步走到车子前头，一抬腿，脚蹬在车后大杠上，一副下一秒就要开踹的架势。  
They were old partners; this was far from the first time they had handled something like this, and they understood each other from the slightest hint. The man in the cap smiled, strode to the front of the car, and lifted one foot onto the rear bar, looking as though he would kick with full force the very next second.  
先前那人低头看安蔓，声音挺平静的：“那屋子，二十四小时我们都盯着，除了你就没别人……再给你个机会，货呢？”  
The first man looked down at An Man, his voice strangely calm. "We had that room watched twenty-four hours a day. Other than you, nobody went in... I'll give you one more chance. Where's the merchandise?"  
货？  
The merchandise?  
什么货？赵江龙倒腾的货吗？安蔓哆嗦着，死死盯着鸭舌帽踩在车后杠上的那只脚，瞳孔都放大了，她如果不说，秦放会死的……  
What merchandise? Something Zhao Jianglong had been moving around? Trembling, An Man stared fixedly at the foot the man in the cap had planted on the rear bar. Her pupils had widened with terror. If she said nothing, Qin Fang would die...  
能拖一分是一分，说不定就是这分分秒秒会有转机呢？  
Every extra minute mattered. What if, somewhere in these dragging seconds, a chance to turn things around appeared?  
安蔓颤抖着说了句：“我没退房，东西……我放在旅馆柜子里……”  
Shaking, An Man said, "I never checked out. The stuff... I left it in a locker at the inn..."  
嘴唇早就被打裂了，这么快被风吹干，说话的时候一丝一丝牵扯得疼，那人的脸上露出满意的笑容，向着鸭舌帽轻轻点了点下颌，鸭舌帽会意，近乎玩味地清了清嗓子，再然后用力一蹬。  
Her lips had long since split open, and now the wind had dried them hard. Every word pulled at the cracks with threadlike pain. A satisfied smile appeared on the man's face. He gave a slight nod of the chin toward the man in the cap. The cap-wearing man understood, cleared his throat almost playfully, and then kicked hard.  
你说，或者不说，结果都是一样的。  
Whether she spoke or kept silent, the result had been the same all along.  
在安蔓撕心裂肺的惨呼声中，车子轰然倾覆，车尾带起土道上的灰尘，在黑色轿车车灯映射下像是细小的舞蹈，但只是那么一瞬，之后接连传来巨大的磕碰，应该是往下坠落时磕到了嶙峋逸出的尖石，再然后就没有声音了。  
Amid An Man's heart-rending scream, the car overturned with a crash. Dust from the dirt road flew up behind it, looking like a tiny dance in the black sedan's headlights, but only for an instant. After that came a series of violent impacts, likely from jagged rocks jutting out along the fall, and then there was no sound at all.  
两个人从地上拖起瘫软的安蔓上车，关上车门时，忽然觉得整座山好像都震了一下，这一下之后，才是真正的安静。  
The two men dragged An Man's limp body off the ground and shoved her into their car. As they shut the door, they suddenly felt the whole mountain seem to tremble once. Only after that single shudder did the true silence arrive.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`条件让步`、`时间状语`、`结果描写`。<br>
句子主干：后段的核心是 `the car overturned with a crash` 和 `the true silence arrive`。<br>
难点拆解：`Whether she spoke or kept silent` 保留原文宿命感；后面把坠落过程拆成“扬尘 -> 磕碰 -> 无声”三拍，读起来更像镜头推进；最后一句特意保留山体一震后的静默，给下一章留悬念。<br>
常用短语：
- `all along`：从头到尾一直如此
- `overturn with a crash`：轰然翻覆
- `jagged rocks jutting out`：嶙峋突出的尖石
- `limp body`：瘫软的身体
- `the true silence arrived`：真正的安静降临
