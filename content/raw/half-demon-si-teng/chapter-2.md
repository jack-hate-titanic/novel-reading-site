语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`时间状语从句`、`独立主格`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：主句核心是 `An Man stood outside Room 188`，后面补充她掌心出汗的状态和原因。<br>
难点拆解：`her palms sweating uncontrollably` 是独立主格，补充站在门口时的状态；`the moment she got nervous` 和 `from the minute she slipped ...` 都在交代时间与起点；英文拆成两句，既保住原文压迫感，也更像自然叙述。<br>
常用短语：
- `outside Room 188`：站在 188 号房外

- `slip a sedative into someone's drink`：把镇静药放进某人的饮料里

- `run into someone`：撞见某人

- `make the first move`：先动手 / 先做第一步

赵江龙火了，一巴掌下来把安蔓打得眼前发黑："特么安小婷你是什么玩意儿你自己不知道吗，怎么给脸不要脸呢？"  
Zhao Jianglong exploded. One slap sent blackness swimming across An Man's vision. "Fuck, An Xiaoting, do you even know what you are? How dare you act like you're too good for this?"  
连骂带打，又是啪啪啪几下，男人手重，又尽是招呼在头脸这种脆弱地方，安蔓的血都充了脑袋，可她也真有那么点邪性，让赵江龙这么一打，原先还犹豫着的，真变成抵死不从了，挣扎着踢打撕咬，拼死也不让他得逞。  
Cursing and hitting her, he landed several more sharp blows. A man's hands were heavy, and he kept aiming for her head and face, the most vulnerable places. An Man felt the blood rush to her skull. Yet there was something fierce and twisted in her too. After Zhao Jianglong hit her like that, the hesitation she had felt earlier turned into absolute refusal. She kicked, clawed, and bit, fighting with everything she had to keep him from getting what he wanted.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`结果表达`、`定语插入`、`并列动作描写`。<br>
句子主干：前面是 `he landed several more sharp blows`，后面落到 `She kicked, clawed, and bit`。<br>
难点拆解：原文情绪很冲，英文按“他施暴 -> 她身体反应 -> 她态度改变 -> 她反抗”推进；`the most vulnerable places` 是补充说明；最后连续动词并列，保住拼死反抗的力度。<br>
常用短语：
- `land a blow`：打下一记重击
- `aim for`：朝着……下手
- `the blood rush to one's skull`：血往头上冲
- `turn into absolute refusal`：变成彻底抗拒
- `get what he wanted`：得逞

撕扯间，赵江龙突然惨呼一声，捂着肚子腾腾腾倒退几步。  
In the middle of the struggle, Zhao Jianglong suddenly let out a scream and staggered back several steps, clutching his stomach.  
安蔓鼻子下头都是血，呼吸间满满的腥味，她颤抖着抬头，正对上赵江龙难以置信的目光。  
Blood covered the area under An Man's nose, and every breath tasted thick with iron. Trembling, she lifted her head and met Zhao Jianglong's disbelieving gaze straight on.  
他的小腹上插着一把刀，而鲜血，正迅速泅上白色的浴袍。  
A knife was buried in his lower abdomen, and blood was rapidly blooming across the white bathrobe.  
安蔓完全懵了，自己动了刀吗？哪拿的？过去的几分钟像是大块大块空白垒砌起来的，毫无印象。  
An Man was completely stunned. Had she done that? When had she grabbed a knife? The last few minutes felt like a wall built from great blank slabs of missing memory. She could remember nothing.  
哆嗦着低头看自己的手，白皙纤长的十根手指，左手中指上带着订婚戒指，那是她和秦放的订婚戒指，圆润流畅的环，熨帖地绕指一周，店员介绍是最畅销款，却合适地像是为她专人定制。  
Shivering, she lowered her head to look at her own hands: ten pale, slender fingers, and on the middle finger of her left hand, an engagement ring. It was the ring Qin Fang had given her, a smooth, rounded band circling her finger perfectly. The salesclerk had called it their best-selling style, yet it fitted her as though it had been custom-made just for her.  
眼前瞬间模糊，带着血色的泪光混着戒指边缘处莹润的银白色泽，居然奇异地幻化出五彩的光晕来，而就在这历来总是被作为吉祥意兆的光晕之中，赵江龙重重倒地。  
Her vision blurred in an instant. The blood-tinged gleam of her tears mixed with the gentle silver sheen at the edge of the ring and, strangely, seemed to bloom into a halo of shifting colors. And within that halo, so often treated as an omen（征兆） of good fortune, Zhao Jianglong crashed heavily to the floor.  

***

安蔓也说不清自己是怎么回到住处的，她失魂落魄般上楼，抖抖索索掏出房卡开门，屋里很黑，静下心来能听到秦放熟睡的呼吸，黑暗中，安蔓背倚着墙站了好久，直到远处的大街上突兀响起刺耳的车声，她才哆嗦了一下，跌跌撞撞扑跪在床边去晃秦放的身子。  
An Man could not even say clearly how she got back to where they were staying. She went upstairs in a daze, fumbled out the key card with shaking hands, and opened the door. The room was dark. Once she forced herself to listen, she could hear Qin Fang's steady breathing in sleep. In the darkness, An Man stood with her back against the wall for a long time, until a shrill car horn abruptly split the distant street. Only then did she shudder and stumble to the bedside, dropping to her knees as she grabbed at Qin Fang's body.  
开始很小幅度，后来就有些失控，哭着叫他："秦放，秦放，你醒一醒啊。"  
At first she shook him very gently. Then it slipped out of control. Crying, she called to him, "Qin Fang, Qin Fang, wake up."  

***

秦放睡得很沉，药物的外力把他拉进深重的睡眠，而睡梦里，他长久地魇在一个场景之中。  
Qin Fang was sleeping deeply. The force of the drug had dragged him into a heavy slumber（沉睡）, and in that sleep he remained trapped for a long time inside a single scene.  
那是个旧时代老式的京戏戏台，两边拉起红布帘子，后头的拉唱班子好生热闹，锣鼓胡琴京二胡，台上生旦净丑唱念做打，各色行头，蟒帔褶靠绶带丝绦济济一堂，他个子小，扒着戏台拼命仰头也只能看到下头的厚底靴、朝方、彩鞋、云履，随着急嘈嘈鼓点上下翻飞，叫人目不暇接。  
It was an old-style Peking Opera stage from another era. Red curtains were drawn up on either side, and behind them the musicians were alive with noise: gongs, drums, huqin（胡琴）, jing'erhu（京二胡）. Onstage, sheng, dan, jing, and chou were singing, chanting, acting, and fighting, each in ornate costume, dragon robes, capes, pleated gowns, armor, sashes, silk cords, crowding the stage in dazzling abundance. He was small. Clinging to the edge of the stage and craning his neck with all his might, he could see only thick-soled boots, square court shoes, embroidered slippers, cloud shoes, all of them rising and falling in a blur with the frantic beat of the drums, too much for the eye to take in.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`并列描写`、`分词结构`、`结果补充`。<br>
句子主干：前面先是 `It was an old-style Peking Opera stage ...`，后面落到 `He was small` 和 `he could see only ...`。<br>
难点拆解：这段是典型镜头式长描写，英文按“戏台全景 -> 乐队声响 -> 台上行当 -> 孩子视角”分层推进；`Clinging ... and craning ...` 是分词结构，写出他努力仰头的动作；最后用 `too much for the eye to take in` 收住“目不暇接”。<br>
常用短语：
- `from another era`：来自旧时代的
- `alive with noise`：热闹喧腾
- `in dazzling abundance`：纷繁夺目地挤在一起
- `cling to the edge of the stage`：扒着戏台边缘
- `too much for the eye to take in`：让人目不暇接

再然后，他突然发现，在戏台最靠里的位置，翻飞的各色衣袂下摆起落的各式戏鞋之间，出现了一双缎面的高跟鞋，鞋头镶着颤巍巍一颗宝珠，光洁足面，圆润的小腿，旗袍的前后片微微拂动……  
Then, all at once, he realized that at the deepest part of the stage, beneath the fluttering hems and among the various opera shoes lifting and falling, there appeared a pair of satin high heels. A trembling pearl shimmered at each pointed toe, along with the glossy arch of a foot, a rounded calf, and the front and back panels of a qipao stirring lightly...  
京戏百音逐渐淡去，到最后，偌大戏台，万千影像，独独只剩了高跟鞋的足音。  
The hundred voices of the opera gradually faded until, at last, on that enormous stage, amid those myriad images, nothing remained but the sound of the high heels.  
蹬，蹬，蹬……  
Click, click, click...  

***

凌晨两点多，旅馆前台打瞌睡的夜班当值洛绒尔甲被安蔓摇醒，夜里寒气重，她穿得严严实实，帽子口罩都套上了，露出的一双眼睛红红肿肿，带着哽咽的音跟他说收到家里的电话，母亲得了重病住院，要连夜赶回去。  
It was a little past two in the morning when An Man shook awake Luorong Erjia, the night clerk dozing at the inn's front desk. The night air was bitter, and she was bundled up tight, hat and mask both on. Only her eyes showed, red and swollen, and in a choking voice she told him she had received a call from home: her mother was gravely ill in the hospital, and she had to rush back overnight.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`时间状语从句`、`过去分词短语`、`宾语从句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段主句是 `An Man shook awake Luorong Erjia`，后面补她的外貌状态和说辞。<br>
难点拆解：`when An Man shook awake ...` 先把时间点落定；`bundled up tight` 是状态补充；`she told him she had received ...` 用宾语从句转述她编出来的话，让英文更自然。<br>
常用短语：
- `a little past two in the morning`：凌晨两点多
- `shake someone awake`：把某人摇醒
- `bundle up tight`：裹得严严实实
- `in a choking voice`：带着哽咽的声音
- `rush back overnight`：连夜赶回去

对于遇到不幸的人是应该施以力所能及的所有帮助的，洛绒尔甲很快就忘记了半夜被人叫醒的不快，他帮安蔓结清房费，拎行李装车，最后帮着她把浑身酒气的秦放扶进车里。  
People suffering misfortune ought to receive every bit of help one can offer. Luorong Erjia quickly forgot the irritation of being awakened in the middle of the night. He helped An Man settle the room charges, carried her luggage to the car, and finally helped her get Qin Fang, reeking of alcohol, into the vehicle.  
安蔓开车离开的时候，洛绒尔甲站在路边一直向车子挥手，心里感慨着汉人姑娘就是能干，连车子都会开，转而想到接下来要走近一个小时的盘山悬崖路，又有些为她担心。  
When An Man drove away, Luorong Erjia stood by the roadside and kept waving after the car. He thought to himself that Han Chinese girls were truly capable; she could even drive. Then he remembered that the road ahead wound for nearly an hour along cliffside mountain bends, and he could not help worrying about her.  
但愿佛祖保佑，嗡嘛呢呗嘧哄。  
May the Buddha bless her. Om mani padme hum.  
他站了好一会儿才呵着气小跑着回屋，几乎就在他关上门揿暗门厅大灯的同时，一辆黑色的轿车从旅馆前头的街道上呼啸而过，橘黄色的车灯遥遥打向的，正是安蔓离开的方向。  
He stood there for quite a while before blowing into his hands and jogging back inside. Almost at the exact moment he shut the door and clicked off the lobby lights, a black sedan roared down the street in front of the inn, its orange headlights aimed at the very direction An Man had taken.
