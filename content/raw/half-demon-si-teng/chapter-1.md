# 《半妖司藤》第一章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件按源文本中的 `第①章` 整理，不包含前面的“引子”。
- 排版规则为“一句中文，一句英文”；遇到较长句子时，补充语法说明和常用短语。

2013年12月，青海藏区囊谦县，近白扎乡。  
December 2013, in Qinghai's Tibetan region, Nangqian County, near Baizha Township.  
阳光倒是不错，可这里的太阳一点都不添暖意。  
The sun was bright, but out here it did nothing to make the air feel warmer.  
安蔓那双脚塞在卖家所谓“纯羊毛、能扛极地严寒”的靴子里，几乎冻成了没知觉的冰坨。  
An Man's feet, stuffed into the seller's supposedly（所谓） pure-wool boots advertised（标榜为） as fit for polar（极地的） cold, were almost frozen into numb（麻木的） lumps.  
饶是如此，她还是倚着车门，顽强地举着手机，东挪西移，活像在搜敌方信号。  
Even so, she leaned against the car door, stubbornly（倔强地） holding up her phone and shifting it this way and that as if she were trying to catch an enemy signal（信号）.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`过去分词短语`、`方式状语从句`、`并列动作描写`。<br>
句子主干：主句核心是 `she leaned against the car door`，后面补充 `holding up her phone` 和 `shifting it this way and that`。<br>
难点拆解：`stuffed into ...` 这类过去分词短语常作后置定语，补充名词状态；`as if she were trying to catch ...` 是方式状语从句，写出“像在搜信号一样”；英文用主句加动作补充，比把所有信息硬塞进一个长句更自然。<br>
常用短语：
- `do nothing to ...`：对……毫无作用
- `fit for polar cold`：适合极寒环境
- `be frozen into numb lumps of ice`：冻得像没知觉的冰块
- `shift ... this way and that`：来回挪动……
- `as if`：仿佛，好像

不远处，不少藏人好奇地盯着她看，脸上都写着跃跃欲试，却没人真敢上来搭话。  
Not far away, quite a few Tibetans watched her with open curiosity（好奇）, their faces all but saying they wanted to come over, yet none of them quite dared speak to her.  
这里太难见到汉人了，尽管电视里见过不少，他们还是想不明白：为什么汉人穿裤子不穿袍子？  
Han Chinese were simply too rare（罕见的） here. They had seen plenty on television, but still found some things hard to understand: why did Han people wear trousers instead of robes?  
为什么大冷天还要裹那么多层？  
Why did women bundle themselves in so many layers in weather this cold?  
这世上还有什么衣服，能比羊皮、狼皮和熊皮更扛寒？  
What in the world could keep out the cold better than sheepskin, wolfskin, or bearskin?  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`特殊疑问句`、`比较结构`、`并列问句`。<br>
句子主干：核心问句是 `What in the world could keep out the cold better than ... ?`，后面并列展开 `sheepskin, wolfskin, or bearskin`。<br>
难点拆解：`What in the world ... ?` 是带强调语气的特殊疑问句；`better than ...` 是比较结构；连续并列几个材质名词，保留了中文里一连串追问的口气。<br>
常用短语：
- `watch ... with open curiosity`：带着明显的好奇看着……
- `find it hard to understand`：很难理解
- `bundle oneself in layers`：把自己一层层裹起来
- `keep out the cold`：抵御寒冷

也不知道是手机终于找对了位置，还是刚才只是卡住了，信号一下子满格。  
Whether she had finally found the right spot or the phone had simply been lagging（卡顿的） before, the signal（信号） suddenly jumped to full bars.  
等了好久的几条微信滴滴滴接连涌进来，前几条都是还在下载的图片，倒是最后一条消息先弹了出来：亲，照片还在精修，先发几张给你看看效果，有问题你吭声哦。  
Several long-delayed WeChat messages came pinging in one after another. The first few were pictures still loading, but the last message popped up first: "Sweetie, the photos are still being retouched（精修过的）, but let me send a few over first so you can see how they look. If anything feels off, just tell me."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`目的状语从句`、`条件状语从句`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `let me send you a few`，后面接 `so you can see how they look` 和 `If anything feels off, just tell me`。<br>
难点拆解：`so you can see ...` 是目的状语从句，表示“先发几张给你看效果”；`If anything feels off` 是条件状语从句；整段先交代照片仍在精修，再补一句“有问题你就说”，很像真实聊天语气。<br>
常用短语：
- `the signal jumped to full bars`：信号一下满格了
- `come pinging in`：叮叮响着接连进来
- `still-downloading pictures`：还在下载中的图片
- `If anything feels off`：如果哪里不太对劲

又等了一会儿，第一张照片终于打开了。  
After waiting a little longer, the first photo finally loaded: the sea, the sunset, her in a wedding dress.  
海边，日落，她穿着婚纱。  
The studio really was reliable（靠谱的）; the retouched shot looked dreamlike（梦一般的）.  
这家影楼倒真靠谱，修出来的片子唯美得像梦一样。  
An Man's eyes instantly grew wet.  
安蔓的眼睛一下子湿了。  
The other photos were all of her too: solo shots with her chin propped on one hand, lost in thought, bending her head to smell the flower between her fingers, laughing freely under a tree-lined lane, and leaning against a bridge beneath a misty umbrella.  
后面几张也都是她：有单人的，有托腮凝思的，有低头轻嗅手里花朵的，有在林荫道上肆无忌惮大笑的，也有斜倚桥头、撑着一把烟雨朦胧的伞的。  
She posted several of the photos to her Moments.  
她把几张照片都发到了朋友圈，配文删了又改、改了又加，最后发出去的是：这世上终有一个注定的人在等你。  
She kept cutting and adding words to the caption（配文）, revising（修改） it over and over before finally posting: Somewhere in this world, there is always one person meant for you.  
直到那时你才会明白，为什么和那些错的人都没有结果。  
Only then do you understand why nothing ever worked out with all the wrong people.  
何其庆幸，千万人之中，遇见你，选择你，只愿意和你走过1314。  
Out of millions, how lucky I was to have met you, to have chosen you, and to want to spend my whole lifetime（一辈子） with only you.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`keep doing` 结构、`there be` 句型、`宾语从句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段的核心表达分别是 `kept revising ...`、`there is always one person ...` 和 `understand why ...`。<br>
难点拆解：`keep doing` 表示动作反复发生；`there is always one person ...` 用 `there be` 引出“总有那么一个人”；`why nothing ever worked out ...` 是跟在 `understand` 后面的宾语从句，说明“想明白为什么”。<br>
常用短语：
- `post ... to Moments`：把……发到朋友圈
- `revise ... over and over`：反复修改……
- `be meant to do`：注定要……
- `work out with`：和……有结果 / 顺利发展
- `a whole lifetime`：一整个人生，一辈子

发完后，她把手机塞回兜里，双手拢到嘴边呵气，又使劲搓了搓，拼命跺脚。  
Once she was done, she shoved the phone back into her pocket, cupped her hands to her mouth, and breathed into them, then rubbed them hard and stamped her feet like mad.  
也不知道跺了第几百下，秦放才回来。  
She had no idea how many times she had done it before Qin Fang came back.  
人还没走近，就半是揶揄地说了句：“够酸的啊。”  
As he walked over, Qin Fang said with a half-teasing smile, "That was sweet enough to rot my teeth." He had almost certainly seen the post.  
十有八九是看到了那条微信，安蔓早有准备，抬起下巴回他：“我就是故意的，就是要膈应那些见不得我好的人。”  
An Man had been ready for that; she lifted her chin and shot back, "I meant to. I wanted to make those bitches who can't stand seeing me do well squirm（坐立不安）." Qin Fang said nothing, only gave her a thumbs-up.  
秦放没说什么，只冲她竖了个拇指。  
But from the blank look on his face, An Man knew his inquiries（打听） had led nowhere.  
看他神色淡淡的，安蔓就知道打听的事没着落：“还是找不到？”  
"Still couldn't find it?" "Worse than that. They said Nangqian was hit during the 2010 Yushu earthquake（地震）. Several nearby mountains collapsed（坍塌）, and some whole villages were swallowed up. Chances are there's nothing left to find."  
“比这还糟。人家说了，2010年玉树地震的时候，囊谦也是灾区，附近塌了好几座山，有的村寨整个都被吞掉了，估计是找不着了。”  
Of course there was nothing to find. This was Qin Fang's family business（家里的事）, supposedly（所谓） tied to fulfilling an old wish passed down by his elders（长辈）, and An Man had never asked too many questions.  
这本来就是秦放的家事，据说是为了还家里老一辈的一个心愿，安蔓没有多问。  
Even before setting out, she had already prepared herself: seventy or eighty years had passed, and the world had changed beyond recognition（认不出来）.  
但出发前她其实就有心理准备：都过去七八十年了，世事风云变幻，十年都足以乾坤倒转，何况七十年？  
Ten years alone could turn everything upside down. Given seventy years, mountains could flatten and rivers could run dry.  
山可平，水可干，要找一个十有八九早已去世的人，实在太难。  
Finding someone who was almost certainly dead was never going to be easy.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`介词短语作状语`、`动名词短语作主语`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：前面是 `Given seventy years, mountains could flatten and rivers could run dry`，后面落到主句 `Finding someone ... was never going to be easy`。<br>
难点拆解：`Given seventy years` 是介词短语作状语，相当于“考虑到七十年之久”；`Finding someone ...` 是动名词短语作主语；英文先铺垫世事巨变，再点明“找一个早已死去的人太难”。<br>
常用短语：
- `family business`：家里的事，家事
- `fulfill an old wish`：完成一个旧心愿
- `change beyond recognition`：变化大到几乎认不出来
- `turn everything upside down`：让一切天翻地覆
- `never going to be easy`：注定不会容易

更何况这中间还横着一场始料未及的7.1级地震。  
Besides, there had been an unexpected 7.1-magnitude earthquake（地震） in the meantime.  
安蔓试探着问了句：“那……我们回去？”  
An Man ventured, "Then... should we head back?"  
人有时候就是这么拧巴，原本就不抱太大希望的事，突然被告知彻底没戏了，心里反倒会涌上一股说不出的不痛快。  
People can be strange that way: when you never had much hope for something to begin with, being told all at once that it is absolutely impossible（不可能的） can still leave you feeling twisted up inside.  
秦放就是这样。  
Qin Fang was exactly like that.  
上了车，他一边打方向盘一边说：“再找找吧。好不容易来一趟，好歹也算圆老太太一个心愿，多少得在恩人坟前磕个头。”  
When they got back into the car, he turned the wheel and said, "Let's keep looking a little longer. We came all this way. At the very least, it would help fulfill（完成） the old lady's wish. At the very least, I should bow（祭拜） at our benefactor's（恩人的） grave（坟墓）."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`when 引导状语从句`、`情态动词 should`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段的落脚句是 `I should bow at our benefactor's grave`，前面先铺垫“好不容易来一趟，也是为了圆心愿”。<br>
难点拆解：`when ...` 状语从句负责交代语境；`should` 表示说话人的应当和坚持；英文把原因、态度和最后的决定拆开写，语气会比直译更稳。<br>
常用短语：
- `to begin with`：一开始，本来
- `all at once`：一下子，突然
- `twist your gut with frustration`：让人心里别扭发堵
- `at the very least`：至少
- `benefactor's grave`：恩人的坟墓

又说：“就当出来玩了，这边景色好，你不是也挺喜欢的吗？心都快被涤荡得跟水晶一样了吧？”  
Then he added, "Just treat it like a trip. The scenery's beautiful here. Don't you like it? Hasn't your soul been polished clear as crystal（晶莹透亮的） by now?" There he was needling her again.  
又来损她。  
An Man shot Qin Fang a look.  
安蔓白了秦放一眼。  
She had been posting a lot on WeChat and Weibo these past few days, mostly because she'd never been here before.  
这些日子她老发微信微博，也是因为以前没来过这儿，看雪山、看藏民、看喇嘛庙，什么都新鲜，经常顺手就报备一下行程，一时冲动还会发几句类似“心灵都被净化了，人就该活得这么纯粹”的感想。  
The snow mountains, the Tibetans, the lamaseries（喇嘛庙）, all of it felt fresh to her, so she kept sharing photos and updating her itinerary. Every now and then, on impulse（一时冲动）, she would post lines like, "My soul has been purified（净化）; this is how purely a person ought to live."  
可那也就是随口一说，他还真以为她喜欢这里啊？  
But that was just talk.  
别的不说，光那能把皮肤晒老的高原紫外线，就够她受的了。  
Did he really think she liked it here? Leaving everything else aside, the plateau（高原） ultraviolet（紫外线的） rays alone, the kind that made your skin age faster, were more than enough to make her miserable（难受的）.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`插入状语`、`分词结构`、`结果表达`。<br>
句子主干：核心是 `... were more than enough to make her miserable`，前面插入 `Leaving everything else aside` 这一类补充。<br>
难点拆解：`Leaving everything else aside` 是插入状语，意思是“别的不说”；分词或补充动作先铺出感受，再落到主句结果；`more than enough to ...` 表示“已经足以让她难受”。<br>
常用短语：
- `needle someone`：拿话刺人，损人
- `keep updating`：不断更新
- `on impulse`：一时冲动地
- `those were just words`：那也就是嘴上说说
- `Leaving everything else aside`：别的不说

她笑嘻嘻地回了一句：“你还不知道我吗，不就是装装样子。”  
She grinned. "You know what I'm like. I was just putting on a show."  
秦放嗯了一声：“诚实。”  
Qin Fang gave a quiet grunt. "Honest."  
她知道秦放爱听什么，也知道他厌烦什么。  
She knew what Qin Fang liked to hear, and she knew what put him off.  
和秦放相识相处这些日子，安蔓承认自己多少有点投其所好、用了点心机。  
An Man admitted that in getting to know him and staying close to him, she had played to his tastes and used a little calculated（刻意经营的） charm.  
可那又怎么样呢？  
But so what?  
男人给女人送花、安排浪漫约会，难道就不算手段吗？  
When men send women flowers and arrange romantic dates, isn't that a kind of maneuver（手段） too?  
重要的是结果。  
What mattered was the result.  
不管秦放最初爱的是谁，最爱的是谁，现在陪他来囊谦处理家事的人是她，以后也只会是她。  
No matter whom Qin Fang had loved first, or loved most, she was the one beside him now, accompanying him to Nangqian as his girlfriend（女友） to deal with family matters（家事）. And in the future, it would only be her.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`让步状语从句`、`反问句`、`强调句式`。<br>
句子主干：核心表达是 `it would only be her`，前面先用 `No matter whom ...` 让步，再用 `But so what?` 强化态度。<br>
难点拆解：`No matter whom ...` 是让步状语从句，表示“不管他爱的是谁”；`But so what?` 不是求答案，而是在强调“那又怎样”；最后用 `it would only be her` 把人物判断落定。<br>
常用短语：
- `know what someone likes hearing`：知道对方爱听什么
- `shape oneself to someone's tastes`：按对方喜好来塑造自己
- `But so what?`：那又怎样？
- `The result was what mattered.`：重要的是结果
- `No matter whom ...`：不管是谁……

两人关系确定的时候，秦放曾说过一句话：“安蔓，我就喜欢你这种明白人。”  
When they first got together, Qin Fang had once said to her, "An Man, what I like about you is that you get it." So An Man knew that with Qin Fang, she did not need to overthink things.  
于是安蔓知道，和秦放相处，不需要想太多，做个明白人就够了。  
All she had to do was be the sensible one.  
“安蔓，我就喜欢你这种明白人。”  
"An Man, what I like about you is that you get it."  
这句话对她很重要。  
That line mattered to her.  
---

两人又在附近待了两天。  
They stayed nearby another two days.  
那条婚纱朋友圈下面点赞无数，也有人一个劲地提醒她别错过青海的景点，比如四大神山之一的阿尼玛卿，比如巴颜喀拉主峰，比如“天下黄河贵德清”。  
Her wedding-dress post racked up countless（无数的） likes, and people kept urging her not to miss Qinghai's scenic spots, mentioning places like Amne Machin, one of the four sacred（神圣的） mountains, the main peak of the Bayan Har range, and Guide, where people say the Yellow River runs clear.  
于是她除了发图片、晒行程，做得最多的就是翻地图看路线。  
So aside from posting photos and sharing her itinerary（行程）, what she did most was pore over maps and study routes.  
也就是这时她才知道，囊谦再往南就是西藏昌都，再往东还有全藏闻名的德格印经院。  
Only then did she realize that farther south of Nangqian lay Chamdo in Tibet, and farther east stood the Derge Printing House, famous throughout Tibet.  
安蔓极力撺掇秦放往那边走，秦放却一口回绝。  
An Man did her best to coax（哄劝） Qin Fang into going that way, but he flatly（断然地） refused.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`倒装`、`宾语从句`、`coax ... into doing` 结构。<br>
句子主干：这一组句子的核心是 `Only then did she realize ...` 和 `An Man ... coaxed Qin Fang ... but he refused`。<br>
难点拆解：`Only then` 放句首时，主句要部分倒装；`that farther south ...` 是跟在 `realize` 后面的宾语从句；`coax ... into doing` 表示“哄劝某人去做某事”。<br>
常用短语：
- `share one's itinerary`：分享行程
- `leaf through maps`：翻看地图
- `Only then did ...`：直到那时才……
- `coax someone into doing`：劝某人去做……
- `flatly refuse`：一口回绝

“不去，听说全藏的佛经都是德格印的。那样神圣的地方，你是想把自己从里到外都涤荡成钻石吗？”  
"No. I've heard all the Buddhist scriptures（佛经） in Tibet are printed in Derge. It's too sacred（神圣的） a place. What, are you trying to have your body and soul polished into a diamond（钻石）?"  
安蔓把失望压了下去。  
An Man hid her disappointment.  
车子掉头，终于离开白扎的时候，她想着秦放说她像水晶、像钻石，忽然有点难过。  
When the car finally turned around and left Baizha behind, she kept thinking about Qin Fang's joke about crystal（水晶） and diamonds（钻石）, and suddenly felt a little sad.  
再怎么涤荡，我也不过是块煤疙瘩罢了，她在心里想。  
No matter how much I get purified（净化）, she thought, I'm still just a lump of coal.

第三天晚上，两人在囊谦县城一家藏餐馆吃饭。  
On the evening of the third day, they were eating at a Tibetan restaurant in Nangqian County.  
回到囊谦，也就算踏上了回程，秦放这才大致把这一趟的缘由跟安蔓说了。  
Now that they were back in Nangqian, they were effectively on the way home, and Qin Fang finally gave An Man a rough account of why he had made the trip.  
秦放的曾祖母是四川靖化县人。  
Qin Fang's great-grandmother（曾祖母） was from Jinghua County in Sichuan.  
靖化县在中国近代史上留过一笔：1936到1937年的川甘大饥荒里，当地人吃人的惨案太多，活活把断案的县长于竹君吓疯了。  
Jinghua left its mark on modern Chinese history because during the great Sichuan-Gansu famine（饥荒） of 1936 to 1937, there were so many cases of cannibalism（人吃人） that the magistrate（县官） handling（审理） them, Yu Zhujun, was driven insane（疯掉的）. It was during that famine（饥荒） that his great-grandmother（曾祖母） fled（逃难） with her family.  
他的曾祖母也就是在那场大饥荒中随家人外出逃荒的。  
At the time, most people headed east toward Jiangnan, long known for its abundance, and presumably（想来） a place where food could still be found.  
那时候大部分人往东走，江南自古富庶，想来总还能有口饭吃；但也有一小部分人把宝押在了西部藏区。  
But a small number gambled（押宝） instead on the Tibetan areas in the west.  
往西的路更险，环境更恶劣，可人少，也就意味着抢饭吃的嘴少。  
The western road was more dangerous and the conditions harsher（更恶劣的）, but fewer people going that way also meant fewer mouths competing（争抢） for food.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`对比并列`、`结果表达`、`名词短语引申义`。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `fewer people going that way also meant fewer mouths competing for food`，前面先说 `The western road was dangerous and the conditions harsh`。<br>
难点拆解：前后两句构成对比并列，路险所以人少；`meant ...` 直接引出结果；`fewer mouths` 不是字面“嘴巴”，而是“少了抢饭的人”。<br>
常用短语：
- `fled with her family`：和家人一起逃荒
- `presumably`：想来，大概
- `gamble on`：把希望押在……
- `harsh conditions`：恶劣环境
- `fewer mouths competing for food`：抢饭吃的人更少

流徙到青海囊谦一带时，家里人死的死、散的散，只剩她一个人。  
By the time they drifted（流落到） into the Nangqian area of Qinghai, some of her family had died and the rest had scattered（离散）, until only she was left. Fortunately, just when death seemed certain, kind people took her in and saved her life.  
万幸的是，在快要丧命的时候，她遇上了好心人收留，总算捡回一条命。  
Her benefactors（恩人） had a daughter a year older than she was, but the girl died suddenly in an epidemic（疫病）.  
恩人家里有个比她大一岁的女儿，后来染了时疫暴亡，于是那家人就把她当女儿养着，后来还让她顶替自家女儿，去履行那门从小定下的婚约。  
After that, they raised Qin Fang's great-grandmother（曾祖母） as their own daughter, and later had her take over the marriage arrangement（婚约） their daughter had entered into as a child.  
当地的习俗是，未出嫁的女人死后凄凉，将来连个上坟磕头的人都没有，因此一定要出钱认个活亲、养个儿子。  
Local custom held that if an unmarried woman died, her lot after death would be a lonely one, and in the future there would not even be anyone to visit her grave（坟墓） and bow（祭拜） for her.  
秦放的曾祖母便把这事应承了下来，说：但凡我有后人上坟磕头，阿姐坟前就少不了扫墓的人；我的儿子就是阿姐的儿子，阿姐的事也会当作亲娘的事一样去办。  
For that reason, the family had to spend money to adopt（认养） a living relative（亲属） and raise a son in her name. Qin Fang's great-grandmother（曾祖母） agreed to do this, saying, "As long as I have descendants（后人） to tend（祭扫） my grave（坟墓）, there will always be someone to tend my elder sister's grave（坟墓） too. My sons will be her sons, and her affairs will be handled（办理） just as though she were their own mother."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`宾语从句`、`条件状语从句`、`as though` 比较状语从句。<br>
句子主干：这段主干是 `Local custom held that ...`，以及后面的 `My sons will be her sons, and her affairs will be handled ...`。<br>
难点拆解：`that ...` 是 `held` 后面的宾语从句；`if an unmarried woman died` 和 `As long as I have descendants ...` 都是在设定条件；`as though she were their own mother` 表示“像亲娘一样”。<br>
常用短语：
- `Local custom held that ...`：当地习俗认为……
- `bow in remembrance`：上坟磕头、祭拜
- `in her name`：以她的名义
- `tend a grave`：给坟墓祭扫
- `as though she were ...`：就像她是……一样

世上的事，向来是立誓容易，践诺难。  
In this world, vows（誓言） are always easier to make than to keep.  
后来她随丈夫到东边谋生做生意，偏偏又赶上兵荒马乱，回去的路就这么一点点断了。  
Later, she followed her husband east to make a living and do business（生意）.  
直到死，她都没能再见故土。  
With war and chaos everywhere, the road home grew more and more distant, until she died without ever seeing that homeland（故土） again.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`比较结构`、`until 时间状语从句`、`without doing` 结构。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `the road back grew more and more remote`，后面接 `until she died ...` 来收束整段。<br>
难点拆解：`easier to make than to keep` 是比较结构，用来概括“立誓容易，守誓难”；`until she died ...` 是时间状语从句；`without ever seeing ... again` 表示“直到死都没能再见”。<br>
常用短语：
- `vows are easier to make than to keep`：立誓容易，践诺难
- `make a living`：谋生
- `with war and chaos everywhere`：在兵荒马乱之中
- `without ever seeing ... again`：直到死都没再见到……

秦放说：“原本这事是指望我爷爷的。可他那时候赶上打仗、建国、轰轰烈烈的大运动，家里成分本来就不好，谁还敢往藏区跑？那年月，不得被当成特务抓起来啊？”  
Qin Fang said, "Originally（原本）, the job was supposed to fall to my grandfather. But his time was full of war, the founding of the country, and all those huge political（政治的） movements（运动）. Our family's class background was bad enough to begin with. Who would have dared run off to the Tibetan region（地区）? Wouldn't he have been hauled off as a spy back then?"  
“我爸结婚那会儿是八十年代，你也知道，那时候穷，进个厂子就是一辈子的铁饭碗，一分钱都得省着花，哪来闲钱出门？再说这也不是什么火烧眉毛的事，磕个头什么时候不行？就这么一年拖一年，一直拖到我爸没了，这事也没成行。”  
"My father got married in the eighties. You know what it was like then: people were poor. Getting into a factory meant an iron rice bowl for life, and every cent had to be pinched. Who had spare money to travel? Besides, it didn't seem that urgent. Paying respects at a grave（坟墓） could wait. So it got put off year after year, until my dad died, and the trip never happened."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`宾语从句`、`动名词作主语`、`并列短句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段的核心是 `Getting into a factory meant ...`，最后落到 `the trip never happened`。<br>
难点拆解：`what it was like then` 是 `know` 后面的宾语从句；`Getting into a factory` 用动名词作主语，表示“进厂这件事”；后面一串短句连着解释时代背景，所以读起来像口语回忆。<br>
常用短语：
- `an iron rice bowl`：铁饭碗
- `every cent had to be saved`：每一分钱都要省着花
- `spare money`：闲钱
- `pay respects at a grave`：到坟前祭拜
- `put off year after year`：一年拖一年

话题有点沉重，安蔓叹了口气，给秦放斟了一杯酥油茶。  
The mood had grown a little heavy, and An Man sighed and poured（倒出） Qin Fang a cup of butter tea.  
“我爸临死前才把这事告诉我，我这才知道，原来我家还承着这么个女人的恩情。我当时就说行啊，我去跑这一趟，一次性替我爷爷和我爸把这个头都磕了。可我爸说别，等你娶了老婆再去，成双成对的，也给地下那女人一点念想。你一个人去，算什么事呢。”  
"My father only told me about it before he died. That was when I learned my family still owed this woman a debt（恩债） of gratitude. I said fine, I'd make the trip and bow（祭拜） on behalf of both my grandfather and my father in one go. But my dad said no, wait until you've found a wife and go then. Go as a pair, so the woman underground can have a little comfort. What kind of gesture is it if you go alone?"  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`表语从句`、`宾语从句`、`时间状语从句`。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `That was when I learned ...`，后面再补 `my family still owed ...` 和 `until you've found a wife`。<br>
难点拆解：`when I learned ...` 放在 `That was` 后面，是表语从句；`(that) my family still owed ...` 是 `learned` 的宾语从句；`until you've found a wife` 交代时间条件，表示“等你先成家再说”。<br>
常用短语：
- `before he died`：在他去世前
- `owe such a debt to ...`：欠着……这样一份恩情
- `on behalf of`：代替……
- `in one go`：一次性地
- `below ground`：在地下，指死后

安蔓笑了：“所以找着我，你就来了？”  
An Man laughed.  
想了想，她又补了一句：“其实人也挺怪的。换了别人，这么点事，隔了七八十年，又隔了好几代，偷个懒不来也就算了。可总还是有人，会把这种事当回事，真的愿意翻山越岭去践这个诺。”  
"So the moment you found me, you came?" After thinking for a moment, she added, "People really are strange. For someone else, something this small, something seventy or eighty years old and several generations removed, might be easy enough to shrug off. But there are always people who take it seriously（当真地）, who will cross mountains and countless（无数的） miles just to keep a promise（承诺）."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`假设语气`、`定语从句`、`目的状语`。<br>
句子主干：先说 `For someone else ... might be easy enough to shrug off`，再转到 `there are always people who take it seriously`。<br>
难点拆解：`might be ...` 带有“换了别人也许会这样”的假设语气；`who take it seriously` 和 `who will cross ...` 都是定语从句，修饰 `people`；`just to keep a promise` 表示目的，“只为了践诺”。<br>
常用短语：
- `After thinking for a moment`：想了想之后
- `several generations removed`：隔了好几代
- `shrug off`：轻轻带过，不当回事
- `cross mountains and countless miles`：翻山越岭，长途跋涉
- `keep a promise`：践诺，守约

秦放很认同这话：“这两天我一直在找人。可有时候我自己也说不清，到底是在干什么，就觉得挺没劲的，像在瞎折腾。真找着了又怎么样？磕不磕这个头，日子不还是照过吗？”  
Qin Fang nodded. "These past two days I've been asking around, but sometimes I don't even know what I'm doing anymore. It feels pointless, like I'm just wearing myself out. Even if I do find it, so what? Whether I bow（祭拜） or not, life will still go on just the same, won't it?"  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`让步状语从句`、`助动词 do 强调`、`反意疑问句`。<br>
句子主干：核心是 `Even if I do find it, so what?` 和 `life will still go on just the same, won't it?`。<br>
难点拆解：`Even if ...` 是让步状语从句；`do find` 不是多余，而是强调“就算我真找到了”；句尾 `won't it?` 是反意疑问句，用来加强“日子照过”的语气。<br>
常用短语：
- `ask around`：四处打听
- `can't even tell what I'm doing`：连自己在干什么都说不清
- `make a fuss`：瞎折腾
- `Even if I do ...`：即使我真的……
- `life will still go on`：日子还是会照常过

有好一会儿，两人都没说话。  
For quite a while neither of them spoke.  
安蔓先开口：“喝酒吗？陪你喝点青稞。”  
Then An Man said, "Want a drink? I'll have some qingke liquor with you." Qin Fang smiled.  
秦放笑了笑，正想说什么，门外忽然响起一阵很大的刹车声。  
He was just about to say something when a loud screech（刺耳刹车声） of brakes came from outside.

外头停了好几辆车，清一色的路虎揽胜。  
Several cars had pulled up, all of them Range Rovers.  
下来的一水儿都是大老爷们，为首的那个谢顶发福，可一身装备倒真不差，上下都是始祖鸟的标，粗粗一看就得好几万。  
The men who got out were all big guys, and the one in front was balding and thickset, but his gear was impressive（像样的）, Arc'teryx from head to toe, easily worth tens of thousands at a glance. They were probably stopping for a meal.  
大概是停车吃饭，一进门就七嘴八舌地嚷嚷起来，接着又喜出望外地跟秦放他们打招呼：“汉人吧？来旅游的？我们刚看见外头停的车，挂的还是内地牌照，就说这儿肯定也有游客。”  
The moment they came in, they started talking over one another, then greeted Qin Fang and An Man in delighted（喜出望外的） surprise. "Han Chinese, right? Here on a trip? We saw the car parked outside, mainland（内地） plates and all, and figured（认定） there had to be other travelers here."  
要是在东南沿海，这种自来熟多半是见不着的。  
If this had been the southeast coast, people probably wouldn't have been this quick to act familiar.  
可囊谦这边汉人少，路上碰见了，总要寒暄几句。  
But Han Chinese were rare（罕见的） out here in Nangqian, and when you ran into them on the road, it was normal enough to stop and exchange（寒暄） a few words. Qin Fang nodded in greeting, but the one leading the group was especially effusive（热络的）.  
秦放欠了欠身，算是打过招呼；领头那个却格外热络，见他们的菜一时半会儿还没上，也不管秦放和安蔓愿不愿意，硬是凑过来聊起天来。  
Seeing that their food had not yet arrived, he ignored whether Qin Fang and An Man actually wanted company（陪伴） and squeezed（硬挤过来） over to chat anyway.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`原因状语结构`、`宾语从句`、`并列谓语`。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `he ignored ... and squeezed over to chat anyway`，前面先用 `Seeing that ...` 交代原因。<br>
难点拆解：`Seeing that their food had not yet arrived` 相当于“看着/因为菜还没上”；`whether Qin Fang and An Man actually wanted company` 是被无视的内容；`ignored` 和 `squeezed` 是并列谓语，写出他自来熟地凑过来。<br>
常用短语：
- `become familiar so quickly`：一下子就这么熟络
- `exchange a few words`：寒暄几句
- `incline one's head in greeting`：点头示意
- `be especially effusive`：特别热络
- `squeeze over to chat`：硬凑过来聊天

他自我介绍姓马，在江西景德镇做瓷器生意，这次和朋友自驾过来。  
He introduced himself as Ma and said he ran a porcelain（瓷器） business（生意） in Jingdezhen, Jiangxi, and had come out on a self-drive（自驾） trip with friends.  
秦放问他是不是准备登山，这位马老板立刻瞪大了眼：“登啥山？冻死我那个球！”  
Qin Fang asked if they were planning to climb anything, and Boss Ma widened（瞪大） his eyes.  
明明穿的是户外圈里号称“领导型”的始祖鸟，衣标SV，专业向导级别，可一路上却抖抖索索地缩在车里，让司机替他“自驾”。  
"Climb what? I'd freeze my balls off!"  
显然又是个噱头大于实质的，不是一路人。  
He was wearing the kind of top-tier（顶级的） Arc'teryx gear that outdoor people jokingly（戏称地） call executive-level, with the SV label meant for professional（专业级的） guides, yet the whole trip he had huddled（缩成一团） in the car, shivering, while the driver did the "self-driving（自驾）."  
秦放懒得跟他多说，他却越聊越起劲，东拉西扯地讲自己的生意，抱怨一路上吃得不好，夸秦放和安蔓养眼又般配，末了还颇为关切地问安蔓：“妹妹，脸色不太好，晕车啊，还是高反啊？”  
He was clearly more show than substance, not Qin Fang's kind of person. Qin Fang had no desire to say much to him, but Boss Ma only got more worked up the longer he talked, rambling（东拉西扯的） about his business（生意）, complaining about the food on the road, praising（夸赞） how good-looking（养眼的） and well-matched Qin Fang and An Man were, and finally asking An Man with concern（关切）, "Miss, you don't look well. Carsick（晕车的）, or is it altitude sickness（高原反应）?"  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`现在分词短语`、`并列补充成分`、`选择疑问句`。<br>
句子主干：整段主干是“他越聊越起劲，最后问安蔓身体是不是不舒服”，其中 `rambling ... / complaining ... / praising ... / asking ...` 一串分词补充说话过程。<br>
难点拆解：几个 `-ing` 形式并列出现时，通常是在补充同一主语的连续动作；`Carsick, or is it altitude sickness?` 是选择疑问句；这种写法能把人物热络又嘴碎的状态写得很活。<br>
常用短语：
- `top-tier gear`：顶级装备
- `huddle shivering in the car`：缩在车里发抖
- `more show than substance`：噱头大于实质
- `ramble about`：东拉西扯地聊
- `altitude sickness`：高原反应

好不容易熬到他那桌上菜，同来的人喊他回桌，这马老板还犹自念念不舍，对秦放说：“兄弟，晚上去我那儿聊聊吧，我跟你投缘，一见如故，说不完的话。我就住城中心的金马大酒店，188号房，你一定来啊，咱们聊聊。”  
At long last, dishes arrived at his table and his companions（同伴） called him back, but Boss Ma was still reluctant（不情愿的） to leave. He said to Qin Fang, "Brother, come by my place tonight and let's talk. I really hit it off with you, like we'd known each other forever. I've got endless（说不完的） things to say. I'm staying at the Jinma Hotel downtown, room 188. You have to come. Let's talk."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`祈使句`、`Let's ...` 邀请结构、`并列短句`。<br>
句子主干：主句依次是 `I'm staying at ...`、`You have to come`、`Let's talk`。<br>
难点拆解：祈使或半祈使表达适合写人物直接招呼别人；`Let's talk` 是典型邀请结构；连续几个短句排开，能保留人物“嘴快话多”的口气。<br>
常用短语：
- `At long last`：好不容易，终于
- `be reluctant to leave`：舍不得走
- `hit it off with someone`：和某人很投缘
- `like old friends at first sight`：一见如故
- `You have to come.`：你一定得来

这马老板也太逗了。  
Boss Ma really was a character.  
晚上临睡前，秦放想起来还止不住发笑，跟安蔓说真是莫名其妙，自己明明都没跟他说上几句话，到了姓马的嘴里，居然就成了“一见如故”。  
Even at bedtime（临睡前） Qin Fang still couldn't stop laughing. He told An Man it was ridiculous（莫名其妙的）: he had barely said two sentences to the man, yet somehow, in Boss Ma's version of events, they had already become "kindred（投缘的） spirits."  
安蔓勉强笑了笑，脸色很是疲倦。  
An Man forced a smile; she looked exhausted（疲惫不堪的）.  
秦放走过来搂住她，在她鬓角亲了亲，说：“姓马的只有一句说对了。你脸色是真不好，是这两天太累了，还是晕车？”  
Qin Fang came over and put an arm around her, kissed her at the temple, and said, "Boss Ma only got one thing right: you really don't look well. Are you carsick（晕车的） from the past couple of days, or just worn out?" An Man nodded and pointed under her eyes.  
安蔓点点头，又指了指自己的眼圈：“进藏之后就睡不大好，晚上吃片安定行么？”  
"I haven't been sleeping well since we came into Tibet. Would it be okay if I took a sleeping pill tonight?" "You've always been on the delicate（娇弱的） side. Don't take too much. One pill is enough."  
“你体质本来就弱，别吃太多，一片就行了。”  
An Man teased him.  
安蔓淘气地笑：“体质好的就能吃得更多吗？要是你，得几片？”  
"So if your constitution（体质） were stronger, you could take more? How many would it take for you?" Qin Fang put on a solemn face.  
秦放故作深沉：“要放倒我这样的猛男，至少两片……三片才保险。”  
"To knock down a tough guy like me? At least two... three, to be safe." An Man burst（突然笑出） into laughter.  
安蔓格格笑起来，挣脱他的怀抱，走到一边打开行李箱取药，拧开盒盖，先倒出一片，怔愣了两秒之后，又倒了两片。  
She slipped out of Qin Fang's arms, went over to the side, opened the suitcase（行李箱） to get the medicine（药）, unscrewed the cap, poured（倒出） out one pill first, froze（愣住） for two seconds, and then poured（倒出） out two more.  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`并列谓语`、`时间顺序连接`、`动作描写`。<br>
句子主干：主句核心是一连串动作：`opened ... unscrewed ... poured ... froze ... poured ...`。<br>
难点拆解：这些动词共用同一主语，所以属于并列谓语；`then` 明确标出前后顺序；英文把“取药、拧盖、倒药、愣住、再倒两片”拆成动作链，画面会更清楚。<br>
常用短语：
- `burst into laughter`：笑出声来
- `slip out of someone's arms`：从某人的怀里挣脱出来
- `unscrew the cap`：拧开盖子
- `pour out`：倒出
- `freeze for two seconds`：愣住两秒

三片安定躺在掌心里，汗却出得厉害。  
Three sleeping pills lay in her palm.  
安蔓心跳得飞快，回头去看秦放。  
Sweat broke out all over, and An Man's heart was pounding（怦怦直跳）.  
他正在开电视调音量，调着调着忽然噗地一声笑出来，说了句：“这王导也太找乐了。”  
She turned back to look at Qin Fang. He was turning on the TV and adjusting the volume, and while he was fiddling（摆弄） with it, he suddenly let out a short laugh and said, "Director Wang really knows how to put on a show."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`完整主谓结构`、`while 时间状语从句`、`并列动作`。<br>
句子主干：主句是 `he suddenly let out a short laugh and said`，前面用 `while he was fiddling with it` 补充背景。<br>
难点拆解：`Three sleeping pills lay in her palm` 这一类写法是完整主谓句，不用照搬中文省略镜头；`while he was fiddling ...` 是时间状语从句；`let out a short laugh and said` 是并列动作，先笑再说。<br>
常用短语：
- `lay in her palm`：躺在她掌心里
- `break out in sweat`：出一身汗
- `one's heart is pounding`：心跳很快
- `fiddle with`：摆弄，调来调去
- `make a show of it`：把场面弄得很热闹 / 很会制造效果

电视里好像在放《爸爸去哪儿》，雪乡那一期，画面上一片白蒙蒙的，几家人正争先恐后地抢房子。  
It looked like *Dad, Where Are We Going? *, an episode（一集） filmed in Snow Town.  
安蔓的嘴唇干得厉害，她不安地舔了一下，说：“秦放，我给你倒杯水吧。”  
The screen was all white, and several families were scrambling（争抢） to claim rooms. An Man's lips felt terribly dry. She licked them anxiously and said, "Qin Fang, I'll get you a glass of water."  
语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`It looked like ...` 句型、`过去进行时`、`并列短句`。<br>
句子主干：这一段先用 `It looked like ...` 带出画面，再写 `were scrambling to claim rooms`，最后落到 `I'll pour you a glass of water`。<br>
难点拆解：`It looked like ...` 常用来引出“看起来像……”的场景；`were scrambling ...` 用过去进行时写当时正在发生的混乱；后面切到 `She licked them anxiously and said ...`，用并列短句把镜头推到人物对白。<br>
常用短语：
- `an episode filmed in ...`：在……拍的那一期
- `be all white`：一片白茫茫
- `scramble to do`：争先恐后去做……
- `feel terribly dry`：觉得特别干
- `pour someone a glass of water`：给某人倒一杯水
