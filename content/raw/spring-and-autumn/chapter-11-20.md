## 第十一章 挑战中央
## Chapter 11: Challenging the Center

俗话说：人挪活树挪死。  
As the old saying goes, people thrive when they move, while trees die when they are moved.
可是，首都搬家绝对是一件伤筋动骨的事情。  
But moving a capital always shakes a state to its bones.
在周朝的伟大首都从西部搬到东面之后，中央的威权迅速衰落，诸侯们渐渐地开始不尿中央这一壶了。  
After the Zhou capital moved from the west to the east, the authority（权威） of the center weakened rapidly, and the feudal lords gradually stopped taking it seriously.
诸侯们互相攻杀 ，目无王法，周王室的威望江河日下。  
The lords fought and slaughtered one another, with no regard for royal law, and the prestige（威望） of the Zhou royal house kept sinking.
现在，来说说周王室的地位变化。  
Now let us look at how the standing of the Zhou royal house changed.
这，需要从周的祖先开始说起。  
To explain that, we have to begin with Zhou’s ancestors.

### 周朝的建立
### The Founding of the Zhou Dynasty

最早从黄帝说起就太久远了，从后稷说起也有些长。  
Starting all the way back with the Yellow Emperor would take too long; even starting with Hou Ji would still be a long story.
只说一点，周王室是黄帝的后裔，姓姬，与黄帝同姓。  
Let us note only this: the Zhou royal house claimed descent from the Yellow Emperor and bore the same Ji surname.
到公刘那一代，跟戎狄混居。  
By the time of Gong Liu, they were living mixed in among the Rong and Di peoples.
什么是戎狄？  
But who exactly were the Rong and the Di?
上古时期，华夏族将周边的民族按照方位分为四个部分：北边的是狄，南边的是蛮，西边的是戎，东边的是夷。  
In antiquity, the Huaxia people divided the surrounding peoples by direction: the Di to the north, the Man to the south, the Rong to the west, and the Yi to the east.
后来常 说南蛮南蛮，就是这么来的。  
That is where the later phrase "southern barbarians" came from.
那么，戎狄就是西北的民族了。  
So the Rong and the Di were the peoples of the northwest.
由于公刘人品好，乐于助人，种粮食的水准又高，基本上就是雷锋加袁隆平这样一个人，因此受到各族群众的拥戴，成为那一带的首领。  
Gong Liu was said to be kind, helpful, and highly skilled at growing grain, basically a blend of Lei Feng and Yuan Longping, so he won wide support and became the leader of that region.
到儿子庆节，周国正式成立，地点应该在今天的甘肃一带。  
By the time of his son Qing Jie, the state of Zhou had formally taken shape, probably in what is now Gansu.
几百年后，到了古公亶（音单）父那一代，戎狄起兵来攻打，要抢财物，古公亶父给他们财物。  
Hundreds of years later, by the time of Gugong Danfu, the Rong and Di came raiding for wealth, and he gave them goods.
之后戎狄又来，要抢地盘和人民。  
Later they came again, this time to seize land and people.
古公亶父又要给他们，老百姓不干了，想要打仗，古公亶父劝他们说：“鬼子来打仗，无非就是要土地和人民。  
Gugong Danfu was ready to yield again, but the people refused and wanted war, so he tried to persuade them: "When raiders come to fight, all they want is land and people.
想想看，大家跟着我也是过，跟着他们也是过，有什么区别？  
Think about it. Whether you live under me or under them, what difference is there?
如果大家为了我而战斗，为我死那么多人，我不忍心 。  
I can't bear it if everyone fights for me and so many people die for me.
” 多么伟大的情操啊，就是华盛顿也比不上。  
"What lofty sentiment. Even Washington might have had trouble matching it.
古公亶父带着自己的家属走了，一直来到了陕西，百姓们也都追随他去了陕西。  
Gugong Danfu took his family and moved into Shaanxi, and the people followed him there.
在那里，周围的部落们知道古公亶父十分贤明，也都过来归附。  
There, the surrounding tribes knew that Gu Gong Danfu was very wise, and they all came to join him.
于是古公亶父修建城墙，建立官府，为民服务。  
So Gugong Danfu built walls, set up offices, and started governing for the people.
说起来，算是真正的公仆。  
In that sense, he was a real public servant.
古公亶父的大儿子叫太伯，二儿子叫虞仲，小儿子叫季历。  
The eldest son of Gu Gong Danfu was called Taibo, the second son was called Yu Zhong, and the younger son was called Ji Li.
季历的儿子叫姬昌，大家都看好他。  
Jili's son was Ji Chang, and everyone thought highly of him.
于是太伯和虞仲为了把王位让给季历，双双逃往南方，后来建立了吴国，成为吴国国君，这是后话。  
So Taibo and Yu Zhong fled south together in order to leave the succession to Jili; later they founded Wu and became its rulers, though that is a later story.
后来季历继位，季历之后是姬昌，也就是周文王。  
Later Ji Li succeeded to the throne, and after Ji Li was Ji Chang, also known as King Wen of Zhou.
周文王发明了“周易”，据专家考证，这是自古以来最伟大的预测学，现在世界上有许多“周易”研究会，夜以继日地想搞懂几成，在可预见的将来，这个研究还将继续下去。  
King Wen of Zhou was credited with creating the *Book of Changes*. According to the experts, it was the greatest system of divination（占卜） ever devised, and people around the world are still studying it day and night, with no end in sight.
没有“周易”，后世的帝王连坟地选在哪儿心里都没谱儿，也就没有今天看上去宏伟壮观的这陵那陵，甚至连故宫都可能盖得乱七 八糟。  
Without the "Book of Changes", the emperors of later generations would have no idea where to choose their tombs, and there would be no mausoleums as majestic as they look today. Even the Forbidden City might have been built in a mess.
所以，周文王的出现，是中国历史上的一件大事。  
So the appearance of King Wen of Zhou was a major event in Chinese history.
周文王可没有他爷爷那么慈悲，先后多次讨伐戎狄，让鬼子闻风丧胆。  
King Wen was nowhere near as gentle as his grandfather. He campaigned against the Rong and Di again and again and made them tremble at his name.
文王之后，武王灭了商朝，建立周朝。  
After King Wen came King Wu, who destroyed the Shang and founded the Zhou dynasty.
需要提醒的是，据传文王有九十九个儿子，后来分封各地，成了许多姓氏的祖先。  
It should be reminded that it is said that King Wen had ninety-nine sons, who were later entrusted to various places and became the ancestors of many surnames.
在汉族常见的姓氏中，大约有一半是出自文王后代。  
Among the common surnames of the Han people, about half are descendants of King Wen.
或者说，当今的中国人，几乎人人身上都流着周文王的血液。  
Put another way, almost everyone in China today could be said to carry the blood of King Wen of Zhou.
读到这里，大家最好保持恭敬的态度。  
By this point, a certain amount of reverence might be in order.
举个例子，当年国共两党打得不可开交，毛泽东、周恩来与蒋介石、宋子文（本姓韩）形同水火，其实若是大家坐下来叙叙旧讲讲历史，就会发现大家都是周文王的子孙，甚至周恩来和蒋介石还都是周公的后代，大家本是一家人。  
For example, the Kuomintang and the Communist Party were at war with each other. Mao Zedong and Zhou Enlai were at odds with Chiang Kai-shek and Song Ziwen (originally Han). In fact, if we sit down and talk about the past and talk about history, we will find that we are all descendants of King Wen of Zhou. Even Zhou Enlai and Chiang Kai-shek are descendants of Duke Zhou. We are all one family.
所以，经常讲讲历史，是有利于社会和谐的。  
So yes, talking about history from time to time really can help social harmony.

### 周朝的统治
### How Zhou Ruled

当时的周朝是很强大的，没有人可以对抗。  
At the time, Zhou was immensely strong, and no one could stand against it.
建国之初，诸侯国一个个都很老实，谁不老实就办谁。  
In the early years after its founding, the feudal states all behaved themselves, and anyone who did not was dealt with.
那时候四面的民族也都很老实，东夷很快就被消灭了，成了姜太公的臣民，也就成了华夏族的一个部分。  
At that time, the ethnic groups all around were also very honest. Dongyi was quickly wiped out and became a subject of Grand Duke Jiang, and became a part of the Huaxia tribe.
一部分逃走的 ，大致就渡海去了朝鲜。  
Some of those who escaped probably crossed the sea to North Korea.
所以，中朝一家亲啊，本来就是一家。  
So if one wants to joke that China and Korea are one family, the point is that they really did begin as one family.
南蛮在楚国的打击下，很快也就服服帖帖了，天上九头鸟，地下湖北佬，大概就是那个时候说起来的。  
Under the attack of the Chu State, the Southern Barbarians soon became submissive. The nine-headed bird in the sky and the Hubei guy in the underground were probably mentioned at that time.
戎狄呢？  
Where is Rong Di?
说起来，话就长一点。  
Speaking of which, the story will be a little longer.
按照《史记》的记载，戎狄们原本是夏朝的遗族，也就是说他们跟夏朝原本是一个民族。  
According to the records in "Historical Records", the Rong Di people were originally the survivors of the Xia Dynasty, which means that they and the Xia Dynasty were originally the same nation.
说来说去，那也就是炎黄子孙了。  
After all, they are the descendants of Yan and Huang.
而戎狄是匈奴的祖先，匈奴又是之后的北方民族的祖先，这么说来，什么蒙古族、朝鲜族、女真族等等，实际上也 都是炎黄子孙，没什么可争议的。  
The Rong Di are the ancestors of the Xiongnu, and the Xiongnu are the ancestors of the subsequent northern peoples. In this way, the Mongols, Koreans, Jurchens, etc. are actually descendants of the Yanhuang people. There is no dispute.
周朝初期，戎狄是要进贡的，叫做“荒服”。  
In the early Zhou period, the Rong and Di were expected to bring tribute（进贡）; this was called “huangfu.”
后来到了周穆王的时候，有两个爱好：第一喜欢旅游，第二喜欢打仗。  
Later, when King Mu of Zhou Dynasty came, he had two hobbies: first, he liked traveling, and second, he liked fighting.
周穆王旅游的事迹不多说了，他喜欢西部旅游，是如今西部旅游的祖师爷。  
I won’t go into details about King Zhou Mu’s travel deeds. He loved traveling to the west and was the founder of today’s travel to the west.
他曾经西部旅游到天山，亲切会见了西王母。  
He once traveled west to the Tianshan Mountains and met the Queen Mother of the West cordially.
打仗的事情说一说，不知道为什么，他就是看戎狄不顺眼，非要打人家，谁劝也不行，于是就打了，戎狄一看大军打来，一个个都跑了，穆王只抓了四头白狼四头白鹿回来，大概这八样东西是戎狄的吉祥物，相当于我们的奥运福娃。  
Let’s talk about the war. I don’t know why, but he just didn’t like Rong Di and insisted on beating others. No one could persuade him, so he fought. When Rong Di saw the army coming, everyone ran away. King Mu only caught four white wolves and four white deer and came back. Probably these eight things are Rong Di’s mascots, equivalent to our Olympic Fuwa.
为什么咱有五个奥运吉祥物，来源应该就是戎狄这 里。  
Why do we have five Olympic mascots? The source should be Rong Di.
抢了人家吉祥物，人家当然不干了。  
Once he seized their sacred emblems, of course they were furious.
就像谁抢了咱那五个福娃，咱也不爽啊。  
If somebody stole our five Olympic mascots, we would not be pleased either.
《史记》记载，从那以后，“荒服不至” ，不进贡了。  
The *Records of the Grand Historian* says that from then on, "huangfu no longer came" and no tribute（进贡） was offered.
不仅不进贡了，还跟你打游击了。  
Not only did they stop paying tribute（进贡）, they also began harrying Zhou in guerrilla fashion.
到宣王的时候，又要打戎狄了，结果这一回连吉祥物也没抢回来，反而打了败仗。  
By the time of King Xuan, Zhou wanted to fight the Rong and Di again, but this time it not only failed to seize their sacred emblems, it also suffered defeat.
从那以后，戎狄就彻底不怕周朝了。  
From that point on, the Rong and Di no longer feared Zhou at all.
到了幽王的时候，戎狄就攻破了伟大首都，幽王也被杀了。  
By the time of King You, the Rong and Di had already broken into the grand capital, and King You himself was killed.
于是，平王迁都洛邑。  
That was why King Ping moved the capital to Luoyi.
说来说去，不该抢人家的吉祥物。  
In the end, he never should have stolen their sacred emblems.

### 周朝的人质
### Zhou and the Hostages

周平王迁都，虽说一时太平无事了，但是也失去了诸侯的尊重。  
When King Ping of Zhou moved the capital, he bought a little temporary（临时的） peace, but he also lost the respect of the feudal lords.
为什么？  
Why?
你堂堂一个周朝天子，竟然被小鬼子逼得迁都，弟兄们的颜面往哪里放？  
You are a majestic emperor of the Zhou Dynasty, but you were forced to move the capital by little devils. Where can you put the dignity of your brothers?
你要不行你吱一声啊，哥几个帮你把小鬼子办了不就行了。  
If you can't do it, just say something, and the brothers can help you deal with the little devil.
眼看大家都靠不住了，周平王这个时候只能依靠一个人了。  
Seeing that no one else could really be relied on, King Ping had only one person left to lean on.
谁？  
Who?
郑庄公。  
Duke Zhuang of Zheng.
郑国紧挨着洛邑，有实力，而且是诸侯里面最 近的亲戚，亲上加亲的亲戚，不仅是堂弟，还是表弟。  
Zheng Guo is close to Luoyi, has strength, and is the closest relative among the princes. He is a close relative, not only a cousin, but also a cousin.
可是，有一个问题。  
But there was one problem.
郑庄公有日子不来了，在家里对付叔段呢。  
Duke Zhuang had not been coming to court for some time, because he was at home dealing with Shu Duan.
周平王心里就有些不舒服，心说你是个中央领导人，不能总是拿着中央的工资干私活啊，你这不是占着茅坑不拉屎吗？  
King Ping was not pleased. He thought to himself: you are a central official, you cannot keep drawing a salary from the center while tending only to your private affairs. That is just holding a post and doing no work.
正在这个时候，虢公忌父从虢国来了，说起来，他是那个虢石父虢总理的儿子，当年虢总理给幽王出了烽火戏诸侯的主意，后来自己也幸福地被犬戎给剁了，也追认烈士了。  
Just then Ji Fu, the Duke of Guo, arrived from Guo. He was the son of Guo Shifu, the old Guo grandee who had once suggested the beacon-fire farce to King You and later ended up hacked to pieces by the Quanrong.
不过，他的儿子就不再担任中央领导人了。  
His son, however, no longer held any top position at the Zhou center.
平王跟虢公聊得挺投缘，平王有了一个想法：“老虢啊，你看老郑这么多年来担任总理职务，时间长了，有些懈怠了，平常也不来中央办公了。  
King Ping got along well with the Duke of Guo, and before long an idea came to him: "Old Guo, Lao Zheng has served as chief minister for many years. Lately he has grown a bit lax and rarely comes to court anymore.
我看，你就继承你爹的遗志，取代老郑，担任总理吧。  
Why don't you carry on your father's legacy, replace Lao Zheng, and take over as chief minister?"
” “这这这这这不行，绝对不行。  
"N-no, no, absolutely not.
”本来好好地说话，听平王这么说，吓得虢公说话都不利索了。  
The Duke of Guo had been speaking just fine, but King Ping's proposal frightened him so badly that he began to stammer.
“有什么关系？  
"What does it matter?
” “大王啊，老郑人家那是实力派啊，我要是夺了他的权，那他不仅怨恨我，也会怨恨大王啊，我这诸侯还想不想干啊？  
"Your Majesty, Lao Zheng is a real power. If I take away his authority, he will resent not only me but you as well. How could a small lord like me go on living?"
”虢公说得实在，自己是个小国，惹不起。  
The Duke of Guo spoke frankly. His state was too small to offend Zheng.
当天晚上，虢公急忙溜了。  
That very night, the Duke of Guo slipped away in a hurry.
他怕夜长梦多，万一平王非要自己取代郑庄公呢？  
He feared that if he stayed any longer, trouble might grow, and King Ping might really force him to replace Duke Zhuang.
还是跑了最放心。  
Running away felt safest.
世上没有不透风的墙，看上去越是坚固的墙，就越是透风。  
No wall in the world is airtight; the more solid a wall looks, the more likely it is to leak.
按理说，天子与臣子的对话应该是严格保密的。  
Logically speaking, the conversations between the emperor and his ministers should be strictly confidential.
可是，郑庄公在第二天就知道了整个事情的经过。  
However, Duke Zhuang of Zheng knew what had happened the next day.
毫无疑问，有人以快 马加急的方式向他汇报了工作。  
Clearly, someone had sent him a report by fast horse.
郑庄公是什么人？  
And what kind of man was Duke Zhuang of Zheng?
别忘了他是春秋第一位猛男啊。  
Do not forget that he was the first real tough man of the Spring and Autumn period.
于是，郑庄公去中央了。  
So Duke Zhuang went straight to the Zhou center.
他要干什么？  
What was he going there for?
辞职。  
To resign.
“大王，您看，我家里有些乱七八糟的事情，总是脱不开身，再说，能力上也有问题。  
"Your Majesty, my household is in a mess and I can never get away. Besides, I am not really equal to the job.
您就让我辞职吧，省得占着茅坑 不拉屎。  
Please let me resign, so I do not go on holding a post while doing no work.
”郑庄公很诚恳地向平王提出辞职。  
"Duke Zhuang of Zheng sincerely submitted his resignation to King Ping.
“兄弟，这怎么行？  
"Brother, how can this be done?
好久不见，正想你呢，咱们多聊聊。  
Long time no see. I was thinking of you. Let’s talk more.
”平王挽留。  
"King Ping persuaded him to stay.
自从虢公给吓跑之后，平王认真思考过，思考的结果是谁都能得罪，就是不能得罪郑庄公。  
After the Duke of Guo fled in alarm, King Ping thought it over carefully and concluded that he could afford to offend anyone except Duke Zhuang of Zheng.

that Chu moved its capital to Ying,  
也就是今天的江陵。  
in what is now Jiangling.  
这两代楚王。  
Between these two kings,  
灭掉的国家数都数不清。  
Chu destroyed states almost too numerous to count.  
有名有姓的。  
Among those clearly recorded  
就有申、邓、息、权等国。  
were Shen, Deng, Xi, Quan, and others.  
其中最耐人寻味。  
Among them, the most thought-provoking  
是邓国和息国的下场。  
were the fates of Deng and Xi.  
毛主席说过。  
Mao once said  
落后就要挨打。  
that backwardness invites blows.  
这话确实没错。  
That is certainly true.  
弱小就要被灭。  
The weak are liable to be destroyed.  
这话也没错。  
That too is often true.  
若既弱小。  
And if a state is weak  
又手里还有好东西。  
while also holding something desirable,  
那就更容易被灭。  
it is even more likely to be wiped out.  

楚文王二年。  
In the second year of King Wen of Chu,  
也就是前688年。  
that is, 688 BCE,  
楚国出兵伐申。  
Chu campaigned against Shen.  
从地理上看。  
Geographically speaking,  
楚和申并不接壤。  
Chu and Shen did not even share a border.  
可楚就是要打。  
But Chu meant to attack anyway.  
不但要打。  
Not only did it attack,  
还是文王亲自带队。  
King Wen led the expedition in person.  

楚军要去申国。  
On the road to Shen,  
就得经过邓国。  
the Chu army had to pass through Deng.  
邓国在今天邓州一带。  
Deng lay around present-day Dengzhou.  
楚军借道。  
Chu asked for passage.  
邓国热烈欢迎。  
Deng welcomed it warmly.  
一来是怕楚国。  
Partly because it feared Chu,  
二来两国还是亲戚。  
and partly because the two houses were related.  
什么亲戚？  
How were they related?  
楚武王的夫人。  
King Wu of Chu's wife  
是邓祁侯的姐姐。  
was the elder sister of the Marquis of Deng.  
也就是说。  
That meant  
邓祁侯是楚文王的舅舅。  
the Marquis of Deng was King Wen's maternal uncle,  
而且是亲舅舅。  
and a true one at that.  

外甥和舅舅见面。  
Nephew and uncle met,  
先狠狠干了几杯。  
and naturally drank several hard cups together first.  
再说些家常。  
Then came the usual family talk:  
“你母亲可好？”  
"How is your mother?"  
“你外婆很想你。”  
"Your grandmother misses you."  
说到底。  
But to be honest,  
这还是他们头一回见。  
this was actually the first time they had met.  
感情并不会真有多深。  
No deep affection could have existed yet.  
在楚文王眼里。  
In King Wen's eyes,  
邓国就是个小国。  
Deng was merely a petty state.  
他高兴时能灭。  
He could destroy it in a good mood,  
不高兴时也能灭。  
and just as easily in a bad one.  
可在邓祁侯眼里。  
Yet in the Marquis of Deng's eyes,  
这个外甥。  
this nephew  
简直像天上掉下来的靠山。  
looked like a patron dropped from heaven.  
以后就指着他罩自己了。  
He hoped to live under Chu's protection from then on.  

酒过三巡。  
After several rounds of wine,  
楚文王话越来越多。  
King Wen of Chu grew more talkative.  
口气也越来越大。  
His tone grew rougher too.  
拍着舅舅肩膀。  
He slapped his uncle on the shoulder  
竟叫起了“老弟”。  
and even started calling him "little brother."  
酒一多。  
With more wine,  
尿也多。  
there comes more need to relieve oneself.  
邓祁侯去解手时。  
When the Marquis of Deng stepped away to relieve himself,  
骓甥、聃甥、养甥。  
three men called Zhui Sheng, Dan Sheng, and Yang Sheng  
也跟了过去。  
followed him.  
看样子。  
Judging by the names,  
他们大概也是他的外甥。  
they too were probably his nephews.  
看来邓祁侯的姐妹。  
It seems the Marquis of Deng  
还真不少。  
had quite a few sisters.  
这样算起来。  
If so,  
他们和楚文王。  
they were also  
也是表兄弟。  
King Wen's cousins.  

这三人劝他说。  
The three men urged him,  
“将来灭邓国的。  
"The man who will destroy Deng  
多半就是这个人。  
will most likely be this very one.  
若不早作打算。  
If we do not act early,  
将来一定后悔。  
we will certainly regret it later.  
今天正是机会。  
Today is exactly the chance.  
不如趁现在。  
Why not use it now  
把他杀了？”  
and kill him?"  
《左传》就是这么记的。  
That is how the *Zuo Zhuan* records it.  
意思其实很直白。  
And the meaning is blunt enough.  
可邓祁侯没有答应。  
But the Marquis of Deng refused.  
十年之后。  
Ten years later,  
楚国出兵灭了邓国。  
Chu marched out and destroyed Deng.  
亲戚靠得住吗？  
Can kinship be trusted?  
邓国死得冤吗？  
Did Deng die unjustly?  
可后面还有。  
There was worse still to come.  

语法说明：<br>
语法标记：`定语从句`、`条件状语从句`、`并列句`。<br>
句子主干：主句核心是 `The man who will destroy Deng will most likely be this very one, and if we do not act early, we will regret it later.`。<br>
难点拆解：`who will destroy Deng` 是定语从句，用来限定“这个人”是谁；`If we do not act early ...` 是条件状语从句，说明为什么他们劝邓侯立刻动手；后半句再用并列把“识人”和“后果”连到一起。<br>
常用短语：
- `ask for passage`：借道而行
- `to be honest`：说到底，说实话
- `live under someone's protection`：靠某人罩着
- `worse still`：更糟的是，更进一步说
