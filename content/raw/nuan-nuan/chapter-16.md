# 《暖暖》第十六章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

暖暖又開了那輛白色車，我將行李箱放進後車廂，發出低沉的碰撞聲。
She was driving the white car again. I put my suitcase in the trunk; it made a dull thud.
關上後車廂，突然覺得冷。
Closing the trunk, I suddenly felt cold.
『原來現在是冬天。』我說。
"So it's winter now," I said.
「是呀。」暖暖說，「上車唄。」
"Yes," she said. "Get in."

車內的暖氣很強，才坐不到半分鐘我便脫掉外套。
The heater inside was blasting. Within thirty seconds I'd taken off my coat.
再過三分鐘，我連毛線衣都脫了。
Three more minutes, and I'd shed my sweater too.
暖暖只是簡單笑笑，沒解釋為何暖氣要開這麼強，我也沒問。
She just smiled briefly, not explaining why the heat was so high. I didn't ask.

二環路出奇的順暢，車子一接近路口也通常碰到綠燈。
The Second Ring Road was unusually smooth. Every intersection seemed to turn green just as we approached.
北京似乎很歡迎我離開。
Beijing seemed eager to see me go.
暖暖說她買了一些北京的小吃，讓我在飛機上吃。
She said she'd bought some Beijing snacks for me to eat on the plane.
「待會別忘了拿。」暖暖說。
"Don't forget to take them," she said.
我立刻收進背包裡，因為待會應該很容易忘了事。
I immediately put them in my backpack. Because I'd probably forget everything soon.

「涼涼。」暖暖說，「跟你商量一件事好嗎？」
"Liangliang," she said. "Can I talk to you about something?"
『嗯。』我點點頭。
"Mm." I nodded.
「待會……」暖暖有些吞吞吐吐，「待會到了機場，我不下車。」
"Later…" She hesitated. "Later, at the airport — I won't get out of the car."
『妳怕掉眼淚嗎？』我說。
"Afraid you'll cry?" I said.
「東北姑娘在冬天是不掉眼淚的。」暖暖說。
"Northeast girls don't cry in winter," she said.
『喔？』
"Oh?"
「在零下三十度的天氣掉淚，眼淚還沒到下巴就結成冰了。」暖暖說，
"Crying in minus thirty degree weather — the tears freeze before they reach your chin."
「那滋味不好受。」
"It doesn't feel good."
『難怪東北女孩特別堅強。』我說。
"No wonder Northeast girls are so tough," I said.
「但夏天眼淚就掉得兇。」暖暖笑了笑，「彌補一下。」
"But in summer, they cry plenty," she smiled. "To make up for it."

「所以……」暖暖說，「我待會不能下車。」
"So," she said, "I can't get out of the car later."
『因為現在是冬天？』
"Because it's winter?"
「是呀。」暖暖說，「但車內暖氣挺強，像夏天。」
"Yes. But the heater is strong — it feels like summer in here."
暖暖抓著方向盤的手有些緊，眼睛盯著前方，側面看來有些嚴肅。
Her hands gripped the wheel tightly, eyes fixed ahead. From the side, she looked serious.

『我不想看妳掉淚。』我說，『如果我再到北京，會挑冬天來。』
"I don't want to see you cry," I said. "If I come to Beijing again, I'll come in winter."
「又是大約在冬季？」暖暖說。
"*About wintertime* again?" she said.
『嗯。』我說，『大的約會，果然還是得在冬季。』
"Mm," I said. "Big dates really do have to be in winter."
「不是在此時，不知在何時，我想大約會是在冬季。」暖暖唱了出來。
"*Not now — I don't know when — perhaps around wintertime*," she sang.
『是啊。』我說。
"Yeah," I said.
然後我和暖暖都沉默了。
Then we both fell silent.

窗外是機場高速公路，兩旁的樺樹已染上淡淡的白。
Outside was the airport expressway. The birch trees on both sides were dusted with pale white.
記得幾天前來的時候，樹木看起來是羞答答的；
I remembered when I'd arrived a few days ago — the trees had seemed shy.
現在卻是淚汪汪。
Now they looked tearful.
暖暖是東北女孩，像潔白挺立的白樺。
She was a Northeast girl, like a white birch standing tall.
而生長在冰凍土地的白樺，原本就該堅強。
Birches growing in frozen ground were meant to be strong.
也只有白樺的堅強，才能長在這，因為她們每天得目送那麼多人分離。
Only birches were strong enough to grow here — they had to watch so many people part ways every day.

首都機場2號航站樓已在眼前，終點到了。
Terminal 2 of the Capital Airport was right ahead. The end.
暖暖靠邊停下車，咚的一聲打開後車廂，然後說：
She pulled over, popped the trunk with a thud, and said:
「自從美國發生911後，安檢變嚴了，你動作要快些，免得誤了班機。」
"Since 9/11, security is strict. Move quickly so you don't miss your flight."
『嗯。』我穿上毛線衣和外套，打開車門，走到後車廂，提起行李。
"Mm." I put on my sweater and coat, opened the car door, walked to the trunk, and lifted out my suitcase.
「下次來北京，記得通知我。」暖暖的聲音從車內傳出。
"Next time you come to Beijing, let me know," her voice came from inside the car.
『妳也一樣。』我拖著行李走到前車門，彎下身說：
"You too." I dragged my luggage to the front door, bent down, and said:
『下次到台灣，記得通知我。』
"Next time you come to Taiwan, let me know."
「我連上次都沒有，哪來下次？」暖暖笑了。
"I haven't even had a first time — how can there be a next time?" she laughed.
我卻笑不出來。
I couldn't laugh.

一離開有暖氣的車子，只覺得冷。
The moment I left the heated car, I felt nothing but cold.
暖暖簡單揮揮手，連一聲再見也沒說便開車走了，我覺得更冷。
She gave a simple wave and drove off without even saying goodbye. I felt even colder.
即使在哈爾濱，也沒像現在一樣，覺得全身的細胞都在發抖。
Even in Harbin, I hadn't felt like every cell in my body was trembling.
拖著行李走了幾步，腦袋有些空白，全身沒了力氣。
I walked a few steps, dragging my luggage. My mind went blank; my body lost all strength.
鬆開手，背靠躺著牆壁，閉上眼睛。
I let go of the handle, leaned against the wall, and closed my eyes.
開始準備接受暖暖不在了的事實。
Beginning to accept the fact that she was gone.

這次來到北京待了四個晚上，只有兩晚在飯店，
This trip to Beijing — four nights, only two at the hotel.
其餘兩晚在北京往返哈爾濱的火車上。
The other two on the train between Beijing and Harbin.
蘇州、杭州、上海、北京、哈爾濱，我似乎總在奔波。
Suzhou, Hangzhou, Shanghai, Beijing, Harbin — I was always on the move.
要見暖暖一面，三千公里只是一瞬間；要離開暖暖，一步也很遙遠。
Three thousand kilometers to see her was an instant; leaving her, even one step was too far.
我即將回到台灣，回到0與1的世界，跟存摺的數字搏鬥。
I was about to return to Taiwan, back to the world of zeros and ones, wrestling with bank account numbers.
而深夜下班回家時突然襲來的關於暖暖的記憶，又該如何排遣？
And the memories of her that would ambush me on the way home from work late at night — how would I deal with them?

或許我可以做些傻事，或者少些理智、多些衝動與熱情。
Maybe I could do something foolish — less rationality, more impulse and passion.
熱情也許不曾磨滅，但現實面的問題卻不斷挑戰我的熱情。
Passion might never die, but reality kept challenging it.
就像人民幣跟台幣之間存在一比四的換算公式一樣，
Like the one-to-four conversion rate between RMB and Taiwan dollars,
我試著找出熱情與現實、台灣與北京之間的換算公式。
I tried to find the conversion formula between passion and reality, between Taiwan and Beijing.
也就是說，雖然熱情依舊，但心裡總不時浮現一個問題：
In other words, although passion remained, a question kept surfacing:
燃燒熱情產生能量足以推進的距離，夠不夠讓我接近暖暖？
Was the energy generated by burning passion enough to propel me close enough to her?

我可以算出北京到香港、香港到台北的距離，這些距離並不遠；
I could calculate Beijing to Hong Kong, Hong Kong to Taipei — those distances weren't far.
但我跟暖暖之間最遠的距離，是台灣海峽。
But the farthest distance between us was the Taiwan Strait.
那不是用長度、寬度或深度所能量測的距離。
A distance that couldn't be measured by length, width, or depth.
用我將會一點一滴消逝的純粹所做成的船，可以航行並穿越台灣海峽嗎？
Could a boat made of my slowly fading purity sail across it?

台灣把另一半叫牽手；北京則叫愛人。
In Taiwan, your other half is called *qianshou* — hand-holder; in Beijing, *airen* — lover.
我將來應該會找到生命中的牽手，暖暖也會找到屬於她的愛人。
I would probably find my hand-holder someday; she would find her lover too.
如果我們連另一半的稱呼都不同，那麼大概很難成為彼此的另一半吧。
If we couldn't even agree on what to call our other halves, it would probably be hard to become each other's.

手機突然響了。
My phone suddenly rang.
一看來電顯示「暖暖」，吃了一驚，趕緊按下接聽鍵。
The caller ID said *Nuannuan*. Startled, I quickly answered.
我精神一振，叫了聲：『暖暖！』
I perked up. "Nuannuan!"

「涼涼！」暖暖的聲音，「快來機場外頭，下雪了！」
"Liangliang!" Her voice. "Come outside the airport — it's snowing!"
抬起頭，天色有些灰暗，颳了點風，少許白點在風中亂飄。
I looked up. The sky was gray, a breeze was blowing, and a few white specks were drifting in the wind.
『我看到了。』我說。
"I see it," I said.
「咋這麼快？」
"How come so fast?"
『因為我還沒走進機場。』
"Because I haven't gone into the airport yet."
「呀？」
"Huh?"

我下意識四處張望，以為或許暖暖正躲著準備趁我不注意時突然現身。
I instinctively looked around — maybe she was hiding, waiting to surprise me.
但只見從停止的車輛中拿出行李走進機場的人，直線移動、方向單調。
But all I saw were people taking luggage from stopped cars and walking into the airport — linear, monotonous.
空中的雪呈弧線亂飄，落地後還不安分地走了幾步，似乎不甘心停止。
The snow swirled in arcs through the air, skittered a few steps on the ground, as if unwilling to stop.
『妳還在開車嗎？』
"Are you still driving?"
「當然的呀。我還得把車開回單位去呢。」
"Of course. I still have to return the car to the office."
我心一沉，地上的雪終於放棄移動。
My heart sank. The snow on the ground finally stopped moving.

『妳打電話來，只是為了告訴我下雪了嗎？』
"You called just to tell me it's snowing?"
「你喜歡下雪不是嗎？」暖暖說，「我想聽聽你高興的聲音。」
"You like snow, don't you?" she said. "I wanted to hear you happy."
『我……』頓了頓，提起精神說：『很高興。』
"I…" I paused, then rallied. "I'm very happy."
「這是高興的聲音嗎？聽起來不像。」
"Is that a happy voice? Doesn't sound like it."
『因為有些冷。』
"Because I'm a little cold."
「冷嗎？」
"Cold?"
『嗯。』
"Mm."

暖暖停頓十秒後，說：「那就進去唄。凍壞了可糟。」
She paused for ten seconds, then said: "Then go inside. Don't freeze."
『我再多看會吧。』我試著擠出笑聲，『畢竟台灣看不到的。』
"Let me watch a little longer." I tried to force a laugh. "Can't see this in Taiwan."
雪變大了，風也更強，地越來越白，身體越來越冷。
The snow grew heavier; the wind strengthened; the ground grew whiter; my body grew colder.
「還是進去唄。」暖暖說。
"Just go inside," she said.

拉高衣領，縮著脖子，拿著手機的左手有些僵，右手來換班。
I pulled up my collar, shrank my neck, swapped the phone from my stiffening left hand to my right.
『我……』聲音有些抖，『可以叫妳的名字嗎？』
"I…" My voice trembled. "Can I say your name?"
「你凍傻了？」暖暖笑了，「當然成。」
"Are you frozen stupid?" she laughed. "Of course."
『暖暖、暖暖、暖暖。』
"Nuannuan, Nuannuan, Nuannuan."
「有用嗎？」
"Does it help?"
『超級有用。』我說。
"Super helpful," I said.
「不是瞎說的吧？」
"You're not making that up?"
『不。是明說。』
"No. I'm telling the truth."
「又瞎說。」
"More nonsense."

『再多叫幾聲好嗎？』
"Can I say it a few more times?"
「嗯。」
"Mm."
『暖暖、暖暖、暖暖……』
"Nuannuan, Nuannuan, Nuannuan…"
叫到第七次時，一不小心，眼睛開始濕潤，喉嚨有些哽咽，便停止。
On the seventh call, my eyes started to moisten and my throat tightened. I stopped.
暖暖應該發覺了，也不多說什麼。
She must have noticed, but said nothing.

「好點沒？」過了許久，暖暖才開口。
"Feeling better?" she said after a long while.
『嗯。』我擦擦眼角，用力吸了口冷空氣，『暖和多了。』
"Mm." I wiped my eyes and took a deep breath of cold air. "Much warmer."
「這就是我名字的好處，多叫幾聲就不冷了。」
"That's the benefit of my name — say it enough and you won't feel cold."
『我很感激妳父親給妳取這麼個好名字。』
"I'm grateful to your father for giving you such a good name."
「我也感激您不嫌棄。」
"And I'm grateful you don't mind it."
『妳聽過有人嫌鑽石太亮嗎？』
"Have you ever heard anyone complain that a diamond is too bright?"
「這倒是沒聽過。」暖暖簡單笑了笑。
"I haven't," she said with a simple smile.

我該走了，再不辦登機手續，可能就走不了。
I had to go. If I didn't check in soon, I might not make the flight.
『暖暖，什麼時候才能再見到妳？』我說。
"Nuannuan — when will I see you again?" I said.
「你說呢？」
"What do you think?"
『也許一個月、也許一年、也許十年、也許……』
"Maybe a month, maybe a year, maybe ten years, maybe…"
我頓了頓，硬生生把「下輩子」吞下肚。
I paused, forcing the words *next life* back down.
「也許是一分鐘呢。」暖暖說。
"Maybe in a minute," she said.
『一分鐘？』
"A minute?"
可能是心理作用，我隱約聽到暖暖的笑聲。
Maybe it was psychological, but I thought I heard her laugh.

「嘿，涼涼。」
"Hey, Liangliang."
『嗯？』
"Mm?"
「涼涼！」
"Liangliang!"
我覺得聲音有些怪，倒不是暖暖音調變了，而是我好像聽到回音。
Something sounded strange — not her tone, but I seemed to hear an echo.
手機裡的聲音跟空氣中的回音重疊在一起，就像在天壇的天心石一樣。
The voice from the phone overlapped with an echo in the air, like on the Heaven's Heart Stone at the Temple of Heaven.
「涼涼！」
"Liangliang!"
這次聽得更清楚了，回音壓過手機裡的聲音。
This time it was clearer — the echo overpowered the phone.

我抬起頭，暖暖白色的車子突然冒了出來，出現在我左前方十公尺。
I looked up. Her white car had suddenly appeared, ten meters to my left.
靠近機場的車道已被佔滿，暖暖的車由左向右，緩緩穿過我眼前。
The lane near the airport was jammed; her car moved slowly from left to right across my vision.
「嘿！涼涼！」暖暖搖下車窗，右手放開方向盤努力伸向車窗外，高喊：
"Hey! Liangliang!" She rolled down the window, let go of the wheel with her right hand, and stretched it out the window, calling loudly:
「涼涼！再見！」
"Liangliang! Goodbye!"
『暖暖！』彈起身，顧不得手機從手中滑落，朝她車後奔跑，『暖暖！』
"Nuannuan!" I sprang up, dropping my phone without a thought, and ran after her car. "Nuannuan!"
只跑了八步，便被一輛黑色轎車擋住去路。
Eight steps in, a black sedan blocked my path.
『暖暖！』我雙手圈著嘴，大聲呼叫。
"Nuannuan!" I cupped my hands around my mouth and shouted.

暖暖並未停車，以緩慢的車速離開我的生命。
She didn't stop. She drove slowly out of my life.
「涼涼……」暖暖的聲音越來越遠、越遠越薄，「再見……」
"Liangliang…" Her voice grew fainter, thinner. "Goodbye…"
我繞過黑色轎車，衝進車道拔腿狂奔，拼命追逐遠處的白影。
I ran around the black car, dashed into the road, and sprinted, desperately chasing the white shape in the distance.
『暖暖！』我用盡力氣大聲喊：『我一定會帶妳去暖暖！』
"Nuannuan!" I shouted with all my might. "I will take you to Nuannuan!"

我突然感到一陣莫名的悲傷。
A wave of inexplicable sadness hit me.
就好像握住臨終老父的手，告訴他將來我會好好聽他的話一樣。
Like holding a dying father's hand, promising to be good from now on.
那只是一種根本做不到卻又想用盡生命中所有力量去遵守的承諾。
A promise impossible to keep, yet one you'd use every ounce of your life force to honor.
在漫天飛雪裡，視野盡是白茫茫一片，我呆立雪地，
In the swirling snow, everything was white. I stood frozen,
不知道該如何呼叫暖暖？
not knowing how to call out to her.

我和暖暖都是平凡人，有單純的喜怒哀樂，也知道幸福必須追求與掌握。
We were both ordinary people — with simple joys and sorrows, knowing happiness must be pursued and held onto.
或許有少許的勇氣去面對困境，但並沒有過人的勇氣去突破或扭轉困境。
Maybe we had a little courage to face hardship, but not enough to break through or overturn it.
時代的洪流會將我沖到屬於我的角落，暖暖應該也是。
The tide of the times would wash me to my corner, and her to hers.
我們會遙望，卻沒有游向彼此的力氣，只能慢慢漂流，直到看不見彼此。
We'd gaze at each other from afar, but lack the strength to swim toward each other — drifting slowly until we could no longer see one another.
在漂流的過程中，我將不時回頭望向我和暖暖曾短暫交會的所在。
In the drifting, I'd keep looking back at where our paths had briefly crossed.
我看清楚了，那是家餐廳，外頭招牌明顯寫著：「正宗湖北菜」。
I saw it clearly now — a restaurant with a sign that read: "Authentic Hubei Cuisine."
然後我聽到暖暖的聲音。
And then I heard her voice.

「嘿，我叫暖暖。你呢？」
"Hey, I'm Nuannuan. What's your name?"

～ The End ～
