# 《暖暖》第十一章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

公司在蘇州有間廠，我這次和幾個工程師一道來蘇州。
The company had a factory in Suzhou. I came with a few other engineers.
大概是做些技術轉移的工作。
We were there for some kind of technology transfer.
我們在上海下了飛機，蘇州那邊來了輛車，把我們接到蘇州。
We landed in Shanghai; a car from Suzhou picked us up.

廠方提供了宿舍，我們以後便住在這。
The factory provided dormitories — this was where we'd live.
我們這些台灣來的工程師，雖被戲稱為台幹，但他們總叫我們「老師」。
We Taiwan engineers were jokingly called Taiwan cadres, but they always called us *laoshi* — teacher.
我知道在內地的用語上，稱人老師是表示一種尊敬。
I knew that on the mainland, calling someone *laoshi* was a sign of respect.
但畢竟這輩子還沒被人叫過老師，因此聽起來總覺得不自在。
But I'd never been called teacher in my life, so it felt strange.

簡單卸下行李，舒緩一下四肢後，我立刻拿起手機。
After dropping my bags and stretching, I immediately picked up my phone.
我已經在蘇州了，這個理由足夠讓我打電話給暖暖。
I was already in Suzhou — that was reason enough to call her.
『請問您認識北京第一大美女秦暖暖嗎？』電話一接通，我說。
"May I ask if you know Qin Nuannuan — the number one beauty of Beijing?" I said the moment she answered.
「呀？」電話那頭的聲音似乎嚇了一跳，「我就是。請問您是哪位？」
"Ah?" She sounded startled. "Speaking. Who is this, may I ask?"
我聽出來了，是暖暖的聲音沒錯。
I recognized it — definitely Nuannuan's voice.

『您聲音這麼好聽，又是北京第一大美女，這還有王法嗎？』我說。
"With a voice so lovely, and being Beijing's number one beauty — is there no justice left in the world?" I said.
「涼涼？」暖暖的聲音有些遲疑。
"Liangliang?" Her voice was hesitant.
『請叫我涼涼老師。』我說。
"Please call me Teacher Liangliang."
「涼涼！」暖暖很興奮，「真是你！」
"Liangliang!" She was excited. "It's really you!"
我也很開心。
I was so happy.
從沒想過只是簡單撥幾個鍵，便會得到這麼多快樂。
I never imagined pressing a few buttons could bring so much joy.

暖暖說她昨晚已收到我的E-mail，原本想打電話給我，沒想到我先打了。
She said she'd gotten my email the night before and was about to call me — didn't expect I'd call first.
我告訴暖暖來蘇州的目的以及停留的時間，暖暖說蘇州很美，別忘了逛。
I told her why I was in Suzhou and how long I'd stay. She said Suzhou was beautiful and I shouldn't miss touring it.
『妳來過蘇州？』我問。
"You've been to Suzhou?" I asked.
「我是聽人說的。」
"I've heard about it."
『又是聽說。』
"Always *heard about it*."
「我耳朵好。」暖暖笑了。
"I have good ears," she laughed.

分離了一年多，我們都有很多話想說，但一時之間卻無法整理出順序。
After over a year apart, we had so much to say but couldn't find the order.
只好說些飛機坐了多久時間、飛機餐裡有些什麼、空中小姐應該是嫁了人
So we talked about trivial things — how long the flight took, what the in-flight meal was like, how the flight attendant must have gotten married
生了好幾個小孩而且最大的小孩已經念高中之類言不及義的東西。
and had several kids, with the oldest in high school — complete nonsense.
我們似乎只是純粹享受聽見對方聲音的喜悅，享受那種純粹，
We seemed to just be enjoying the pure joy of hearing each other's voices.
然後覺得彼此都還活著是件值得慶祝的事。
And feeling that it was worth celebrating that we were both still alive.

不知道為什麼，跟暖暖說話的同時，我腦海裡浮現出天壇回音壁的影像。
I didn't know why, but as I talked to her, the Temple of Heaven's Echo Wall came to mind.
大概是因為我們現在都是對著手機說話、從手機聽到回答，
Maybe because we were both speaking into phones and hearing answers from phones,
跟那時對著牆壁說話、從牆壁聽到回答的感覺很像。
just like speaking to the wall and hearing answers from the wall back then.
也想起那時把在心裡流竄的聲音——我喜歡妳，輕聲告訴暖暖的勇氣。
And I remembered the courage of whispering what had been running through my heart — *I like you* — to her.
雖然我知道暖暖一定沒聽見。
Even though I knew she definitely hadn't heard.

『暖暖。』我提高語調。
"Nuannuan." I raised my pitch.
「嗯？」
"Mm?"
『暖暖。』我降低語調。
"Nuannuan." I lowered my pitch.
「說唄。」
"Go on."
『這是聲音高亢的暖暖和聲音低沉的暖暖。』
"This is high-pitched Nuannuan and low-pitched Nuannuan."
「說啥呀。」
"What are you talking about?"
『嘿嘿，暖暖。』
"Heh heh, Nuannuan."
「你到底想說啥？」
"What are you trying to say?"
『這是加了嘿嘿的暖暖。』
"This is Nuannuan with a 'heh heh' attached."
「北七。」暖暖說。
"*Bei qi*," she said.
暖暖並不知道，只要能單純地開口叫著暖暖，就是一件幸福的事。
She didn't know that just being able to call her name was happiness enough.

這通電話講了半個多小時才結束。
The call lasted over half an hour.
掛上電話，我覺得嘴角有些酸。
When I hung up, my mouth felt sore.
大概是聽暖暖說話時，我不知不覺保持著嘴角上揚的表情。
I must have been unconsciously smiling the whole time.
我打開行李箱，整理簡單的日常生活用品，看一些廠方準備的資料。
I opened my suitcase, organized my things, and looked over some materials the factory had prepared.
畢竟我不是來玩的，得把該做的事做好。
I wasn't here on vacation — I had to do my job.

在蘇州的工作性質很單純，甚至可說比在台灣工作輕鬆。
The work in Suzhou was simple — even easier than in Taiwan.
除了人在異地、人生地不熟所造成的些微困擾外，我適應得很好。
Apart from the slight discomfort of being in an unfamiliar place, I adapted well.
倒是下班時間不知該如何排遣，才是最大的問題。
The biggest problem was how to fill my time after work.
同事們偶爾相約去KTV唱歌，KTV裡多數是台灣流行歌曲，我很熟悉。
Colleagues occasionally invited me to KTV — mostly Taiwanese pop songs, which I knew.
但我唱歌難聽，不好意思把自己的快樂建築在別人的痛苦之上。
But I was a terrible singer and didn't want to inflict my voice on others.
所以下班後，我常一個人窩在宿舍。
So after work, I often holed up in my dorm alone.

遇到假日時，我會到蘇州市區走走。
On holidays, I'd wander around downtown Suzhou.
曾聽人說過，蘇州是最像台北的都市。
I'd heard people say Suzhou was the city most like Taipei.
台北我並不熟，不知道眼前的蘇州市容到底像不像台北？
I didn't know Taipei well, so I couldn't tell if Suzhou really resembled it.
我想大概是因為在蘇州的台灣人多，思鄉之情殷切，才會有這種感覺。
I figured it was just the large number of Taiwanese in Suzhou, their homesickness creating that impression.
但有一點類似，蘇州的摩托車像台北一樣多而且也任性。
But one thing was similar: Suzhou had as many scooters as Taipei, and they were just as unruly.
雖然嚴格說來，蘇州的摩托車多半其實是電動車。
Though technically, most of Suzhou's scooters were electric.

經過繁華商業路段，耳畔響起《聽海》這首歌，但唱的人並不是張惠妹。
Passing a busy commercial area, I heard *Listen to the Sea* — but it wasn't A-Mei singing.
「聽兒……海哭的聲音兒……」
"*Listen… to the sound of the sea crying…*"
哭的應該是張惠妹吧。
A-Mei must have been crying.
整體來說，這真的是座會讓人聯想到台灣的城市。
Overall, it really was a city that reminded you of Taiwan.

我並不會因此起了想家的念頭。
It didn't make me homesick.
不過有次在廠裡遇見一個福州人，他用福建話跟我交談。
But once I met a man from Fuzhou at the factory who spoke Fujianese with me.
除了腔調有些差異外，根本就是台灣話，我嚇了一大跳。
Apart from some accent differences, it was exactly Taiwanese. I was shocked.
事實上應該是我大驚小怪，台灣話就是閩南話，當然會跟福建話相似。
Actually, I was overreacting — Taiwanese is just Southern Min dialect, so of course it's similar.
於是每當跟這位福州同事講起福建話，我才開始想念起台灣的一切。
From then on, whenever I spoke Fujianese with this colleague, I started missing Taiwan.
不過大多數的時間，我還是想起暖暖。
But most of the time, I still thought of Nuannuan.

當我第一次想寫E-mail給暖暖時，一看鍵盤上並沒有注音符號，
When I first wanted to email her, I saw the keyboard had no phonetic symbols.
我的心便涼了半截。
My heart sank.
在台灣中文字通常是靠注音符號打出來的，但簡體字是靠漢語拼音。
In Taiwan, we type using phonetic symbols; simplified Chinese uses pinyin.
偏偏台灣一直沿用通用拼音，漢語拼音我完全不懂。
Taiwan still uses the Tongyong system — I knew nothing about pinyin.
才打了暖暖兩個字（嚴格來說，是一個字），我就已經滿頭大汗。
Just typing *Nuannuan* — two characters — I was already sweating.
只好向蘇州同事求救，一字一字請他們教我怎麼拼。
I had to ask Suzhou colleagues to teach me the pinyin for every character.
100個中文字的E-mail，他們幫了我88個字。
For a 100-character email, they helped me with 88 of them.

本想乾脆用英文寫，雖然我的英文程度勉強可以表達事情，
I thought about writing in English — my English could barely express facts —
但若要表達心情甚至是感情，味道可能會不對。
but expressing feelings or emotions would lose something.
比方說「暖暖暖暖的問候溫暖了涼涼涼涼的心」這句，
Take the sentence: *Nuannuan's warm greeting warms Liangliang's chilled heart.*
翻成英文恐怕少了些意境。雖然這句話也幾乎沒什麼意境可言。
In English it would lose the wordplay. Though it didn't have much literary value to begin with.
所以每當要寫E-mail給暖暖時，我總是請教蘇州同事們字的漢語拼音。
So every time I wanted to email her, I asked my Suzhou colleagues for the pinyin.
還好問的次數多了，漸漸摸出一些門道，自己嘗試拼音，
Luckily, after asking enough times, I started to figure out the patterns and tried pinyin myself.
通常也拼得出來，只是要多試幾次。
I could usually manage, just needed more tries.

我也常想打電話給暖暖，但還是認為得找到特別的理由才能打電話。
I also often wanted to call her, but felt I needed a special reason.
暖暖在工作了，或許很忙，我不希望我的心血來潮打擾了她。
She was working now, probably busy. I didn't want to disturb her on a whim.
即使我知道再怎麼忙碌的暖暖也一定不會認為我的電話會打擾她。
Even though I knew no matter how busy she was, she'd never think my call was a bother.
但今天我又有足夠特別的理由打電話給暖暖。
But today I had another good reason.
突然想起我的手機是台灣門號，用來打暖暖的手機電話費會很貴。
I suddenly remembered my phone was a Taiwan number — calling her would be expensive.
如果像上次一樣一聊就半個鐘頭，每天來一通我就會破產。
If we talked for half an hour like last time, one call a day would bankrupt me.
我到街上買了張電話卡，直接在街邊打公用電話，電話費就省多了。
I bought a phone card on the street and used a payphone — much cheaper.

『生日快樂！』暖暖一接起電話，我立刻說。
"Happy birthday!" I said the moment she answered.
「涼涼？」暖暖說，「今天不是我生日呀。」
"Liangliang?" she said. "It's not my birthday today."
『不是嗎？』我說。
"It's not?" I said.
「當然不是。你咋覺得我今天生日？」
"Of course not. Why'd you think it was my birthday?"
『我也不知道為什麼。只是覺得，如果妳過生日卻沒人跟妳說生日快樂，
"I don't know why. I just thought — if it were your birthday and no one said happy birthday,
妳會很可憐的。』
you'd be pitiful."
「涼涼。」
"Liangliang."
『嗯？』
"Mm?"
「生日快樂。」暖暖說。
"Happy birthday," she said.
『妳怎麼知道我今天生日？』我很驚訝。
"How did you know it was my birthday?" I was surprised.
「就你那點心眼，我還會猜不出？」暖暖笑得很開心。
"With your simple mind, you think I can't guess?" She laughed happily.

我跟暖暖說，既然是我生日，可不可以把電話卡講完？
I asked if, since it was my birthday, I could use up the whole phone card.
暖暖笑著說好。
She laughed and said yes.
在電話發出刺耳的一聲嗶提醒你只剩最後幾秒時，暖暖大聲說：
When the phone gave that harsh beep warning there were only seconds left, she shouted:
「涼涼！生日快樂！」
"Liangliang! Happy birthday!"
我還沒回話，電話便自動斷了。
Before I could reply, the line went dead.

那時是秋末，深夜的蘇州街頭有些涼意。
It was late autumn. The Suzhou streets at night had a chill.
暖暖的一句生日快樂，讓我打從心底覺得溫暖。
Her *happy birthday* warmed me from the bottom of my heart.
「暖暖暖暖的問候溫暖了涼涼涼涼的心」這句，如果有意境，就在這了。
The line *Nuannuan's warm greeting warms Liangliang's chilled heart* — if it had any meaning, it was right here.
我把那張用完的電話卡收好，當成是暖暖送我的生日禮物。
I put the used phone card away as a birthday gift from her.

轉眼間來到蘇州快三個月了，再兩個禮拜左右便要離開。
Almost three months in Suzhou. About two weeks left.
暖暖的E-mail老是提到「江南園林甲天下，蘇州園林甲江南」，
Nuannuan's emails kept quoting *Jiangnan gardens are the best in the world; Suzhou gardens are the best in Jiangnan.*
催我一定得去看看，不看會後悔、後悔了還是得去。
She urged me to go see them — regret if I didn't, and regret would still mean going.
找了個假日，跟另外幾個台灣工程師一道去蘇州古城區逛逛。
One holiday, I went with some other Taiwan engineers to Suzhou's old town.

蘇州建城已有千年歷史，建城之初即水陸並行、河街相鄰，現在依然。
Suzhou was over a thousand years old — built with waterways alongside land routes, rivers next to streets, still the same.
難得的是古城區至今仍座落於原址。
Remarkably, the old town still sat on its original site.
古城內五步遇小古，十步賞大古，偶爾還會遇見歷史上名人的故居。
Every few steps you'd encounter a small historic site; every dozen steps a major one, with occasional former residences of historical figures.
這裡與我所待的滿是新建築的蘇州市區大異其趣，也使得蘇州新舊雜陳。
It was completely different from the new-building-heavy Suzhou I'd been staying in — the city was a mix of old and new.
走在蘇州古城區如果還能讓你聯想到台北，那麼你應該去寫科幻小說。
If walking through old Suzhou still reminded you of Taipei, you should write sci-fi.

拙政園位於古城區東北，是蘇州四大園林中最著名的。
The Humble Administrator's Garden, northeast of the old town, was the most famous of Suzhou's four great gardens.
園內以水為主，池邊楊柳隨風搖曳，迴廊起伏、亭閣臨水而築；
Water was the main feature — willows swaying by the ponds, winding corridors, pavilions built over the water.
石橋像雨過天晴後橫跨大地的一道絢麗彩虹。
Stone bridges arched like rainbows after a storm.
全園景色自然，保持明代園林渾厚質樸的風格，具濃厚的江南水鄉風光。
The garden's scenery was natural, preserving the Ming dynasty's robust and rustic style — classic Jiangnan waterscape.

從一踏入古城區開始，街景和園林景觀都讓我有種似曾相識的感覺。
From the moment I entered the old town, the streets and gardens felt strangely familiar.
後來猛然驚覺，不就是頤和園的蘇州街嗎？
Then it hit me — wasn't this exactly like the Suzhou Street at the Summer Palace?
蘇州街原本即是仿蘇州街景而造，即使規模和景觀皆不如蘇州園林，
Suzhou Street was modeled after Suzhou's streets, though smaller and less impressive,
但仍然有些許蘇州園林的神韻。
but it still captured some of Suzhou garden's spirit.

我想起和暖暖沿蘇州街漫步的情景；
I remembered strolling along Suzhou Street with her.
也想起和暖暖坐在茶館二樓，俯視小橋曲水，而蘇州河水正緩緩流動；
I remembered sitting in the second-floor teahouse, looking down at the little bridges and winding water as the Suzhou River flowed slowly.
最後想起蘇州街算字的老先生。
And finally, the old fortune-teller.
在台灣時，通常是讓相片或腦中殘留影像，勾起對暖暖的思念；
In Taiwan, photos or memories would trigger my longing for her.
而眼前是具體景物，不是平面而是立體的，我甚至能感覺暖暖正在身旁。
But here, the scenery was real — three-dimensional, not flat. I could almost feel her beside me.
我發覺思念暖暖的心，遠比我所想像的熾熱。
I realized my longing for her burned far hotter than I'd ever imagined.

我起了到北京找暖暖的念頭。
I started thinking about going to Beijing to find her.
但回台灣的機票已訂，回去後也還有很多工作正等著我。
But my return ticket was booked, and work was waiting for me.
如果不從蘇州向南回台灣，反而往北到北京，會不會太任性？
Would it be too willful to go north to Beijing instead of south back to Taiwan?
而且萬一暖暖這陣子正忙得焦頭爛額，豈不讓她為難？
And what if she was swamped with work — wouldn't it put her in a difficult position?
我反覆思量，拿不定主意。
I went back and forth, unable to decide.

終於到了離開蘇州的前夕，廠方為了慰勞我們這幾個台灣工程師的辛勞，
Finally, on the eve of leaving Suzhou, the factory management, to thank us Taiwan engineers,
特地派了輛車，載我們到杭州西湖遊覽，隔天再上飛機。
sent a car to take us to Hangzhou's West Lake, before our flight the next day.
第一眼看見西湖時，便覺驚豔，深深被她的美吸引。
The first sight of West Lake was stunning — I was captivated by its beauty.
然而沒隔多久，我竟聯想起北大未名湖、頤和園昆明湖，甚至是什剎海。
But soon I found myself thinking of PKU's Weiming Lake, the Summer Palace's Kunming Lake, even Shichahai.
我明明知道這些湖的美跟西湖的美是完全不一樣的，
I knew these lakes were all beautiful in different ways,
但我還是不自覺想起跟暖暖在未名湖、昆明湖、什剎海旁的情景。
but I couldn't help remembering the times by those lakes with Nuannuan.

上了人力三輪車，準備環西湖而行。
I got on a pedicab to circle the lake.
車伕才踩了幾圈，我又想起跟暖暖坐三輪車逛胡同的往事。
After just a few rotations, I was remembering the pedicab ride through Beijing's *hutongs* with her.
即使西湖十景是如此嬌媚，仍然無法讓我分心。
Even the Ten Scenes of West Lake in all their charm couldn't distract me.
正確地說，我已分心在暖暖身上，無法靜下心欣賞美景。
More accurately — my mind was already on Nuannuan, unable to focus on the scenery.
真可謂：眼前美景看不得，暖暖始終在心頭。
Truly: *The beauty before me goes unseen — Nuannuan is always in my heart.*
連坐我身旁的台灣工程師，我都差點把他當成暖暖。
I almost mistook the Taiwanese engineer sitting next to me for her.

從西湖回到宿舍，整理好所有行李，上床後我竟然失眠了。
Back from West Lake, after packing everything, I lay in bed — and couldn't sleep.
在台灣即使我也很想念暖暖，但從不曾因而失眠；
Even in Taiwan, when I missed her terribly, I'd never lost sleep over it.
沒想到在離開北京快一年半時，我竟然人在蘇州因暖暖而失眠。
Now, almost a year and a half after leaving Beijing, here I was in Suzhou, kept awake by thoughts of her.

思念有生命，因為它會長大；
Longing has life — it grows.
記憶無生命，因為它不會變老。
Memory has no life — it never ages.
就像我對暖暖的思念與日俱增；而跟暖暖在一起時的記憶，
My longing for her grew by the day; but the memories of being with her,
即使日子再久，依然鮮明如昨日。
no matter how much time passed, remained as vivid as yesterday.

我要去北京找暖暖。
I will go to Beijing to find Nuannuan.
