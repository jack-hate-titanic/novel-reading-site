# 《暖暖》第七章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

「昨晚跑哪去？」一走進教室，暖暖見到我劈頭就說：「我找不著你。」
"Where did you disappear to last night?" Nuannuan asked the moment I walked in. "I couldn't find you."
『找我有事嗎？』
"Did you need me for something?"
「沒事不能找你說說話嗎？」  
"Can't I talk to you even if I don't need anything?"
『我們還是當同胞就好。』我說。  
"Let's just be countrymen," I said.  
「說啥呀。」  
"What are you talking about?"  
『嗯。』我點點頭，『這個問題很深奧，我得思考思考。』  
"Mm." I nodded. "This is a deep question. I need to think about it."  
說完後我便坐下，留下一頭霧水的暖暖。  
I sat down, leaving Nuannuan completely confused.  

昨晚在床上翻來覆去，腦海裡盡是與學弟的對話。  
I'd tossed and turned all night, my mind full of my conversation with my junior.  
隨著這些天跟暖暖的相處，彼此距離越來越近，漸漸有種錯覺：  
After all these days with Nuannuan, growing closer, I'd gradually developed the illusion  
覺得每天看到暖暖、跟暖暖說說話是件理所當然的事，也是習慣；  
that seeing her every day, talking with her every day, was something natural, something I'd gotten used to —  
卻忘了這是生命中偶然的交會，交會過後又要朝各自的方向繼續前進。  
forgetting that this was a chance crossing of paths — and after the crossing, we'd each continue in our own direction.  
明天的這個時候，我應該是在前往機場的車上，那時我的心情會如何？  
This time tomorrow, I'd be on the bus to the airport. What would I be feeling?  
暖暖的心情又如何？  
What would she be feeling?  

『被變種蜘蛛咬了，會變成維護正義的蜘蛛人。』我嘆口氣，說：  
"If you're bitten by a mutated spider, you become Spider-Man, defender of justice," I sighed.  
『但被瘋狗咬了只會得狂犬病。』  
"But if you're bitten by a mad dog, you just get rabies."  
「又說啥？」暖暖問。  
"What are you on about?" she asked.  
『這世界存在的道理，不是年輕的我所能理解。』我說。  
"The principles of this world aren't something I, in my youth, can understand."  
「你還沒睡醒？」暖暖看了我一眼。  
"Still not awake?" She glanced at me.  
是啊，昨晚一直沒睡好，現在開始語無倫次了。  
Yeah, I hadn't slept well, and now I was starting to ramble.  

來上課的老師也是昨天在北大治貝子園上課的老師，但今天講孔孟。  
The teacher was the same one from Prince Bei's Garden at PKU yesterday, but today he was lecturing on Confucius and Mencius.  
孔孟孔孟，「恐」怕會讓我想作「夢」。  
Confucius and Mencius — I was afraid I'd dream of them.  
雖然很想打起精神，但眼皮是生命中無法承受之輕；  
I tried to stay alert, but my eyelids were life's unbearable lightness.  
一旦它想閤上，力氣再大也打不開。  
Once they wanted to close, no amount of effort could keep them open.  

這教室我已習慣，不覺陌生，有種安定感，像家一樣；  
The classroom felt familiar now, no longer strange — a sense of comfort, like home.  
而老師的聲音則像母親溫情的呼喚：回家吧，孩子，你累了。  
And the teacher's voice was like a mother's gentle call: *Come home, child. You're tired.*  
彷彿聽到耳畔響起：「儒家強調道德倫理，重視人的社會性；道家則強調  
I seemed to hear: "Confucianism emphasizes moral ethics and values human sociality; Daoism emphasizes  
究竟真實，重視人的自然性……」  
ultimate truth and values human naturalness…"  
然後我就不省人事了。  
And then I was out.  

偶然醒來，看見面前的白紙寫了好多次「北七」，數了數，共十七次。  
I woke briefly to find the paper in front of me covered with *bei qi* written over and over — I counted seventeen times.  
「你醒了？」暖暖低聲說。  
"You awake?" she whispered.  
『迴光反照而已。』我也低聲說。  
"Just a brief rally before the end," I whispered back.  
「別睡了。」  
"Don't sleep."  
『我也想啊。』  
"I wish I could help it."  
暖暖拿起筆，在我面前寫上：我要去暖暖。  
Nuannuan picked up a pen and wrote in front of me: *I want to go to Nuannuan.*  
『我醒了。』我說。  
"I'm awake," I said.  

中途下課出去洗把臉，勉強趕走一點睡意。  
During the break I splashed water on my face and barely managed to shake off some of the drowsiness.  
繼續上課時，總感覺暖暖在一旁窺探，我精神一緊張，便不再打瞌睡。  
Back in class, I could feel her watching me from the side; the tension kept me from dozing off.  
終於把課上完後，我鬆了一口氣。  
When the class finally ended, I breathed a sigh of relief.  
突然想到這不僅是我在北京的最後一堂課，也是我學生時代最後一堂課。  
It suddenly hit me — this wasn't just my last class in Beijing; it was the last class of my student days.  
沒想到最後一堂課會以打瞌睡結束，我真是晚節不保。  
I never thought I'd end my last class by nodding off. What a way to finish.  

中午大夥驅車前往紀曉嵐的故居。  
At noon, we drove to the former residence of Ji Xiaolan.  
一下車便看到兩棵互相交纏的紫藤蘿，樹幹虯曲、枝葉茂盛、花香撲鼻。  
The moment we got off, we saw two wisteria vines intertwined — gnarled trunks, lush branches, fragrant blossoms.  
這兩棵紫藤蘿是紀曉嵐親手種植，已兩百多歲了，依然生機盎然。  
Ji Xiaolan had planted them himself over two hundred years ago, and they were still thriving.  
紫藤蘿原本在故居院內，但修路時拆了部分建築物，於是裸露街邊。  
The vines were originally inside the residence, but road construction had demolished part of the buildings, exposing them to the street.  
要不是樹下立了個石碑述說紫藤蘿的來歷，即使你從旁經過，  
If not for the stone tablet beneath the trees telling their story, you could walk right past  
也未必多看一眼。  
without giving them a second glance.  

紀曉嵐故居東側有家晉陽飯莊，我們中午就在這吃飯。  
East of Ji Xiaolan's residence was the Jinyang Restaurant, where we had lunch.  
晉陽飯莊雖叫「飯莊」，卻以山西麵食聞名。  
Despite being called a restaurant, it was famous for Shanxi-style noodles.  
李老師點了刀削麵、貓耳朵、撥魚等麵食，讓我們大快朵頤一番。  
Teacher Li ordered knife-cut noodles, cat-ear noodles, and fish-shaped dough pieces — a feast.  
剛聽到貓耳朵時，還頗納悶，原來是一片片小巧且外型像貓耳朵的麵食。  
When I first heard "cat ears," I was puzzled — they're small pieces of dough shaped like cat ears.  
而撥魚是水煮麵，有點像麵疙瘩，但是頭尖肚圓，形狀像魚。  
And *boyu* — fish-pulling noodles — are boiled dough, like gnocchi, but pointed at the head and round in the middle, shaped like little fish.  
山西菜口味較重，也較鹹，外觀不花俏，但風味獨具。  
Shanxi cuisine is heavy and salty, not showy in appearance, but uniquely flavorful.  
香酥鴨和蠶繭豆腐這兩道菜更是讓所有學生嘖嘖讚嘆。  
The crispy duck and silkworm-cocoon tofu drew praise from every student.  

飯後我們便走進紀曉嵐故居內參觀。  
After lunch we went into Ji Xiaolan's former residence.  
這裡最初的主人並不是紀曉嵐，而是雍正年間大將、岳飛的後裔岳鐘琪。  
The original owner wasn't Ji Xiaolan, but Yue Zhongqi — a general under Yongzheng and a descendant of Yue Fei.  
後來岳鐘琪獲罪拘禁，當時紀曉嵐父親剛好到京任職，便買下此宅。  
When Yue fell from grace and was imprisoned, Ji Xiaolan's father, who had just taken a post in Beijing, bought the house.  
兩百多年來，此宅屢易主人、歷經滄桑，晉陽飯莊也在此營業。  
Over the next two centuries, the house changed hands many times and weathered many storms; the Jinyang Restaurant operated there too.  
2001年晉陽飯莊遷到故居東側，同時開始整修紀曉嵐故居。  
In 2001, Jinyang moved to the east side, and restoration of the residence began.  
隔年紀曉嵐故居終於正式對外開放。  
The following year, Ji Xiaolan's residence finally opened to the public.  

紀曉嵐故居現存只剩兩堂一院，呈南北走向，面積不到原來的三分之一。  
Only two halls and one courtyard remained, running north-south, less than a third of the original size.  
南邊是正廳，目前當作紀念館陳列室，展出紀曉嵐生平及各種相關史料，  
The south hall served as a memorial exhibit, displaying Ji Xiaolan's life and various historical materials —  
例如他當年主持編纂的《四庫全書》和晚年所作的《閱微草堂筆記》；  
including the *Complete Library of the Four Treasuries* he oversaw and his later work *Notes from the Thatched Abode of Close Observation*;  
還有紀曉嵐生前用過的部分物品以及藏書，包括著名的煙袋鍋。  
along with some of his personal belongings and books, including his famous tobacco pipe.  

裡頭有張和人同高的紀曉嵐畫像，是個臉孔清瘦、長鬚垂胸的老者。  
Inside was a life-size portrait of Ji Xiaolan — a gaunt-faced old man with a long beard reaching his chest.  
同學們初見畫像的反應幾乎都是驚訝，眼前這位老者相貌一般，  
The students were all surprised: the old man in the painting looked quite ordinary,  
甚至可說醜陋；而紀大學士在人們心中的形象是風流倜儻、一表人才。  
even ugly; yet Grand Secretary Ji was remembered as elegant and handsome.  
這樣也好，紀曉嵐聰明多才、風趣幽默，如果又相貌堂堂，未免太過。  
Which was fair — Ji Xiaolan was brilliant, talented, and witty; if he'd also been handsome, it would have been too much.  
幾個男同學面露安慰的笑容，可能他們心想其貌不揚的人也可風流倜儻。  
A few male students smiled with relief, thinking that even plain-looking people could be dashing.  
風流倜儻的人也許相貌一般，但不代表相貌一般的人就容易風流倜儻。  
A dashing person might be plain-looking, but that doesn't mean a plain-looking person is naturally dashing.  
劉德華長得像豬、豬長得像劉德華，這兩者意義完全不一樣啊！  
"Andy Lau looks like a pig" and "a pig looks like Andy Lau" mean completely different things!  

「你今天咋了？」暖暖說，「嘴裡老是唸唸有詞。」  
"What's wrong with you today?" she said. "You keep muttering to yourself."  
『是嗎？』我回過神。  
"Am I?" I snapped back.  
暖暖眼神在我臉上掃了掃後，點點頭說：「有股說不出的怪。」  
She scanned my face and nodded. "Something's off, but I can't quite put my finger on it."  
『可能是昨晚沒睡好、今早睡太飽的緣故。』我笑了笑，接著說：  
"Probably from not sleeping well last night and sleeping too much this morning." I smiled and added,  
『妳會不會覺得紀曉嵐的畫像，很像昨天在蘇州街遇見的老先生？』  
"Don't you think Ji Xiaolan's portrait looks a lot like the old fortune-teller from Suzhou Street yesterday?"  
暖暖仔細打量畫像，說：「經你一說，還真的有些神似。」  
Nuannuan studied it carefully. "Now that you mention it, there is a resemblance."  
『妳身上還有銅錢嗎？』我說，『給他一枚，問他在這裡快樂嗎？』  
"Do you still have any copper coins?" I said. "Give him one and ask if he's happy here."  
「無聊。」暖暖說。  
"Boring," she said.  

北邊即是紀曉嵐的書齋——閱微草堂。  
The north hall was Ji Xiaolan's study — the Thatched Abode of Close Observation.  
草堂內有幅紀曉嵐官服畫像，看起來三分氣派、七分自在。  
Inside hung a portrait of him in official robes — three parts grandeur, seven parts ease.  
牆上掛滿字畫，還有一幅孔子的畫像。  
The walls were covered with calligraphy and paintings, including a portrait of Confucius.  
草堂內主要分成待客飲茶、讀書寫作以及生活起居三個地方。  
The study was divided into three areas: tea and entertaining, reading and writing, and daily living.  
整體看來，只是間簡單的書房，顯示紀曉嵐的淡泊與儉樸。  
Overall, it was just a simple study, reflecting Ji Xiaolan's frugality and detachment.  

我們走到院子，院子很小，四周有些草地，西側有個大水缸。  
We went into the courtyard — small, with some grass around the edges and a large water vat on the west side.  
有株兩層樓高的海棠孤伶伶站在院子東北角，在簡單的院子裡特別顯眼。  
A two-story-tall crabapple tree stood alone in the northeast corner, conspicuous in the simple yard.  
正對著海棠樹則有尊婢女模樣的塑像，手裡拿了把扇子。  
Directly opposite it was a statue of a maid holding a fan.  
李老師領著大家走到海棠樹旁，開始說起這株海棠的故事。  
Teacher Li led us to the tree and began telling its story.  

海棠是紀曉嵐親手種植，原先有兩株，其中一株在改造老房時被砍掉。  
Ji Xiaolan had planted the tree himself — originally there were two, but one was cut down during renovations.  
這是紀曉嵐為了懷念他的初戀情人——文鸞而種的。  
He planted it in memory of his first love — Wenluan.  
紀曉嵐初識文鸞時，她才十三歲，是紀曉嵐四叔家的婢女。  
Ji Xiaolan first met Wenluan when she was thirteen, a servant in his fourth uncle's household.  
文鸞性情乖巧、聰慧美麗，兩人年紀相仿，常在四叔家的海棠樹下嬉戲。  
She was gentle, clever, and beautiful; they were about the same age and often played under the crabapple tree at his uncle's house.  
隔年紀曉嵐父親要帶著他離鄉赴京任職，紀曉嵐萬分不捨，  
The next year, Ji Xiaolan's father took him away to Beijing for an official post. He was deeply reluctant to leave,  
臨行前匆匆跑去四叔家與文鸞道別，並給了她一枚扇墜作為紀念。  
and rushed to his uncle's house to say goodbye, giving her a fan pendant as a keepsake.  

幾年後紀曉嵐回到老家，文鸞已亭亭玉立、標緻動人。  
Several years later, Ji Xiaolan returned home; Wenluan had grown into a beautiful, graceful young woman.  
兩人在海棠樹下許下誓言、互訂終身，約好紀曉嵐取得功名後回鄉迎娶。  
Under the crabapple tree, they exchanged vows and promised to marry once Ji Xiaolan passed the imperial exams.  
紀曉嵐初次應試卻名落孫山，一直等到二十四歲那年才終於高中解元。  
Ji Xiaolan failed his first attempt and didn't pass until he was twenty-four.  
紀曉嵐並未忘記當初的誓約，立即託人到文鸞家提親。  
He hadn't forgotten his promise and immediately sent a matchmaker to Wenluan's family.  
但文鸞父親趁機獅子開口需索巨額財禮，親事因此耽擱。  
But her father seized the opportunity to demand an enormous bride price, and the marriage was delayed.  
文鸞並不知道父親從中作梗，以為紀曉嵐早已將誓言忘得一乾二淨。  
Wenluan didn't know her father was blocking it; she thought Ji Xiaolan had completely forgotten their vows.  
從此憂思成疾，身子日漸消瘦，終至香消玉殞。  
She fell ill with grief, grew thinner day by day, and finally passed away.  

「紀曉嵐悲痛欲絕，便在這裡親手種下海棠。」李老師說，「二十年後，  
"Ji Xiaolan was heartbroken. He planted this crabapple tree here with his own hands." Teacher Li said. "Twenty years later,  
紀曉嵐有天在樹下假寐時，夢見一女子翩然走來，站立不語。醒來後，  
he was dozing under the tree one day when he dreamed of a woman approaching gracefully, standing in silence. When he woke,  
知道是文鸞，便向人詢問文鸞葬在何處，但人家回答說文鸞之墓久埋於  
he knew it was Wenluan and asked where she was buried. But they told him her grave had long been lost  
荒榛蔓草間，早已不能辨識。紀曉嵐感慨萬千，寫下《秋海棠》一詩。  
among the overgrown weeds and could no longer be found. Overwhelmed, he wrote the poem *Autumn Crabapple*.  
這段夢境描述於他所寫的《閱微草堂筆記》中，你們可以讀一讀。」  
This dream is recorded in his *Notes from the Thatched Abode of Close Observation* — you can read it."  

「《秋海棠》這首詩，老師知道嗎？」暖暖問。  
"Do you know the poem *Autumn Crabapple*, Teacher?" Nuannuan asked.  
李老師微微一笑，指著一旁的石碑，說：「在這《海棠碑記》裡。」  
He smiled and pointed at a stone tablet. "It's in this *Crabapple Stele Inscription*."  
大夥圍過去看碑文，碑文上說這株紀曉嵐種植的海棠已經兩百多歲了，  
Everyone gathered to read it. The inscription said Ji Xiaolan's crabapple tree was over two hundred years old,  
至今仍是春來花開滿樹，秋來果實彎枝。碑文也寫下紀曉嵐當時的心情：  
that it still bloomed in spring and bore fruit in autumn. It also recorded Ji Xiaolan's feelings at the time:  
萬端慟憐中，植此海棠樹，睹物思舊人，一生相與隨。  
*In the depths of grief, I planted this crabapple tree — seeing it, I think of you, my companion through life.*  
最後附上《秋海棠》的詩句：  
It ended with the poem *Autumn Crabapple*:  
憔悴幽花劇可憐，斜陽院落晚秋天。詞人老大風情減，猶對殘紅一悵然。  
*A withered, hidden flower so pitiful — in the slanting sun of a late autumn courtyard. The poet grows old, his passion fades — yet still he faces the fading reds, lost in sorrow.*  

大夥不勝唏噓，這時也才明瞭那尊拿了把扇子的婢女塑像是文鸞。  
Everyone sighed deeply, and only then realized the maid statue holding a fan was Wenluan.  
李老師讓我們在海棠樹下走走，試著感受深情的紀曉嵐。  
Teacher Li let us walk under the crabapple tree and try to feel Ji Xiaolan's deep affection.  
「紀曉嵐的軼聞趣事總脫不了風流多情，今天就當成是幫紀曉嵐平反。」  
"Ji Xiaolan's anecdotes always paint him as a romantic. Today, let's see this as a vindication of his character."  
李老師說完後，逕自走開。  
He walked away.  

我和暖暖在院子四周漫步，腳步很輕。  
Nuannuan and I wandered around the courtyard, our steps light.  
看見晉陽飯莊推出的「閱微草堂名人宴」廣告，裡面有道菜叫海棠情思。  
We saw an ad for the "Thatched Abode Celebrity Banquet" at Jinyang Restaurant, featuring a dish called Crabapple Longing.  
我很懷疑知道海棠典故的人，吃得下海棠情思嗎？  
I seriously wondered: could anyone who knew the crabapple's story actually eat "Crabapple Longing"?  
『暖暖。』我說，『妳父親為人如何？』  
"Nuannuan," I said. "What's your father like?"  
「提我父親作啥？」暖暖問。  
"Why are you asking about my father?"  
『只是想知道而已。』  
"Just curious."  
「他這人挺好的呀。」  
"He's a good person."  
『那就好。』我說。  
"That's good," I said.  

張老師要所有同學圍在海棠樹下合張影，然後我們便離開紀曉嵐故居。  
Teacher Zhang gathered everyone under the tree for a group photo, then we left Ji Xiaolan's residence.  
李老師買了幾小袋紀曉嵐老家的特產金絲小棗，每人分一些，在車上吃。  
Teacher Li bought some bags of golden-thread dates — a specialty from Ji Xiaolan's hometown — and shared them out for us to eat on the bus.  
經過門前的紫藤蘿時，李老師說有幾位偉大的文人作家如老舍等，  
Passing the wisteria vines, Teacher Li mentioned that several great writers, like Lao She,  
曾在紫藤蘿棚架下，賞古藤、品佳肴。  
had once admired the ancient vines and savored fine food under their canopy.

『如果世上的男女都能以純真的心對待彼此，』我仰頭看了一眼夜空，

「我知道。」暖暖說。  
"I know," she said.  

然後我們都不再說話，單純地坐在一起。  
Then we stopped talking and just sat together.  
我開始回憶這幾天來相處的點點滴滴，想著想著，不自覺露出微笑。  
I started recalling the little moments we'd shared over these days, and found myself smiling.  
「你想起哪段？」暖暖問。  
"Which part are you thinking of?" she asked.  
『嗯？』  
"Hm?"  
「你不是正想著我們這些天做了啥、說了啥嗎？」  
"Aren't you thinking about what we did and said these past days?"  
『妳知道我在想什麼？』  
"You know what I'm thinking?"  
「我知道。」暖暖露出神秘的微笑。  
"I know." She gave a mysterious smile.  

時間剛過12點，嚴格來說，今天就得離開北京。  
It was just past midnight. Strictly speaking, today was the day we'd leave Beijing.  
暖暖站起身說了聲晚了，我點點頭，也站起身。  
Nuannuan stood and said it was late. I nodded and stood up too.  
只往回走了兩步，突然意識到這也許是我和暖暖獨處的最後一點時間。  
I'd taken only two steps back when it hit me — this might be the last moment alone with her.  
我想開口說些話，說什麼都好，但話到嘴邊總是又吞了回去。  
I wanted to say something — anything — but the words kept getting stuck in my throat.  
這樣不行啊，我心裡一定有某些話只能現在說，不說就再也沒機會了。  
This wouldn't do. There had to be something in my heart that could only be said now, that would never have another chance.  
雖然我曾告訴學弟，我不會跟暖暖說我喜歡她；  
I'd told my junior I wouldn't tell Nuannuan I liked her.  
但現在卻有股衝動，想突破自己內心畫出的方格。  
But right now, I felt an impulse to break through the box I'd drawn in my mind.  

我自認有賽車手的心臟、拳擊手的血液，  
I thought I had a race-car driver's heart and a boxer's blood,  
但此刻再也無法維持正常的心跳和血溫。  
but at this moment I couldn't keep my heartbeat or blood temperature normal.  
『暖暖。』我鼓起勇氣開口：『妳知道的。』  
"Nuannuan." I gathered my courage. "You know."  
暖暖轉頭看了一眼我的神情，點了點頭，說：「嗯。我知道。」  
She turned, saw my expression, and nodded. "Mm. I know."  
暖暖，我也知道。  
Nuannuan — I know too.  
我知道妳知道我想說什麼。  
I know you know what I wanted to say.  

「明朝即長路，惜取此時心。」暖暖說。  
"*Tomorrow stretches a long road — cherish this moment in your heart.*"  
我停下腳步。  
I stopped.  
「這是錢鍾書的詩句。」暖暖又說。  
"That's a line by Qian Zhongshu," she said.  
明天就要遠行，今夜此情此景，我大概想忘也忘不掉。  
Setting out on a long journey tomorrow — this night, this scene, I probably couldn't forget it even if I tried.  

『暖暖。』我說，『我會的。』  
"Nuannuan," I said. "I will."  
「我知道。」暖暖說。  
"I know," she said.  
我們相視而笑，各自走回寢室。  
We smiled at each other and went back to our rooms.

Back in the dorm, I wanted to shower first, then pack.

"What did Wang Ke say?"

"Look on the bright side — at least she didn't slap you."

"Yeah," he said quietly. "Look on the bright side."  

洗完澡，剛走回寢室，徐馳和高亮立刻送東西給我。  
After my shower, as soon as I got back to the room, Xu Chi and Gao Liang handed me gifts.  
徐馳送了四片木製書籤，上頭彩畫了一些山水花鳥；  
Xu Chi gave me four wooden bookmarks painted with landscapes, flowers, and birds.  
高亮送的是一套三張的藏書票。  
Gao Liang gave me a set of three bookplates.  
我急忙道謝收下，想起自己也該回送些什麼，但卻兩手空空。  
I thanked them quickly and took them, realizing I should give them something in return — but I had nothing.  
只好從皮夾起掏出兩張電話卡，剛好上頭印了台灣名勝。  
I pulled two phone cards from my wallet — they happened to have Taiwan scenic spots printed on them.

徐馳和高亮都笑了笑，直說沒事。

The bus arrived. Time to board.

上了車，隔著車窗用心看著每張揮手的臉。  
I got on the bus and looked through the window at every waving face, memorizing them.  
我相信幾個月後甚至幾年後，我仍然會記住這些微笑的臉龐。  
I believed I'd still remember these smiling faces months, even years later.

我的視線最後停留在暖暖身上。  
My gaze finally stopped on Nuannuan.
