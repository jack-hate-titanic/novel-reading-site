# 《暖暖》第十章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

開始上班的日子很規律，也很正常。
Work life was regular — and normal.
以前當研究生的日子也叫規律，卻不正常。
My grad student days were regular too, but not normal.
之所以叫規律是因為總是天亮說晚安、中午吃早餐；
Regular because I'd say goodnight at dawn and eat breakfast at noon.
但那種日子不能叫正常吧。
But that's not what you'd call normal.

我現在有兩個室友——小曹和小何，都是男的。
I now had two roommates — Xiao Cao and Xiao He, both guys.
每人一間房，共用客廳、廚房和浴室。
Each had his own room; we shared the living room, kitchen, and bathroom.
他們的工作性質和我類似，我們都在竹科上班。
Their work was similar to mine — we all worked at the Hsinchu Science Park.
我們這類人彼此間熟得快，只要一起打場連線電動就熟了。
People like us bonded fast — one LAN game session and we were friends.
我們三人專業背景相似、說話投機，連笑聲都像突然被電到的猴子。
We had similar backgrounds and hit it off; even our laughs sounded like monkeys getting zapped.

搬進來當天，我便重新組裝好電腦，連上網，發了封E-mail給暖暖。
The day I moved in, I set up my computer, connected to the internet, and sent Nuannuan an email.
然後才開始將行李拆箱，整理房間。
Then I started unpacking.
沒什麼是不能適應的，孤身一人在哪落地，自然會生根。
There's nothing you can't adapt to. Land somewhere alone, and you'll put down roots.
每天七點半出門，八點進公司，原則上五點半下班，但我都會待到八點。
Out by 7:30, at the office by 8. Officially off at 5:30, but I usually stayed until 8.
反正回家也通常是坐在電腦前，不如坐在公司速度比較快的電腦前。
I'd just be sitting in front of a computer at home anyway — might as well use the faster one at work.

試用期是三個月，但我兩個禮拜後就進入正軌。
The probation period was three months, but I was on track in two weeks.
同事們相處也很融洽，不會出現電視劇裡常演的辦公室勾心鬥角情節。
Colleagues got along well — none of that office politics you see on TV.
工程師不是靠嘴巴闖蕩江湖，你肚子裡有沒有料，大家都心知肚明；
Engineers don't get by on talk; everyone knows whether you've got the goods.
而且通常那種特別厲害的工程師，都不太會講話或是應酬。
The really brilliant ones usually aren't good at small talk or socializing.
偶有幾個比較機車的人，但比例比學校中要少。
A few jerks here and there, but fewer than in school.
如果你在念大學，你應該能深刻體會大學裡機車的老師還真不少。
If you're in college, you know there are plenty of jerky professors.

公司裡大部分都是男同事，難得出現的女同事通常負責會計、行政工作。
The office was mostly men; the rare female colleagues handled accounting or admin.
女同事們的外表看起來……
The female colleagues looked…
嗯，用委婉的話說，是屬於不會讓你分心的那種。
Well, to put it gently — they were the kind that didn't distract you.
甚至會逼你更專注於工作上。
They almost forced you to focus more on work.
小曹和小何的公司也有類似情形，小曹甚至說他的公司會嚴格篩選。
Xiao Cao and Xiao He's companies were similar. Xiao Cao said his company deliberately filtered applicants.
「如果找漂亮一點的女生進來，公司裡那麼多男工程師怎麼專心？」
"If they hired prettier women, how could all those male engineers concentrate?"
小曹說，「所以面試時，公司會嚴格篩選，專挑恐龍。」
"The company screens carefully during interviews," he said. "They specifically pick dinosaurs."
我想想也有道理。
I guess that made sense.

對我們這種人而言，電腦就是我們的愛人；
For people like us, the computer was our lover.
而網路就是愛人的靈魂。
And the internet was its soul.
讓我們瘋掉很簡單，網路斷線就夠了。
Drive us crazy was easy — just cut the internet.
我們成天幻想未來另一半的樣子，但不知道會在哪遇見她？
We spent all day fantasizing about our future other half, but had no idea where we'd meet her.
只知道一定不會在公司裡。
Only knew it wouldn't be at the office.

我們不會也不懂得搭訕，因為不擅言辭；
We didn't know how to flirt — lousy with words.
我們拙於表達，因為表達用的是文字而非程式語言。
Poor at expressing ourselves — we communicated in code, not language.
我們很天真，因為電腦0與1的世界黑白分明，不像現實社會顏色紛亂。
We were naive — the computer's world of 0s and 1s was black and white, not the messy colors of real life.
我們常在網路上被騙，不是因為笨，也不是因為太容易相信人；
We got scammed online often — not because we were stupid or too trusting,
而是因為渴望異性的心熾熱到心甘情願承受被騙的風險，
but because the longing for the opposite sex burned so hot we willingly accepted the risk,
即使這風險高達九成九。
even if it was 99%.
但欺騙我們的感情就像欺騙父母雙亡冬夜在小巷口賣花的小女孩一樣，
But messing with our feelings was like messing with an orphan selling flowers in a winter alley —
都叫沒有人性。
simply inhuman.

但我和他們有一點不同。
But I was different from them in one way.
那就是我曾遇見美好的女孩，她叫暖暖，她讓我的生命發亮。
I had met a wonderful girl — her name was Nuannuan — and she made my life shine.
我不用幻想未來的另一半，因為我已經知道她的樣子。
I didn't need to imagine my future other half — I already knew what she looked like.
雖然我不知道是否能在一起，而且恐怕不能在一起的機率高得多，
I didn't know if we'd end up together — and the odds were against it —
但起碼我已不需要想像。
but at least I didn't need to imagine anymore.
從這個角度來說，我的心是飽滿的，很難再被塞進任何女生的倩影。
From that perspective, my heart was full; there was no room for another girl's image.
即使一個五星級美女嗲聲嗲氣、眼角放電、臉上掛著迷人的微笑跟我說：
Even if a five-star beauty cooed at me, eyes sparkling, with a charming smile:
「帥哥，幫我修電腦。好不好嘛，好不好嘛……」
"Handsome, help me fix my computer. Please? Please…"
我也能保持鎮定，然後以零下十度的口吻說：『沒空。』
I'd stay calm and say in a minus-ten-degree tone: "Busy."

所以雖然看不到暖暖、聽不到暖暖的聲音，但暖暖始終在我心裡。
So even though I couldn't see her or hear her voice, she was always in my heart.
我偶爾會發E-mail給暖暖，說些生活上的瑣事。
I'd occasionally email her about daily trivia.
然而對我這種無論何時何地走路一定靠右邊的人而言，
But for someone like me, who always walked on the right side of the road no matter where,
所謂的瑣事既不瑣也不多。
trivia was neither trivial nor plentiful.
有次實在很想發E-mail給暖暖，卻怎麼樣也找不到瑣事，
Once I really wanted to email her but couldn't find any trivia at all,
只好寫：今天是連續第七天出太陽的日子。
so I wrote: *Today marks the seventh consecutive day of sunshine.*
暖暖回信說：辛苦您了。乾脆說說你室友吧。
She replied: *You've worked hard. How about telling me about your roommates?*

我的室友也沒啥好說的，他們跟我一樣枯燥乏味。
My roommates weren't worth mentioning — as boring as I was.
而某些比較特別或有趣的事，我也不方便跟暖暖說。
And some things were too embarrassing to tell her.
比方說，一天不打電動就活不下去的小曹，有天突然看起文學名著。
Like the day Xiao Cao, who couldn't survive a day without gaming, suddenly started reading literary classics.
而且還是《紅樓夢》。
*Dream of the Red Chamber*, no less.
我和小何大驚失色，因為這是典型的失戀症狀。
Xiao He and I were horrified — this was a textbook symptom of heartbreak.

「我今天逛進一個網站，上面寫著日本AV女優的各項資料。沒想到她們
"I visited a site today with profiles of Japanese AV actresses. I never expected
的興趣欄裡，竟然多數填上讀書。」小曹說，「讀書耶！AV女優耶！
their hobbies section to mostly say *reading*," he said. "*Reading*! AV actresses!
像我這種血性男兒怎麼可能不被激勵呢？」
How could a red-blooded guy like me not be inspired?"
我和小何轉身就走，完全不想理小曹。
Xiao He and I turned and walked away, completely ignoring him.

還有一次，小何從浴室洗完澡出來，頭髮還梳得整整齊齊。
Another time, Xiao He came out of the shower with his hair perfectly combed.
他用緩慢且慎重的步伐走近書桌抽屜，輕輕拉開，拿出一片光碟。
He walked to his desk drawer with slow, deliberate steps, gently pulled it open, and took out a CD.
微微向光碟點頭致敬，然後用顫抖的手放入光碟機裡，神情非常肅穆。
He nodded slightly to the CD in salute, then with trembling hands placed it in the drive, his expression solemn.
「你在幹嘛？」我和小曹異口同聲。
"What are you doing?" Xiao Cao and I said in unison.
「我的女神。」小何用虔誠的口吻說，「高樹瑪利亞。」
"My goddess," he said reverently. "Maria Takagi."

「身為你的室友，我有義務糾正你這種錯誤的行為。」小曹高聲說。
"As your roommate, I'm obligated to correct this erroneous behavior," Xiao Cao announced.
「喔？」小何轉過頭。
"Oh?" Xiao He turned.
「所謂的女神……」小曹單膝跪地，雙手合十，仰頭向天，說：
"A true goddess…" Xiao Cao knelt on one knee, hands clasped, face raised to heaven.
「只有川島和津實。」
"There is only Kawashima Azumi."
然後他們兩人吵了起來。
Then they started arguing.

我的室友們是這樣的人，我怎能跟暖暖啟齒？
My roommates were like this — how could I bring this up with Nuannuan?
所以我還是只能盡量找出生活上的瑣事告訴暖暖。
So I still had to dig for trivial things to tell her.
而且這些瑣事最好跟小曹和小何無關。
And preferably unrelated to Xiao Cao and Xiao He.

隨著我的工作量加大，回家時間也變晚。
As my workload increased, I came home later.
這時才開始試著跟暖暖提到一些心情。
Around this time I started sharing more feelings with her.
暖暖。
*Nuannuan.*
昨晚十點被CALL去公司改程式，凌晨兩點回來。
*Called into the office at 10 PM last night to fix code. Came back at 2 AM.*
突然覺得深夜的街景很陌生。
*Suddenly the late-night streets felt foreign.*
有些心慌，還有累。
*A little panicked. And tired.*
涼涼在台灣。
*Liangliang, in Taiwan.*

沒想到十分鐘後就收到暖暖的回信。
I didn't expect a reply within ten minutes.
涼涼。
*Liangliang.*
人在江湖飄，哪能不挨刀。
*Drifting through the rivers and lakes, you're bound to get cut.*
工作壓力大，難免有感觸。
*Work stress runs high — feelings are hard to avoid.*
今早的太陽，總會照亮昨夜的黑。
*This morning's sun will always light up last night's darkness.*
暖暖在綏化。
*Nuannuan, in Suihua.*

綏化？
Suihua?
我立刻回信問暖暖，綏化是什麼地方？
I immediately wrote back asking where Suihua was.
暖暖也立刻回信說，綏化是她老家。
She replied just as quickly — it was her hometown.
她昨天回家，開學了再回北京。
She'd gone home yesterday. She'd return to Beijing when school started.

我腦海裡幻想著綏化的樣子。
I tried to picture Suihua.
想起在什剎海旁，暖暖問我如果她在老家工作，我去不去找她？
I remembered her asking by Shichahai if I'd come find her if she worked back home.
那時也不知道是哪股衝動，我竟然說會。
I didn't know what impulse had driven me, but I'd said yes.
綏化聽起來應該是座大城市，如果真要去黑龍江找暖暖，應該不難吧。
Suihua sounded like a big city — if I really wanted to find her in Heilongjiang, it shouldn't be hard.

我也跟徐馳和高亮通了幾次信，他們剛從大學畢業，也順利找到工作。
I also exchanged a few letters with Xu Chi and Gao Liang. They'd just graduated and found jobs too.
高亮沒忘了他說過要帶我去爬司馬台長城；
Gao Liang hadn't forgotten his promise to take me to Simatai Great Wall.
徐馳則不斷交代：以後到北京，一定得通知他。
Xu Chi kept saying: *Whenever you come to Beijing, you must let me know.*
我相信這不是客套，便把這話記下了。
I believed it wasn't just courtesy, so I made a note of it.

學弟還在唸書，我們偶爾會通電話。
My junior was still in school; we'd talk on the phone occasionally.
「學長。我跟你說一件事。」有次學弟打來。
"Senior. I have to tell you something," he said once.
『什麼事？』
"What?"
「我今天有打電話給王克喔。」學弟的聲音很興奮。
"I called Wang Ke today." His voice was excited.
『喔。她還好嗎？』
"Oh. Is she doing well?"
「不好。」
"No."
『她怎麼了？』
"What's wrong?"

「她接到我的電話，竟然喜極而泣呢。」
"She was so happy to get my call, she cried tears of joy."
『…………』
"…"
「學長，你知道什麼叫喜極而泣嗎？」
"Senior, do you know what *tears of joy* means?"
『知道。』
"I know."
「喜——極——而——泣耶！」
"*Tears — of — joy!*"
『你是打電話來炫耀的嗎？』
"Did you call just to show off?"
「不是向你炫耀，而是要刺激你。我知道你一定不敢打電話給暖暖。」
"Not showing off — motivating you. I know you don't dare call Nuannuan."
『你管我。』
"Mind your own business."
「喜——極——而——泣啊！」
"*Tears — of — joy!*"
『喜你媽啦！』
"Tears of your mom!"
我掛上電話，不想理他。
I hung up. Didn't want to deal with him.

試用期過了，薪水也調高了些，我開始有了穩定的感覺。
My probation ended; my salary went up a bit. I started feeling settled.
有時甚至會有即將老死於此的感覺，不禁全身冒冷汗。
Sometimes I felt like I'd grow old and die here — it made me break out in cold sweat.
暖暖。
*Nuannuan.*
我工作穩定了。
*Work is stable.*
但很怕因為穩定而失去活力，久了便成為雕像。
*But I'm afraid stability will drain my vitality and turn me into a statue.*
而且還是面無表情的雕像。
*A blank-faced one.*
涼涼在台灣。
*Liangliang, in Taiwan.*

涼涼。
*Liangliang.*
沒聽過有人嫌穩定。
*Never heard anyone complain about stability.*
難不成你想亂飄？
*What, you want to drift?*
江湖求穩，亂飄易挨刀。
*Stability is the way — drift too much and you'll get cut.*
而且還沒來北京找我前，你不會變雕像。
*And before you come find me in Beijing, you won't turn into a statue.*
暖暖在北京。
*Nuannuan, in Beijing.*

時序進入秋季，我和小曹、小何開了輛車到谷關洗溫泉。
Autumn arrived. Xiao Cao, Xiao He, and I drove to Guguan for hot springs.
途中經過天冷，我們停下車買冰棒吃。
Passing through Tianleng, we stopped for popsicles.
那時我突然想起和暖暖在紫禁城神武門外吃冰棍的往事。
I suddenly remembered eating popsicles with her outside the Gate of Divine Prowess.
然後想起暖暖問我什麼時候帶她去暖暖，而我回答大約在冬季。
Then I remembered her asking when I'd take her to Nuannuan, and me answering *about wintertime.*
最後由大約在冬季想起離開北京前夕，我和暖暖在教室外的談話。
Which led to remembering our conversation outside the classroom on the eve of departure.

「明朝即長路，惜取此時心。」
*Tomorrow stretches a long road — cherish this moment in your heart.*
暖暖的聲音彷彿在耳畔響起。
Nuannuan's voice seemed to echo in my ear.

回憶依然如此清晰，並沒有被時間弄淡。
The memories were still vivid — time hadn't faded them.
在北京雖只八天，但每一天都在時間的座標軸上留下深深的刻痕。
Only eight days in Beijing, but each day had carved deep marks on the timeline of my life.
不管在生命中的哪些瞬間回頭看，都能清楚看見那些刻痕。
No matter when I looked back, I could see those marks clearly.
暖暖，我很想念妳。
Nuannuan — I miss you.
妳知道嗎？天冷的冰棒真的很好吃。
You know? The popsicles at Tianleng are really good.

冬天悄悄來臨，最先感受到的不是氣溫的降低，而是風勢的加強。
Winter crept in. The first sign wasn't the dropping temperature, but the strengthening wind.
新竹的強風會這麼有名不是沒道理的。
Hsinchu's famous winds aren't famous for nothing.
下班回家時，還被風吹得整個人搖搖晃晃。
Coming home from work, the wind blew me sideways.
打開信箱，發現一封用手寫的，寄給我的信。
Opening my mailbox, I found a handwritten letter addressed to me.
這實在太難得了，可以去買張樂透了。
This was so rare I should buy a lottery ticket.
自從網路和手機發達後，我已經幾百年沒收過手寫的信。
With the internet and cell phones, it'd been centuries since I got a handwritten letter.

等電梯時，看了看寄件人住址——北京。
Waiting for the elevator, I checked the return address — Beijing.
第一反應便是想到暖暖。
My first thought was Nuannuan.
我趕緊離開電梯，走出門，在門口哇哇亂笑一陣、手舞足蹈一番，
I quickly left the elevator, went outside, laughed crazily and danced around,
然後再走進門，來到電梯口。
then went back inside to the elevator.
不這樣做的話，待會上樓萬一太過興奮，會被小曹和小何嘲笑。
If I didn't do this, I'd be too excited going upstairs and get mocked by Xiao Cao and Xiao He.

『回來了。』走進家門，我淡淡地說。
"I'm back," I said casually, stepping through the door.
「第三個宅男終於回來了。」小曹說。
"The third otaku finally returns," Xiao Cao said.
『又是平凡的一天，路上半個正妹也沒。』我說。
"Another ordinary day — not a single hottie on the road," I said.
「醒醒吧，阿宅。」小何說。
"Wake up, nerd," Xiao He said.
我強忍笑意，把信藏好，一步一步走向房間。
I held back my smile, hid the letter, and walked slowly to my room.
在快得內傷前終於進了房間，關上門，身子往後飛上床。
Right before I'd die from holding it in, I got to my room, closed the door, and threw myself onto the bed.

把信拆開，暖暖寫了滿滿兩張信紙。
I opened the letter. Nuannuan had filled two full sheets.
暖暖說她課業很重，睡眠時間變少了，興許很快就老了。
She said her coursework was heavy; she was sleeping less and probably aging fast.
然後暖暖說了很多日常生活的瑣事，也說她變瘦了。
Then she wrote about daily life trivia, and said she'd lost weight.
她還說前幾天買了些炸奶糕吃，知道我愛吃，可惜吃不著。
She said she'd bought some fried milk cakes the other day — knew I loved them, but I couldn't have any.
於是她將炸奶糕放進紙袋，用信紙包起來，經過七七四十九個小時，
So she'd put them in a paper bag, wrapped it in letter paper, and after 49 hours,
再把信紙拿來寫信。
used that paper to write the letter.
「你聞到炸奶糕的香味了嗎？」
"Can you smell the fried milk cakes?"

我聞了聞信紙，好像還真的可以聞出一股香味。
I sniffed the paper — I really could detect a fragrance.
但我相信，這香味來自暖暖的心。
But I believed the fragrance came from Nuannuan's heart.
