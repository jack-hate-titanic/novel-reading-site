# 《暖暖》第二章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

第二天一早，用過早飯後，大夥出發前往紫禁城。  
Early the next morning, after breakfast, the group set out for the Forbidden City.  
同行的北京學生都是外地來北京唸書的學生，但他們到北京的一件事，  
The Beijing students with us were all from out of town, but one of the first things they had done after arriving in Beijing  
幾乎都是逛紫禁城，因此他們對紫禁城熟得很。  
was tour the Forbidden City, so they knew it inside out.  
老師們只說了集合時間和地點，便撒手讓北京學生帶著台灣學生閒逛。  
The teachers gave us only a meeting time and place, then turned us loose, letting the Beijing students lead the Taiwanese around.  
剛走進午門，所有學生的第一反應，都是學起戲劇裡皇帝勃然大怒喊：  
The moment we stepped through the Meridian Gate, every student's first instinct was to imitate an enraged emperor from a TV drama, bellowing:  
推出午門斬首！  
"Drag him out the Meridian Gate and behead him!"  
雖然也有人解釋推出午門只是不想污染紫禁城的意思，實際刑場在別處。  
Some did point out that "drag him out the Meridian Gate" only meant moving the execution outside so it wouldn't pollute the palace; the actual chopping happened elsewhere.  
但不可否認午門給人的印象似乎就只是斬首而已。  
But you can't deny the Meridian Gate has, in everyone's mind, become synonymous with one thing only: beheading.  

如果是我，我的第一反應是：  
If it were me, my first reaction would be:  
咦？怎麼沒經過早門，就到午門了呢？那下個門是否就是晚門？  
huh, did I skip the Morning Gate and walk straight to the Noon Gate? Is the next one the Evening Gate?  
不過我本來就不是正常的人，所以不要理我沒關係。  
Then again, I've never been a normal person, so feel free to ignore me.  
「涼涼，原來你在這兒。」暖暖突然跑近我，「快！我看到你家了！」  
"Liangliang, there you are." Nuannuan suddenly ran up to me. "Quick — I just saw your house!"  
『什麼？』雖然我很驚訝，但還是跟著暖暖後面跑。  
"What?" I was startled, but I followed her anyway.  

跑了三十幾步，暖暖停下腳步，喘口氣右手往前一指：「你家到了。」  
After thirty-odd steps, Nuannuan stopped, caught her breath, and pointed with her right hand: "Here's your house."  
順著她的手勢，我看到一個中年男子正拿著灰白色的布袋裝東西。  
I followed her finger and saw a middle-aged man stuffing things into a grayish-white cloth sack — a *budai*.  
轉過頭看暖暖，她右手撫著肚子，一副笑到肚子疼的樣子。  
I turned to look at Nuannuan; she had her right hand pressed to her stomach, laughing so hard it hurt.  
『非常好笑。』我說。  
"Very funny," I said.  
「等等。」暖暖笑岔了氣，努力恢復平靜，但平靜不到一秒，又開始笑。  
"Hold on." Nuannuan was breathless from laughing, struggling to compose herself, but every time she did, she barely made it a second before bursting out again.  
「再等等……」  
"Just wait..."  
看來暖暖似乎也不太正常。  
It seemed Nuannuan wasn't quite normal either.  

雖然暖暖漸漸停止笑聲，但眼中的笑意短時間內大概很難散去。  
She finally stopped laughing out loud, but the amusement in her eyes wasn't going anywhere soon.  
我想暖暖現在的心情很好，應該是我良心發現的好時機。  
With Nuannuan in such a good mood, this seemed a fine moment for my conscience to surface.  
穿過金水橋，我們像古代上朝的官員一樣，筆直地往太和殿的方向走。  
We crossed the Inner Golden Water Bridge and walked, like court officials heading to an audience, straight toward the Hall of Supreme Harmony.  
走著走著，我清了清喉嚨說：『我跟妳說一件事。』  
As we walked, I cleared my throat. "I have something to tell you."  
「有話就直說唄。」  
"Just spit it out."  
『其實我不叫涼涼。』  
"Liangliang isn't really my name."  
「啥？」  
"What?"  
『說真的，我不叫涼涼。』  
"Seriously, my name isn't Liangliang."  
暖暖眼中的笑意慢慢散去，取而代之的是疑惑不解，然後是埋怨。  
The amusement in her eyes faded, replaced first by confusion, then by reproach.  

「連名字都拿來開玩笑，你有毛病。」  
"You used your *name* as a joke? There's something wrong with you."  
『Sorry。』  
"Sorry."  
「幹嘛講英文？」  
"Why are you speaking English?"  
『台灣的用語在這時候通常是說對不起，我不知道北京是否也這麼說。』  
"In Taiwan we usually say *duibuqi*, but I wasn't sure if Beijingers say it too."  
「你病傻了嗎？」暖暖差點笑出聲，「當然是一樣！」  
"Have you lost your mind?" She nearly laughed. "Of course we say the same thing!"  
我也覺得有點傻，傻笑兩聲。  
I felt rather foolish, and gave a foolish little laugh.  

「喂，你還沒告訴我，為什麼你要說你叫涼涼？」  
"Hey, you still haven't told me why you said your name was Liangliang."  
『一聽到暖暖，我的第一反應就是涼涼。』  
"The moment I heard 'Nuannuan,' my first reaction was 'Liangliang.'"  
「嗯？」  
"Mm?"  
『因為冬暖夏涼。』  
"Because — *warm in winter, cool in summer*."  
「同志。」暖暖的眼神很疑惑，「你的想法挺深奧的。」  
"Comrade," Nuannuan said, looking baffled, "your mind works in deep ways."  

『如果妳問我AB的弟弟是誰？』我試著解釋我的深奧想法，  
"If you asked me, who is AB's little brother?" I said, trying to explain my deep thinking,  
『我會回答CD。』  
"I'd answer CD."  
「啥？」暖暖的眼神更疑惑了。  
"What?" She looked even more puzzled.  
『就像我一聽到陳水扁這名字，直覺想到他家一定有五個兄弟。』  
"It's like when I hear the name Chen Shui-bian, I immediately assume he must have four brothers at home."  
「五兄弟？」  
"Five brothers?"  
『金木水火土。陳金扁、陳木扁、陳水扁、陳火扁、陳土扁。』我說，  
"Metal, wood, water, fire, earth. Chen Jin-bian, Chen Mu-bian, Chen Shui-bian, Chen Huo-bian, Chen Tu-bian," I said.  
『他們家照五行排行，陳水扁排行老三。』  
"In his family they're ranked by the Five Elements, and Shui-bian is the third son."  

「照你這麼說，達芬奇排行老大而且還有個弟弟叫達芬怪囉。」暖暖說。  
"By that logic, Da Fenqi is the eldest, and he must have a younger brother called Da Fenguai." (*qi* meaning "marvelous," *guai* meaning "strange.")  
『達芬奇是誰？』  
"Who's Da Fenqi?"  
「你不知道？」暖暖眼睛睜得好大，「就畫蒙娜麗莎那個。」  
"You don't know him?" Nuannuan's eyes went wide. "The one who painted the Mona Lisa."  
『喔。』我恍然大悟，『台灣的翻譯叫達文西，他並不是老大而是老二，  
"Oh." It dawned on me. "In Taiwan we translate it as Da Wenxi. He's not the eldest, he's the second —  
因為達文東、達文西、達文南、達文北。』  
because there's Da Wendong, Da Wenxi, Da Wennan, and Da Wenbei, after the four directions."  
「所以翻譯名字不同，兄弟就少了好幾個？」  
"So a different translation costs him several brothers?"  
『看來是這樣。』  
"Apparently so."  

暖暖不再回話，緩緩往前走。我跟在後頭，心裡頗為忐忑。  
Nuannuan stopped responding and walked slowly on. I followed behind, feeling uneasy.  
過了一會，暖暖回頭說：「別悶了。我說個笑話給你聽。」  
After a while, she turned back. "Don't sulk. Let me tell you a joke."  
『嗯。』  
"Mm."  
「公交車上擠滿了人，有個靚女不留神踩了個漢子一腳，靚女轉頭慢慢地  
"On a packed bus, a pretty girl accidentally steps on a guy's foot. She turns and says, slowly,  
說：先生，我Sorry你。結果你猜那漢子咋說？」  
'Sir, I Sorry you.' Now guess what the guy says back?"  
『他說什麼？』  
"What does he say?"  
「那漢子眼睛瞪得老大說：啥？你Sorry我？我還Sorry你全家咧！」  
"He goggles his eyes and says, 'What? You Sorry me? Then I Sorry your whole family!'"  
說完暖暖便笑了起來，我也陪著笑兩聲。  
Nuannuan laughed; I gave a couple of polite laughs along with her.  
因為暖暖先學靚女嬌生嬌氣，後學漢子扯開粗啞嗓子的表演很生動有趣。  
Because she'd done both voices — first the dainty girl, then the rough hoarse man — the act was lively and funny.  

「你讓我說一句，我就原諒你。」暖暖停止笑聲後，說。  
"Let me say one line, and I'll forgive you," Nuannuan said when she'd stopped laughing.  
『沒問題。』  
"Go ahead."  
「你剛說Sorry……」暖暖一副憋住笑的樣子，「我Sorry你全家。」  
"You just said Sorry…" She was holding back another laugh. "I Sorry your whole family."  
『非常榮幸。』  
"I'm honored."  
「樑子算揭過了，」暖暖笑著說，「但我以後還是偏要叫你涼涼。」  
"All right, we're square," she said, smiling. "But I'm going to keep calling you Liangliang anyway."  
『好啊。』  
"Fine."  
「那就這麼著，以後你的小名就叫涼涼。」  
"That's settled then. From now on your nickname is Liangliang."  
我點了點頭，笑了笑。跟上她，一起往前走。  
I nodded, smiled, caught up with her, and walked on.  

到了太和殿前的寬闊平台，有學生朝我們招手，喊：「過來合個影！」  
When we reached the broad terrace before the Hall of Supreme Harmony, some students waved us over: "Come take a group photo!"  
我和暖暖快步跑去，在太和殿下已有十幾個學生排成兩列。  
Nuannuan and I hurried over; below the hall, a dozen-plus students were already lined up in two rows.  
準備拍照時，我伸出雙手的食指和中指各比個V，暖暖很好奇。  
As we got ready, I held up both hands with index and middle fingers in a V; Nuannuan looked puzzled.  
『台灣學生的習慣要嘛比V耍帥；要嘛攤開拇指和食指用指縫托住下巴，  
"Taiwanese students either flash V signs to look cool, or spread thumb and index finger to cradle the chin,  
或用指頭抵著臉頰，哪一個指頭都行，這叫裝可愛。』  
or press a finger — any finger — against the cheek. We call it 'acting cute.'"  
我話剛說完，聽到拍照的同學喊「茄子」，在一片茄子聲中，閃了個光。  
The moment I finished, the photographer called "*qiezi!*" — eggplant — and amid a chorus of "eggplants," the flash went off.  
問了暖暖為什麼要說茄子？  
I asked Nuannuan why people say *qiezi*.  
得到的答案就像在台灣要說英文字母C一樣，都是要人露齒微笑而已。  
The answer was the same as why Taiwanese say "cheese": both make you bare your teeth in a smile.  

我和暖暖走進太和殿，這是皇帝登基的地方，得仔細看看。  
We stepped into the Hall of Supreme Harmony — where emperors were enthroned, worth a careful look.  
殿內金磚鋪地，有六根直徑一米的巨柱，表面是瀝粉貼金的雲龍圖案。  
The floor was paved with golden bricks, and six enormous one-meter-thick columns rose, their surfaces patterned with gold-leaf clouds and dragons.  
龍椅和屏風即在六根盤龍金柱之間，安置在兩米高的金色台基之上。  
The dragon throne and folding screen sat between the six coiling-dragon pillars, atop a golden dais two meters high.  
看著那張金色龍椅，開始數龍椅上是否真有九條龍，數著數著竟出了神。  
I stared at the golden throne, started counting the dragons to see if there really were nine, and ended up lost in thought.  
「想起了前世嗎？」暖暖開玩笑問。  
"Remembering a past life?" Nuannuan teased.  
『不。』我回過神，說：『我的前世在午門。』  
"No," I said, snapping back. "My past life ended at the Meridian Gate."  
「你這人挺怪。」暖暖笑著說。  
"You're a strange one," Nuannuan said with a laugh.  

走出太和殿後，我還是跟著暖暖閒晃。  
Once out of the hall, I went on wandering with Nuannuan.  
暖暖的方向感似乎不好，又不愛看沿路的指標，常常繞來繞去。  
She had a poor sense of direction and didn't bother with the signs, so we kept circling.  
別人從乾清宮走到養心殿，我們卻從養心殿走到乾清宮。  
While others walked from the Palace of Heavenly Purity to the Hall of Mental Cultivation, we did it backwards.  
「唉呀，不會走丟的，你放心。」她總是這麼說。  
"Relax, we won't get lost," she'd say each time.  
一路上暖暖問起台灣的種種，也問起我家裡狀況。  
Along the way she asked about Taiwan and about my family.  
我說我在家排行老二，上有一姐，下有一妹。  
I told her I was the second of three — an elder sister above me, a younger sister below.  

「有兄弟姐妹應該挺熱鬧的。不像我，家裡就一個小孩。」暖暖說。  
"It must be lively having siblings. Unlike me — I'm the only one at home."  
『可是我老挨打耶。』  
"But I got hit a lot."  
「咋說呢？」  
"How so?"  
『當孩子們爭吵，父親有時說大的該讓小的，我就是被打的大的；但有時  
"When kids fight, sometimes my father said the older one should give in, and I'd be the older one getting hit; other times he said  
卻說小的要聽大的，我卻變成被打的小的。所以老挨打。』  
the younger one had to obey the elder, and I'd be the younger one getting hit. Either way I got hit."  
「會這樣嗎？」  
"Really?"  

我嘿嘿兩聲，接著說：  
I gave a short laugh and went on,  
『人家說當老大可以培養領導風格，老么比較任性，但也因任性所以適合  
"They say being the eldest builds leadership; the youngest is willful, and that willfulness makes them well suited  
成為創作者。至於排行中間的，由於老挨打，久而久之面對棍子就會說  
to be artists. As for the middle ones, since they keep getting hit, eventually, when faced with the rod, they say,  
打吧打吧，打死我吧，因此便學會豁達。』  
'Go on, hit me, beat me to death,' and so they learn to be philosophical."  
「豁達？」暖暖不以為然，「那叫自暴自棄。」  
"Philosophical?" Nuannuan disagreed. "That's called giving up on yourself."  
『但也有一些排行中間的人很滑溜，打哥哥時，他變成弟弟；打弟弟時，  
"But some middle children are slippery. When the elder is being hit, he becomes the younger; when the younger is being hit,  
他卻變成哥哥。這些人長大以後會成為厲害角色。』  
he becomes the elder. People like that grow up to be quite something."  
「是嗎？」  
"Really."  
『例如五兄弟排行老三的陳水扁，就是這種變來變去的厲害角色。』  
"Take Chen Shui-bian, third of five — exactly that sort of shape-shifting character."  

「淨瞎說。」過了一會，暖暖吐出這句話。  
"Pure nonsense," she said after a moment.  
『我不知道妳還要帶我繞多久才可以離開紫禁城，不瞎說會很無聊的。』  
"I don't know how much longer you plan to drag me around this place; if I don't talk nonsense, I'll die of boredom."  
「喏，御花園到了。」她停下腳步指著前方，「穿過御花園就到神武門，  
"There — the Imperial Garden." She stopped and pointed. "Cross the garden and you reach the Gate of Divine Prowess;  
出了神武門就離開紫禁城了。」  
once you exit that gate, you're out of the Forbidden City."  

從踏入紫禁城到現在，覺得世界的形狀盡是直、寬、廣、方，  
From the moment we'd entered the Forbidden City, the whole world had felt straight, wide, vast, square;  
沒想到御花園是如此小巧玲瓏、幽雅秀麗。  
the Imperial Garden, by contrast, was small and exquisite, refined and graceful.  
園內滿是疊山石峰、參天古木、奇花異草和典雅樓閣，  
Inside were rockeries, ancient towering trees, rare flowers and grasses, elegant pavilions,  
腳底下還有彎彎曲曲的花石子路。  
and underfoot, winding paths laid in patterned pebbles.  
我和暖暖在御花園的花木、樓閣、假山間悠遊，還看到連理樹。  
Nuannuan and I drifted among the flowers, pavilions, and rockeries, and saw the Twin Cypresses.  
這是由兩棵柏樹主幹連結在一起，彷彿一對戀人含情脈脈緊緊擁抱。  
Two cypresses with their trunks fused, as though a pair of lovers were clasped in a long, tender embrace.  
一堆人在連理樹下照相，而且通常是一男一女。  
A crowd of people were taking photos beneath the trees — usually one man, one woman.  
暖暖說這連理樹有四百多歲了，是純真愛情的象徵。  
Nuannuan said the trees were over four hundred years old, a symbol of pure love.  

「挺美的。」凝視連理樹一會後，暖暖說：「不是嗎？」  
"They're beautiful," Nuannuan said after gazing at them. "Don't you think?"  
『美是美，但應該很寂寞。』  
"Beautiful, yes — but they must be lonely."  
「寂寞？」  
"Lonely?"  
『因為在宮廷內見證不到純真愛情，所以只好一直活著。』  
"Inside a palace, they could never witness real love, so they had to keep on living."  
「呀？」  
"Hm?"  
『如果有天，世上的男女都能以純真的心對待彼此，又何需連理樹來提醒  
"If one day every man and woman in the world treated one another with sincere hearts, what need would there be for twin trees to remind us  
我們愛情的純真？到那時連理樹就可以含笑而枯了。』  
of love's purity? At that point the trees could smile and wither in peace."  
「你熱暈了嗎？」暖暖很仔細地打量我，「待會我買根冰棍請你吃。」  
"Has the heat gotten to you?" Nuannuan looked me over carefully. "I'll buy you a popsicle in a minute."  
『…………』  
"…"  
呼，確實好熱。  
*Phew.* It really was hot.  

七月的北京就像台灣一樣酷熱，更何況還走了一上午。  
July in Beijing was as scorching as Taiwan, and we'd been walking all morning.  
穿過神武門後，我又一個勁往前走，暖暖在背後叫我：  
After we passed through the Gate of Divine Prowess, I kept marching straight ahead. Nuannuan called from behind,  
「涼涼！你要去哪？想學崇禎嗎？」  
"Liangliang! Where are you going? Want to follow Chongzhen's example?"  
『崇禎？』我停下腳步，回頭發現暖暖出神武門後便往右轉。  
"Chongzhen?" I stopped, turned around, and saw she'd gone right after exiting the gate.  
「李自成攻入北京時，崇禎皇帝便像你那樣直走到對面景山自縊身亡。」  
"When Li Zicheng broke into Beijing, Emperor Chongzhen walked straight ahead just like that, up onto Jingshan, and hanged himself."  
暖暖笑了笑，朝我招招手：「快過來這兒，別想不開了。」  
She smiled and waved. "Come over here. Don't go doing anything drastic."  
『好險。』我走回暖暖身旁說。  
"Close call," I said as I went back to her.  

這裡有超過五十米寬的護城河，我們在護城河邊綠樹蔭下找個角落歇息。  
The moat there was more than fifty meters wide; we found a shaded spot under the trees by the water.  
暖暖買了兩根冰棍，遞了一根給我。  
Nuannuan bought two popsicles and handed one to me.  
學生大多走出來了，三三兩兩地閒聊、拍照或是喝冷飲。  
Most of the students had come out by now, scattered in twos and threes, chatting, taking pictures, sipping cold drinks.  
我和暖暖邊吃冰棍邊擦汗，她說我好像恢復正常，我說那就表示不正常。  
Eating popsicles and wiping sweat, she said I seemed back to normal; I said normal meant abnormal.  
我又告訴暖暖，台灣有個地方叫天冷，那裡的冰棒還特別好吃。  
I told her there was a place in Taiwan called Tianleng — Cold-Sky — where the popsicles were especially good.  
『冰棒就是你們說的冰棍啦。』我特地補充說明。  
"*Bingbang* is what you call *binggun*," I added helpfully.

"I understood *bingbang* fine." Nuannuan smiled — a slightly odd smile.

"Hey — when are you going to take me to Nuannuan?" she said.  
原來我剛說天冷時，又讓暖暖想起了暖暖。我想了一下，說：  
So my mention of Tianleng — cold sky — had reminded her of Nuannuan again. I thought a moment and said,  
『大約在冬季。』  
"Sometime in winter, perhaps."  
「這首歌前些年火得很，幾乎都成了國歌。」  
"That song was huge a few years back — practically the national anthem."

徐馳手裡拿了台數位相機，說：「也給你們倆來一張。」  
He had a digital camera. "Let me take one of you two."  
我和暖暖以身後城牆為背景，彼此維持一個風起時衣袖剛好接觸的距離。  
With the palace wall behind us, Nuannuan and I stood the kind of distance apart where, if a breeze rose, our sleeves might just brush.  
準備拍照時，我照例比了兩個V，暖暖叫我裝可愛，我說我老了不敢。  
Habit took over; I flashed two Vs. Nuannuan told me to do "cute," I said I was too old to dare.  
徐馳喊一、二、三、茄子，暖暖也開口說茄子。  
Xu Chi counted "one, two, three, *qiezi*," and Nuannuan said "*qiezi*" too.  
我抓住那瞬間喊：芭樂。  
I caught the moment and shouted, "*Bala!*" — guava.  

「你說啥呀。」暖暖撲哧笑了出聲。  
"What did you just say?" Nuannuan burst out laughing.  
徐馳快門一按，似乎湊巧抓住了那瞬間。  
Xu Chi pressed the shutter at exactly the right moment.  
暖暖急忙跑過去，看了看相機內的影像後，緊張地說：  
Nuannuan rushed over to the camera, looked at the photo, and said anxiously,  
「不成！你得把這張刪了。」  
"No way — you have to delete that one."  
我也跑過去，看到剛好捕捉到暖暖撲哧笑容的影像，暖暖的笑容好亮。  
I went over too. The photo had caught her mid-laugh; her smile was radiant.  

我突然想到昨晚聽到的「靚」這個字。  
I suddenly remembered the character *jing* I'd heard the night before.  
「靚」這個字在台灣唸「靜」的音，在北京卻唸「亮」的音。  
In Taiwan we pronounce it *jing* — like *jing* in "quiet" — but Beijingers say *liang*, like "bright."  
所謂的靚女註定是要發亮的，看來這個字在北京唸「亮」是有幾分道理。

「我給你一根冰棍，你把它刪了。」暖暖對徐馳說。  
"I'll give you a popsicle if you delete it," Nuannuan told Xu Chi.  
『我給你兩根，不要刪。』我也對徐馳說。  
"I'll give you two if you don't," I told him.  
「咱們是哥兒們。」徐馳拍拍我肩膀，「我死都不刪。」  
"We're brothers," Xu Chi said, slapping my shoulder. "I'd die before I'd delete it."  
我虎目含淚，緊緊握住他雙手，灑淚而別。  
With tears welling up in my heroic eyes, I gripped his hands and parted from him in a flood of tears.  

「你幹嘛不讓刪？」暖暖語氣有些抱怨，「我嘴巴開得特大，不端莊。」  
"Why won't you let him delete it?" Nuannuan complained. "My mouth is wide open. It's unladylike."  
『怎麼會呢？那是很自然、很親切的笑容，總之就是一個好字。』  
"Not at all. It's a natural, warm smile — in a word, just *good*."  
「又瞎說。」  
"More nonsense."  
『妳看。』我轉身對著她，『我眼睛有張開，所以是明說，不是瞎說。』  
"Look." I turned to face her. "My eyes are wide open. So I'm speaking *with sight*, not blindly."  

暖暖正想開口回話時，聽到老師們的催促聲，催大家集合。  
She was about to reply when the teachers called everyone to gather.  
學生們都到齊後，全體一起照張相，便到附近的飯館吃飯。  
Once we'd all assembled, we took a group photo, then walked to a nearby restaurant for lunch.  
分組果然有好處，吃飯時就按組別分桌，不必猶豫懷疑。  
The grouping really did help — at meals we sat by group, no more hesitation about where to go.  
我和暖暖同一組，同桌的學生也大致有一定的認識，吃起飯來已經不難。  
Nuannuan and I were in the same group, and we knew the others at our table well enough by now that the meal felt easy.  
這頓飯吃的是水餃、餛飩再加上點麵食，天氣熱我胃口不好，沒吃多少。  
We had dumplings, wontons, and some noodle dishes; with the heat my appetite was poor, and I didn't eat much.  

飯後要去逛北海，北海是皇家御苑，就在紫禁城西北方，很近。  
After lunch we headed to Beihai, the imperial park just northwest of the Forbidden City — a short walk away.  
前門西側有座圓形團城，團城上承光殿內北面的木刻雕龍佛龕內，  
On the west side of the front gate stood the round Tuancheng — Round City — and inside its Hall of Receiving Light, in a wood-carved dragon shrine on the north wall,

玉佛潔白無暇，散發清潤光澤，可惜左臂有一道刀痕，是八國聯軍所為。  
The jade Buddha was flawlessly white, with a cool gleam — though the left arm bore a sword mark, left by the Eight-Nation Alliance.  
我猜是因為八國都想要，於是想把玉佛切成八塊，但是沒有成功。  
I figured all eight nations wanted it and tried to slice it into eight pieces, without success.  
可見玉佛是絕美的藝術品，讓人在殺人放火之餘還可冷靜考慮公平分配。  
Which only proved how exquisite it was, that even amid murder and arson men could pause to think about fair shares.  

承光殿前有個藍琉璃瓦頂的亭子，亭中石蓮花座上擺放一個橢圓形玉甕。  
In front of the hall stood a pavilion with blue-glazed roof tiles; on a stone lotus base inside sat an oval jade urn.  
玉甕是墨綠色帶有白色花紋，高七十公分，周長約五米，簡直像浴缸。  
The urn was dark green with white veining, seventy centimeters tall and five meters around — practically a bathtub.  
浴缸是玉缸，玉缸像浴缸，道是浴缸卻玉缸，怎把玉缸當浴缸。好繞舌。  
Bathtub is jade tub, jade tub like bathtub, says it's a bathtub but it's a jade tub, how to take a jade tub for a bathtub. A real tongue-twister.  
北京李老師說這是元世祖忽必烈入主北京後，為大宴群臣犒賞將士，  
Teacher Li from Beijing said it had been made after Kublai Khan of the Yuan took Beijing and wanted a great feast for his ministers and soldiers.  
令工匠開採整塊玉石再精雕細刻而成，作為酒甕，可盛酒三十幾石。  
He had craftsmen quarry a single jade boulder and carve it into a wine vessel, capable of holding more than thirty *dan* of liquor.  
玉的白紋勾勒出洶湧波浪、漩渦激流，張牙舞爪的海龍上半身探出水面；  
The white veins traced surging waves, swirling rapids, and a clawed sea-dragon rearing half out of the water;  
又有豬、馬、犀牛等遍體生鱗的動物，像是神話裡龍宮中的獸形神怪。  
along with scaled pigs, horses, and rhinos — beasts out of the mythical undersea palace of the Dragon King.  
整體雕刻風格顯現出游牧民族剽悍豪放的氣魄。  
The whole carving had the fierce, unrestrained spirit of a nomadic people.  

「乾隆年間對這玉甕又修飾了四次，由於元、清的琢玉技法、風格不同，  
"In the Qianlong era they reworked the urn four times. Yuan and Qing jade-carving styles and methods differ,  
因此可以區分出修飾過的差異。」李老師說，「同學們看得出來嗎？」  
so you can tell where the alterations are." Teacher Li said, "Can you students spot it?"  
大夥仔細打量這玉甕，議論紛紛。暖暖問我：「你看得出來嗎？」  
Everyone studied the urn intently, murmuring. Nuannuan asked me, "Can you tell?"  
『當然。』我點點頭，『元代雕刻的線條較圓，清代的線條則較輕。』  
"Of course," I nodded. "The Yuan lines are rounder; the Qing lines are lighter."  
「是嗎？」暖暖身子微彎，聚精會神看著玉甕。  
"Really?" Nuannuan leaned forward, peering closely.  
『元代圓，清代輕。』我說，『這是朝代名稱背後的深意。』  
"*Yuan* means round, *Qing* means light," I said. "That's the deep meaning behind the dynasty names."  
暖暖先是一楞，隨即直起身，轉頭指著我說：「明明不懂還充內行。」  
Nuannuan was momentarily stunned, then straightened and pointed at me. "You don't know a thing and you're playing the expert."  
我當然不懂，如果這麼細微的差異都看得出來，我早就改行當米雕師了。  
Of course I had no idea; if I could spot differences that subtle, I'd have switched careers and become a rice-grain engraver.  

北海其實是湖，湖中有座瓊島，下團城後走漢白玉砌成的永安橋可直達。  
Beihai is actually a lake, with Qionghua Island in its center; from Tuancheng you reach it via the Yong'an Bridge of white marble.  
瓊島上有座白塔，暖暖說這是北海的標誌，塔中還有兩粒舍利子。  
On the island stands a White Pagoda — Nuannuan said it's Beihai's signature, and that two relics are kept inside.  
登上白塔，朝四面遠眺，視野很好，可看到北京中心一帶的建築。  
We climbed the pagoda; the view was excellent, taking in the buildings around central Beijing.  
瓊島北面有船，可穿過湖面到北岸，同學們大多選擇上船；  
On the island's north side were boats that crossed to the far shore, and most of the group chose to go by boat;  
但我想從東面走陟山橋到東岸，再繞湖而行。  
but I wanted to take the Zhi Shan bridge on the east, walk to the east bank, and then around the lake.  

暖暖說不成，現在天熱，萬一我熱暈了，又要說些如果世上的男女都能以  
Nuannuan said no — the heat was bad, and if it got to me I'd start spouting things like "if every man and woman in the world  
純真的心對待彼此，到那時北海就可以含笑而乾了之類的渾話。  
treated each other with sincere hearts, then Beihai could smile and dry up" — that sort of nonsense.  
『算命的說我這個月忌水。』我還是搖搖頭。  
"The fortune-teller said I should avoid water this month," I said, shaking my head.  
「還瞎說。」暖暖告訴身旁的人，「同志們，把他拉上船！」  
"More nonsense." She turned to the others. "Comrades, drag him onto the boat!"  
兩個男同學一左一右把我架上船，暖暖得意地笑了。  
Two male classmates, one on each side, hauled me aboard; Nuannuan grinned in triumph.  

下了船，一行人走到九龍壁。  
After we got off the boat, we walked to the Nine-Dragon Screen.  
九龍壁雙面都有九條大龍，而且壁面上有獨一無二的七彩琉璃磚，  
Nine giant dragons coil along each side of the screen, on uniquely seven-colored glazed tiles,  
我早在台灣的教科書課本上久仰大名。  
something I'd long admired in Taiwanese textbooks.  
我特地叫來徐馳，請他幫我拍張獨照，我還是在九龍壁前比了兩個V。  
I called Xu Chi over to take a solo photo of me, and once again I flashed two Vs in front of the screen.  
「龍動了唷。」暖暖笑說。  
"The dragons are moving," Nuannuan laughed.  
我回過頭，色彩鮮豔的琉璃再加上光的反射，還真有龍動起來的錯覺。  
I turned back; with the bright glaze and the reflections of light, the dragons really did seem to stir.  

離開九龍壁，經過五龍亭，再沿西岸走到西門，車子已在西門外等候。  
Leaving the screen, we passed the Five Dragon Pavilions and walked down the western shore to the west gate, where the bus was waiting.  
上了車，打了個盹後，就回到睡覺的大學。（沒有侮辱這所大學的意思）  
We got on, took a nap, and were back at the university where we slept. (No offense to said university intended.)  
簡單洗把臉，待會有個學者要來上課，是關於故宮的文化和歷史方面。  
A quick wash of the face, then a scholar was coming to lecture on the culture and history of the Forbidden City.  
課上得還算有趣，不是寫黑板，而是用power point放映很多圖片。  
The lecture was reasonably interesting — no blackboard, but plenty of slides on PowerPoint.  
上完課後，還得補昨晚沒做的自我介紹。  
Afterward we still had to do the self-introductions we'd skipped the night before.  
老師們也希望台灣學生發表一下對北京或故宮有何感想。  
The teachers also wanted the Taiwanese students to share impressions of Beijing or the Forbidden City.  

自我介紹形式上的意義大於實質上的意義，因為同學們已經混得很熟。  
The introductions were more form than substance by then — everyone already knew everyone else.  
令我傷腦筋的，是所謂「感想」這東西。  
What gave me a headache were the "impressions."  
我回想起在機場等待班機飛離台灣時，心裡裝滿興奮，裝不下別的。  
Back at the Taiwan airport waiting to fly out, my heart had been full of excitement, with no room for anything else.  
飛到香港要轉飛北京前，在登機口看到「北京」兩字，  
At the Hong Kong gate, transferring to Beijing, I'd seen the two characters "北京",  
興奮感變透明，雖然存在，卻好像不真實。  
and the excitement had turned transparent — present, yet somehow unreal.  

北京這地名一直安詳地躺在我小學、中學甚至是大學的課本裡。  
The name "Beijing" had lain quietly in my textbooks all through primary, secondary, and university.  
我常常聽見他的聲音，卻從未看過他的長相。  
I'd often heard its voice, but had never seen its face.  
我無法想像一旦碰觸後，觸感是什麼？  
I couldn't picture what touching it would feel like.  
這有點像聽了某人的歌一輩子，有天突然要跑去跟他握手。  
It was like listening to someone's songs your whole life and suddenly being told you were going to shake their hand.  
握完了手，你問我感想是什麼？  
After the handshake, you ask me how it felt.  
我只能說請你等等，我要問一下我的右手。  
I can only say — please wait, I have to ask my right hand.  

如今我站在台上，說完自己的名字後，我得說出握完手的感想。  
Now I stood on the platform, having said my name, and was supposed to share my post-handshake feelings.  
我能張開右手告訴他們 talk to this hand 嗎？  
Could I open my right hand and tell them, "talk to this hand"?  
我只能說故宮大、北京更大，連中飯吃的水餃和餛飩都比台灣大。  
All I could say was that the palace was big, Beijing bigger, and even our dumplings and wontons at lunch were bigger than Taiwan's.  
『總之就是一個大字。』我下了結論。  
"In a word, *big*," I concluded.  

「然後呢？」北京李老師問。  
"And?" Teacher Li from Beijing asked.  
『因為大，所以讓人覺得渺小。』  
"Because it's big, it makes you feel small."  
「還有呢？」北京張老師問。  
"And?" Teacher Zhang from Beijing asked.  
『嗯……』我想了一下，『渺小會讓人學會謙卑。不過我本來就是個謙卑  
"Mm…" I thought about it. "Feeling small teaches humility. But I'm already a humble  
的人，而且五成謙、五成卑，符合中庸之道。到了北京看完故宮，變為  
person — fifty percent *qian*, fifty percent *bei* — perfectly balanced. After seeing the Forbidden City,  
兩成謙、八成卑，有點卑過頭了。我應該再去看看一些渺小的事物才能  
I'm down to twenty *qian* and eighty *bei* — too much *bei*. I'll need to go look at some small things  
矯正回來。』  
to balance back out."  

全場像電影開場前的安靜。  
The room went as quiet as a theater right before the movie starts.  
『我可以下台了嗎？』等了一會，我說。  
"May I get off the stage now?" I said after a pause.  
不等老師開口，全體同學迫不及待拍手歡送我下台。  
Without waiting for a teacher to answer, the students burst into applause, clearly eager to send me off.  
『怎麼樣？』我坐回位子，轉頭問暖暖，『很令人動容吧？』  
"How was it?" I asked Nuannuan as I sat down. "Pretty moving, right?"  
「總之就是一個瞎字。」暖暖說。  
"In a word, *bullshit*," Nuannuan said.  

自我介紹兼感想發表會結束，便是令我期待已久的晚餐時分。  
Once the introductions and impressions were over, it was finally time for the dinner I'd been looking forward to.  
因為中午吃得少，晚上餓得快。  
With so little lunch, I'd gotten hungry quickly.  
走進餐館前，我特地打量一下招牌，發現「渝菜」這個關鍵字。  
Before going in, I made a point of checking the sign and noticed the word *Yu cuisine*.  
我中學時地理課學得不錯，知道渝是重慶的簡稱，所以是重慶菜。  
I'd done well in geography, so I knew *Yu* was short for Chongqing — Chongqing food.  
重慶在四川省境內，應該和川菜頗有淵源。  
Chongqing is in Sichuan Province, so this would be closely related to Sichuan cuisine.  
川菜……？  
Sichuan cuisine…?  
我開始冒冷汗。  
I started to break out in cold sweat.  
我不太能吃辣，以前在台灣第一次吃麻辣鍋後，拉了三天肚子。  
I can't really handle spicy food. My first hotpot in Taiwan once gave me three days of diarrhea.  
拉到第三天時，走出廁所，我終於領悟到什麼叫點點滴滴。  
By day three, walking out of the toilet, I'd finally come to understand what *drop by drop* really meant.  

「能吃辣嗎？」剛走進餐館，北京李老師便微笑詢問。  
"Can you handle spicy?" Teacher Li asked with a smile as we stepped inside.  
你看過撕了票、進了戲院的人，在電影還沒播放前就尖叫逃出來的人嗎？  
Have you ever seen someone tear up their ticket, walk into the theater, then run out screaming before the movie has even started?  
『還行。』我只好說。  
"It's fine," I had to say.  
「那你會吃得非常過癮。」李老師又說。  
"Then you're in for a treat," he added.  
我不禁流下男兒淚。  
I couldn't help shedding manly tears.  

果不其然，第一道菜就讓我聯想到以色列的紅海。  
Sure enough, the first dish made me think of the Red Sea of Israel.  
湯上頭滿滿浮了一層紅色的油，我不會天真到以為那是蕃茄汁。  
A thick layer of red oil floated on the soup, and I wasn't naive enough to think it was tomato juice.  
「嘿嘿。」暖暖笑了。  
"Heh heh," Nuannuan giggled.  
『笑什麼？』我問。  
"What's funny?" I asked.  
「據說挺能吃辣的人，看到辣臉會泛紅；不能吃辣的人嘛，臉會發青。」  
"They say people who handle heat well flush red when they see it; people who can't go pale."  
『妳想說什麼？』  
"What are you trying to say?"  
「沒事。」暖暖說，「我瞧你臉色挺紅潤的，由衷為你高興而已。」  
"Nothing," she said. "Your color looks rosy. I'm just happy for you."  
說完後，暖暖又嘿嘿兩聲。  
And she gave another two little laughs.  

「請容許小妹跟您解說這道菜。」暖暖笑了笑說：「將生魚肉片成薄片，  
"Permit this humble little sister to explain this dish to you," she said with a smile. "Slice raw fish thin,  
用滾燙辣油一勺一勺地澆熟，這道菜就成了。」  
then ladle boiling chili oil over it, spoon by spoon, until it's cooked. There's the dish."  
『……』  
"…"  
「一勺一勺的唷。」暖暖還加上手勢。  
"Spoon by spoon," she added, with hand motions.  
我試著拿起碗，但左手有些抖，碗像地震時的搖晃。  
I tried to lift my bowl, but my left hand trembled, and the bowl shook like a small earthquake.  
「請容許小妹替您服務。」暖暖舀起幾片魚肉放進我的碗，再淋上湯汁，  
"Permit this humble little sister to serve you." She ladled several slices of fish into my bowl and poured the broth over them.  
「嚐嚐。」  
"Try it."  

我夾起一片魚肉，在暖暖充滿笑意的眼神中吃下肚。  
I picked up a slice of fish and, under her laughing gaze, swallowed it.  
辣到頭皮發麻，感覺突然變成岳飛，已經怒髮衝冠了。  
The heat made my scalp prickle; I felt I'd suddenly become Yue Fei — hair raised in fury under my helmet.  
「感想呢？」暖暖問。  
"Impressions?" Nuannuan asked.  
『這……在……辣……』我舌頭腫脹，開始口齒不清。  
"This… is… spicy…" My tongue had swollen, and my speech was slurring.  
「請容許小妹幫您下個結論。」暖暖說，「魚肉辣、湯汁更辣，總之就是  
"Permit this humble little sister to draw a conclusion for you," she said. "The fish is spicy, the broth even spicier — in a word,  
一個辣字。」  
*spicy*."  
『這實在太辣了。』我終於說：『我不太能吃辣。』  
"This is really too much." I finally got it out. "I can't really handle spicy."  
「您行的，別太謙卑。多吃這渺小的辣，您就會謙回來，不會太卑了。」  
"You can do it; don't be too humble. Eat more of these *small* spicy things, and you'll come back to humility — without being too lowly."  

第二道菜又是一大盤火紅，看起來像是盤子著了火。  
The second dish was another mountain of fiery red, like the plate itself was on fire.  
紅辣椒佔多數，雞丁只佔少數，正懷疑是否現在辣椒便宜雞肉昂貴時，  
Most of it was red chilies; only a small portion was diced chicken. Just as I was wondering whether chilies had gotten cheap and chicken expensive,  
暖暖已經盛了小半碗放我面前。只有兩小塊雞丁，其餘全部是辣椒。  
Nuannuan had already scooped half a bowl in front of me. Two pieces of chicken, the rest all chili.  
「這是辣子雞，聽說辣椒才是主角，雞丁只是配菜。」暖暖笑著說。  
"This is *lazi ji* — chili chicken. The chilies are the stars, the chicken's just a side," she said with a laugh.  
我不敢只吃辣椒，便同時夾塊雞丁和辣椒，辣椒上面還有一些小點。  
Not daring to eat only chili, I grabbed a piece of chicken with a chili — and noticed little dots on the chili.  
才咬一口，我已經忘了椅子的存在，因為屁股都發麻了。  
One bite and I forgot the chair was there — my whole backside had gone numb.  
「別小看這小點，那是花椒。」暖暖用筷子挑起紅辣椒上的小點，  
"Don't underestimate those dots — that's Sichuan peppercorn." She picked one up with her chopsticks.  
「會讓你麻到群魔亂舞。」  
"It'll have you tingling like a horde of demons dancing."  

這道菜既麻又辣，實在太黯然、太銷魂了。  
Numb and spicy at once — too sorrowful, too soul-shattering.  
「涼涼，你哭了？」暖暖說。  
"Liangliang, are you crying?" Nuannuan said.  
『民族依舊多難。』我擦了擦眼角，『實在令人感傷。』  
"Our nation still suffers so." I wiped my eyes. "It's truly heartbreaking."  
「那再多吃點，養好精神才能報效祖國。」  
"Then eat more — build up your strength so you can serve the motherland."  
『我不行了。』  
"I can't take any more."  
「您行的。」  
"Yes you can."  
『暖暖，我錯了。饒了我吧。』  
"Nuannuan, I was wrong. Please, have mercy."  
暖暖嘩啦嘩啦笑著，非常開心的樣子。  
Nuannuan laughed brightly, clearly delighted.  

肚子實在餓得慌，我又勉強動了筷子。  
But my stomach was howling with hunger, so I forced my chopsticks back into action.  
『吃麻會叫媽，吃辣就會拉。』我說。  
"Eat *ma* and you cry *ma*; eat *la* and you *la*," I said — eat numbing pepper and you scream for mama, eat chili and you get the runs.  
「你說啥？」暖暖問。  
"What did you say?" Nuannuan asked.  
我想我已經辣到臨表涕泣，不知所云了。  
I think I'd been spiced into babbling — tears at the desk, no idea what I was saying.  

『沒想到川菜這麼麻辣。』我要了杯水，喝了一口後說。  
"I didn't realize Sichuan food was so numbing-hot." I asked for water and took a sip.  
「這是渝菜。你若說渝菜是川菜，重慶人肯定跟你沒完。」  
"This is *Yu* food. Call it Sichuan food and people from Chongqing won't let you off easy."  
『原來渝菜不是川菜。』  
"So Yu food isn't Sichuan food."  
「你若說渝菜不是川菜，那成都人肯定有兒大不由娘的委屈。」  
"And if you tell Chengdu people that Yu food isn't Sichuan food, they'll feel like the parent whose grown child has slipped their grasp."  
『喂。我只是個不能吃辣又非得填飽肚子的可憐蟲，別為難我了。』  
"Hey, I'm just a poor wretch who can't eat spicy but has to fill his stomach. Don't make this harder."  
「其實是因為渝菜想自立門戶成為中國第九大菜系，但川菜可不樂見。」  
"It's that Yu cuisine wants to break off and become the ninth great Chinese cuisine, and Sichuan cuisine isn't pleased about it."  
『渝菜和川菜有何區別？』  
"What's the difference between Yu and Sichuan, then?"  

「簡單說，川菜是溫柔婉約的辣，渝菜則辣得粗獷豪放。」暖暖笑了笑，  
"Put simply, Sichuan is a soft, lyrical heat; Yu is bold and unrestrained heat." She smiled.  
「我待會挑些不太辣的讓你吃。」  
"I'll pick out the less spicy bits for you in a moment."  
『感激不盡。』我急忙道謝。  
"I'm forever in your debt," I said quickly.  
「我只能盡量了。畢竟這就像是雞蛋裡挑骨頭。」  
"I can only do my best. It's like picking bones out of an egg."  
我嘆了口氣，看來今晚得餓肚子了。  
I sighed; it looked like I'd be going to bed hungry.  

『為什麼今晚要吃這麼麻辣的渝菜呢？』  
"Why are we eating such fiery Yu food tonight?"  
「我估計老師們可能要給你們這些台灣學生來個下馬威。」  
"I'd guess the teachers want to give you Taiwanese students a welcome slap-down."  
『下馬威應該是昨天剛下飛機時做的事才對啊。』  
"That should have been done yesterday, when we first got off the plane."  
「如果昨晚下馬威，萬一下過頭，你們立馬就回台灣可不成。」暖暖說，  
"If they'd done it last night and overdone it, you'd have flown straight back to Taiwan, which wouldn't do." She said,  
「今天下剛好，上了戲台、化了花臉，就由不得你不唱戲。」  
"Today is just right. You're already on stage, your face painted; you don't get to refuse to sing."  
『太狠了吧。』  
"That's vicious."  
「我說笑呢，你別當真。」暖暖笑著說。  
"I'm joking, don't take it seriously," she said with a smile.  

暖暖似乎變成了試毒官，先吃吃看辣不辣，再決定要不要夾給我。  
Nuannuan turned into my food taster — testing each dish first, deciding whether to pass it on.  
夾給我時，也順便會把辣椒、花椒類的東西挑掉。  
When she did, she'd pluck out the chilies and peppercorns first.  
只可惜渝菜是如此粗獷豪放，拿掉辣椒也不會變成文質彬彬。  
Sadly, Yu cuisine was so bold and unrestrained that removing the chilies wouldn't turn it gentlemanly.  
結果這頓飯我只吃了幾口菜，連湯都不敢喝。  
In the end I ate a few bites and didn't even dare touch the soup.  
但同行的台灣學生大多吃得過癮，只有兩三個被辣暈了。  
Most of the Taiwanese students, though, loved it; only two or three were knocked out by the heat.  

回到寢室後，覺得空腹難受，便溜到街上找了家麵館，叫了碗麵。  
Back in the dorm, I felt awful from the empty stomach, so I slipped out to find a noodle shop and ordered a bowl.  
麵端來了，好大一碗。看看桌上，只有筷子。  
The noodles arrived, an enormous bowl. On the table were only chopsticks.  
我起身向前，走到櫃台邊，問：『有沒有湯匙？』  
I got up and went to the counter. "Got a *tangchi*?"  
「啥？」煮麵的大嬸似乎聽不懂。  
"What?" The cook lady didn't seem to understand.  
我想她大概聽不懂台灣腔，試著捲起舌頭，再說一次：『湯匙？』  
Figuring she couldn't make sense of my Taiwanese accent, I curled my tongue and tried again. "*Tangchi*?"  
「啥？」大嬸還是不懂。  
"What?" Still no recognition.  
我只好用手語比出舀湯然後送入口中的動作。  
So I mimed scooping soup and bringing it to my mouth.  

「勺是唄？」大嬸拿根勺給我，嘴裡還大聲說：  
"You mean a *shao*?" she said, handing me one and announcing loudly,  
「勺就勺唄，說啥湯匙？湯裡有屎嗎？」  
"A *shao* is a *shao* — what's *tangchi*? Is there *shi* — crap — in your soup?"  
店內的客人哇哈哈大笑，大嬸也跟著笑，好像在比誰大聲。  
The customers howled with laughter, the cook joined in, like a contest of who could be loudest.  
大嬸，我台灣來的不懂事，您應該小點聲，這樣我很尷尬耶。  
Auntie, I'm an ignorant boy from Taiwan; you should keep it down, this is very embarrassing.  

我匆匆吃了大半碗麵便趕緊走人。  
I gulped down most of the bowl and beat a hasty retreat.  
回寢室途中，剛好碰見學弟走出廁所，「拉肚子了。」他說。  
On the way back, I ran into my junior coming out of the bathroom. "I've got the runs," he said.  
『還好嗎？』我問。  
"You okay?" I asked.  
「不好。」他搖搖頭，「我的菊花已經變成向日葵了。」  
"Not okay." He shook his head. "My chrysanthemum has become a sunflower."  
『混蛋！』我趕緊摀住他的嘴，『不要在這裡說白爛話。』  
"You idiot!" I clapped a hand over his mouth. "Don't say crap like that out here."  

我和學弟走回寢室，剛好碰見高亮。  
We walked back to the room and bumped into Gao Liang.  
「老蔡，大夥要逛小吃一條街。一道去吧。」他說。  
"Old Cai, the gang's heading to Snack Street. Come along," he said.  
原來北京學生擔心台灣學生吃不慣麻辣，便提議去小吃一條街打打牙祭。  
The Beijing students, worried the Taiwanese hadn't taken to the heat, had proposed going for some real snacks.  
老師們並不阻止，只叮嚀出門要留神、回來別晚了、  
The teachers didn't stop us, only reminding us to be careful and not to be back too late,  
別裝迷糊把酒吧一條街當成小吃一條街。  
and not to play dumb and end up on Bar Street instead of Snack Street.  

小吃一條街跟台灣的夜市很像，只不過台灣的夜市還賣些衣服、鞋子、  
Snack Street felt like a Taiwanese night market — except night markets back home also sell clothes, shoes,  
CD之類的東西，偶爾還有算命攤、按摩店；但小吃一條街全都是吃的。  
CDs, the occasional fortune-teller's stall or massage shop. Snack Street is purely about food.  
剛吃了大半碗麵，肚子並不餓，因此我光用聞的，反正聞的不用錢。  
I'd just had most of a bowl of noodles, so I wasn't hungry; I'd just smell, since smelling was free.  
逛了些時候，食物的香味誘出了食慾，開始想嚐些新玩意。  
After a while, the aromas had teased my appetite back, and I wanted to try something new.  
「涼涼。」我轉頭看見暖暖，她遞給我兩根羊肉串，說：「喏，給你。」  
"Liangliang." I turned and saw Nuannuan, who handed me two lamb skewers. "Here, take these."  
『不辣吧？』我問。  
"Not spicy, right?" I asked.  
「你說呢？」  
"You tell me."  

我有些害怕，用鼻子嗅了嗅，再伸出舌頭輕輕舔了舔。  
Cautious, I sniffed at the meat and then gave it a tentative little lick.  
「唉呀，別丟人了。」暖暖笑著說：「像條狗似的。」  
"Oh stop, you're embarrassing me," she laughed. "You look like a dog."  
『好像不太辣耶。』我說。  
"Doesn't seem too spicy," I said.  
「我特地叫他們別放太辣。」暖暖說。  
"I told them to go easy on the chili for you."  
『謝謝。』  
"Thank you."  
暖暖微微一笑，「你晚上吃得少，待會多吃點。」  
She smiled. "You barely ate at dinner. Eat more now."  

我跟暖暖說了偷溜出去吃碗麵的事，順便說要湯匙結果鬧笑話的過程。  
I told her about sneaking out for noodles, and about the *tangchi* fiasco.  
暖暖笑得合不攏嘴，好不容易把嘴巴合攏後，說：  
She laughed so hard she could barely close her mouth. When she did, she said,  
「既然吃過了，咱們就吃點小吃。」  
"Since you've already had noodles, let's just have snacks."  
說完便帶我去吃驢打滾、愛窩窩、豌豆黃之類的北京風味小吃。  
She led me off to try *ludagunr*, *aiwowo*, *wandouhuang* — classic Beijing-style snacks.  
依台灣的說法，這些都可歸類為甜點。  
By Taiwanese reckoning, all of them would count as desserts.  
我們盡可能吃少量多種，如果吃不完便會遞給身旁的同學，然後說：  
We tried to eat a little of many things, and when we couldn't finish, we'd hand the leftovers to nearby classmates with,  
『給你一個，算是結緣。』  
"Here, have one — call it a bond between us."  

逛了一個多小時，大夥便回學校。  
After an hour or so, the group headed back to campus.  
我吃得好撐，便躺著休息；學弟、徐馳和高亮在看今天的相片檔。  
Stuffed, I lay down to rest; my junior, Xu Chi, and Gao Liang were going through today's photos.  
「老蔡，你的芭樂。」徐馳說。  
"Old Cai, your *bala* shot," Xu Chi said.  
我從床上一躍而下（我還在上鋪喔），擠進他們，說：『在哪？』  
I leapt down from the bed (I was on the top bunk, mind you), squeezed in among them. "Where?"  
徐馳將數位相機的顯示畫面湊到我眼前，我可以清楚看見暖暖的笑容。  
Xu Chi held the camera screen up to me; I could see Nuannuan's smile clearly.  
我凝視暖暖幾秒後，徐馳按了下一張，我立刻按上一張，再凝視幾秒。  
I gazed at her for several seconds; Xu Chi pressed *next*; I pressed *previous* and stared a few seconds more.  
「老蔡，你回台灣後，我會把這些相片給你發過去。」徐馳說。  
"Old Cai, when you're back in Taiwan, I'll send these photos over."  
『馳哥。』我很高興，一把抱住他，『我可以叫你馳哥嗎？』  
"Brother Chi," I said, delighted, throwing my arms around him. "Can I call you Brother Chi?"  

這晚我們四人的精神都很好，砍大山砍到很晚。  
That night the four of us were wide awake, *kan-da-shanning* — chopping down the big mountain — well into the small hours.  
學弟偶爾砍到一半便跑出去上廁所，高亮問：「沒事吧？」  
Now and then my junior would interrupt his chopping for a toilet break. Gao Liang asked, "You all right?"  
「我的屁股變成梵谷的模特兒了。」學弟說。  
"My backside has become a model for Van Gogh," he said.  
徐馳和高亮弄了半天才搞清楚梵谷就是梵高，只是翻譯名稱的差別而已。  
It took a while for Xu Chi and Gao Liang to grasp that *Fan Gu* and *Fan Gao* were the same painter, just different translations.  
我思考了很久才想起梵谷最愛畫的花是向日葵。  
And I had to think a long time before I remembered that Van Gogh's favorite flower to paint was the sunflower.  

翻下床想掐住學弟的脖子讓他為亂說話付出代價，  
I jumped down to throttle my junior and make him pay for his crude tongue,  
但他嘴巴張開，臉呈痴呆，似乎已進入夢鄉。  
but his mouth was open, his face slack — he was already off in dreamland.  
只得再翻上床，閉上眼睛，讓暖暖的笑容伴我入眠。  
I climbed back up, closed my eyes, and let Nuannuan's smile carry me to sleep.
