# 《暖暖》第九章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

一覺醒來，已快中午。  
I woke up to find it was almost noon.  
打開電腦，收到暖暖的回信。  
I opened my computer and found a reply from Nuannuan.  
信上寫：  
It read:  
涼涼。  
Liangliang.  
你還活著就好。我很好，也活著。  
Glad you're still alive. I'm fine — also alive.  
快去吃飯吧。  
Go eat.  
暖暖在北京。  
Nuannuan, in Beijing.  

我洗了把臉，下樓去覓食。  
I washed my face and went downstairs to find food.  
街景是熟悉的，人們講話的腔調也熟悉，我果然回到家了。  
The streets were familiar; the accents were familiar. I was home.  
在北京連續八天聽了太多捲舌音，老覺得聲音在空中不再是直線傳遞，  
After eight straight days of Beijing retroflex sounds, I felt like voices no longer traveled in straight lines  
而是化成一圈一圈像漩渦似的鑽進耳裡。  
but curled into spirals that drilled into my ears.  
我的耳朵快多長一個渦了。  
My ears were about to grow another whorl.  
下意識摸了摸耳朵，說：之前讓您受累了。  
I instinctively touched my ear and said: *You've worked hard.*  

吃飽飯後，又看了一次徐馳寄來的相片檔。  
After eating, I looked through Xu Chi's photos again.  
視線依然在暖暖的影像前駐足良久。  
My eyes still lingered long on Nuannuan's image.  
看完後眼睛有些酸，擦了擦不知是因為眼酸或是難過而有些濕潤的眼角。  
My eyes stung. I wiped the corner of my eye — not sure if from strain or sadness.  
關上電腦，躺在床上。  
I shut down the computer and lay down.  

再度睜開眼睛時，天已經黑了。  
When I opened my eyes again, it was dark.  
不管是白天或黑夜，我重複覓食、開電腦、看相片、發呆、躺下的過程。  
Day or night, I'd repeat the cycle: find food, open the computer, look at photos, stare into space, lie down.  
感覺三魂七魄中少了一魂兩魄，人變得有些恍惚。  
I felt like I was missing one soul and two spirits — dazed.  
就這麼度過第一個完全看不到暖暖的日子。  
That's how I spent the first day without seeing Nuannuan at all.  

之後連續兩天，我仍然無法脫離北京狀態，腦子裡有些錯亂。  
For the next two days I still couldn't shake the Beijing state of mind.  
覺得實在無法靜下心時，便寫E-mail給暖暖。  
Whenever I couldn't settle down, I'd write her an email.  
兩天內寫了七封E-mail，暖暖也回了我七封。  
I wrote seven emails in two days; she replied to all seven.  
信的內容都是具體的事物，而不是抽象的感覺。  
Our letters were about concrete things, not abstract feelings.  
我不會寫：台灣的風，在沒有妳的黑夜裡，依然無情地颳著。  
I didn't write: *Taiwan's wind, in the nights without you, still blows mercilessly.*  
暖暖也不會寫：失去你的身影，北京的太陽也無法照亮我的心房。  
She didn't write: *Without your figure, even Beijing's sun can't light up my heart.*  
我們都只是告訴對方：正努力活著，做該做的事。  
We just told each other: *Still living, doing what needs to be done.*  

偶爾也起了打手機給暖暖的念頭。  
Occasionally I thought about calling her.  
現在手機普遍，可隨時隨地找到人；  
Phones are everywhere now — you can reach anyone anytime.  
但也因隨時隨地，對方人在哪裡、做什麼事，你完全沒概念。  
But precisely because it's anytime, anywhere, you have no idea where they are or what they're doing.  
比方說，我在北京第三天時，接到一通大學同學打來的電話。  
Like the third day in Beijing when I got a call from a college classmate.  
「現在有空嗎？」他說。  
"Free right now?" he said.  
『有啊。』我說。  
"Yeah."  
「出來看場電影吧。」  
"Let's go see a movie."  
『可是我人在北京耶。』  
"But I'm in Beijing."  
「…………」  
"…"  

所以我總是克制住想打手機給暖暖的欲望。  
So I always held back the urge to call her.  
一方面是因為電話費可能會很貴；  
Partly because the phone bill would be expensive.  
另一方面是覺得沒什麼特別奇怪的事值得打電話。  
Partly because nothing strange enough to call about ever happened.  
如果我在路上撿到很多錢或是突然中了樂透，那麼兩方面都可滿足；  
If I found a pile of money on the street or won the lottery, that would satisfy both conditions:  
既有錢且這種事非常罕見。  
I'd have money, and it would be unusual enough.  
但我一直沒撿到錢，樂透也沒買。  
But I never found any money, and I didn't buy lottery tickets.  

第四天醒來時就好多了，起碼想起自己還得找工作、寄履歷。  
By the fourth day I was better — at least I remembered I still needed to find a job and send out resumes.  
打開電腦後，收到一封陌生的E-mail，岳峰姑娘寄來的。  
I opened my computer and found an email from a stranger — Yue Feng.  
我跟岳峰的互動不多，算不上很熟，臨走前她也沒跟我要E-mail。  
I hadn't interacted much with Yue Feng; we weren't close. She hadn't asked for my email before leaving.  
為什麼寫信給我呢？  
Why was she writing me?  
看了看信件標題：想麻煩你一件事。麻煩我什麼事？做她的男朋友嗎？  
Subject line: *I need to ask you a favor.* What favor? Be her boyfriend?  
只怪我再怎麼樣也稱得上是風度翩翩，岳峰會陷進去算是情有可原。  
Then again, I was pretty charming — it would be understandable if she'd fallen for me.  
唉，我真是造孽啊。  
Sigh. What a curse I am.  

打開了信，信裡頭寫：  
I opened the letter. It read:  
從暖暖那兒知道你的E-mail，請告訴我，你學弟的E-mail，王克要的。  
*Got your email from Nuannuan. Please give me your junior's email — Wang Ke wants it.*  
岳峰。  
*Yue Feng.*  
ps. 順道問你一聲好。  
*PS. A quick hello.*  

有沒有搞錯？  
You've got to be kidding me.  
寄信給我竟然只在ps裡問好，而且還是順道。  
She emails me and the greeting is just a PS — and a casual one at that.  
我連回都不想回，直接把這封信轉寄給學弟。  
I didn't even bother replying; I just forwarded it to my junior.  
然後我收拾起被岳峰姑娘戲弄的心，開始整理履歷表。  
Then I recovered from Yue Feng's prank and started working on my resume.  

除了早已準備好的學經歷及專長的表格外，我又寫了簡單的自傳。  
Besides the standard education-and-experience form, I wrote a brief autobiography.  
自傳用手寫，寫在從北大買回來的信紙上。  
Handwritten, on the letter paper I'd bought from Peking University.  
在這電腦發達的時代，算得上是特別吧。或許可因此多吸引些目光。  
In this age of computers, it was distinctive enough to catch some attention.  
我一共找了五家公司，自傳寫了五份。  
I picked five companies and wrote five copies.  
寫完後，連同表格，分別裝進五個北大信封裡，然後下樓寄信。  
I put each in a PKU envelope and went downstairs to mail them.  

三天後，我接到通知我面試的電話。  
Three days later, I got a call for an interview.  
隔天我便盛裝坐火車北上去面試。  
The next day, I dressed up and took the train north.  
果然一見面他就問我：「為什麼用北大的信封信紙？」  
Sure enough, the interviewer asked, "Why are you using PKU envelopes and paper?"  
『我是北大校友。』我說，『北大這所學校的朋友，我在那待過半天。』  
"I'm a PKU alumnus," I said. "I'm friends with the school. I spent half a day there."  
他楞了一下，然後說：  
He paused, then said:  
「我念碩士班時做過一個研究：喜歡講老梗冷笑話的人，上班特別認真。  
"When I was in grad school, I did a study: people who love stale, corny jokes are especially hardworking at their jobs.  
因為這種人沒有異性緣、人際關係也不好，工作便成了唯一的寄託。」  
Because they have no luck with the opposite sex and poor social skills — work becomes their only refuge."  

我不知道這代表好或是不好？心裡頗為忐忑。  
I didn't know if that was good or bad. I felt uncertain.  
「你什麼時候可以來上班？」過了一會，他說。  
"When can you start?" he said after a moment.  
『越快越好。』我說。  
"Sooner the better," I said.  
「那就下星期一開始。」  
"Monday."  
『沒問題。』  
"No problem."  

我找到工作了，沒什麼特別興奮的反應，好像只是完成一件該做的事。  
I'd found a job. No particular excitement — just completing a task.  
後來又陸續接到兩通電話，我都以找到工作為由回絕了。  
Two more calls came later; I turned them down.  
反正對我這種專業的社會新鮮人而言，工作性質都是類似的。  
For a fresh graduate like me, the jobs were all similar anyway.  
我找好了新房子，準備北上就業。  
I found a new apartment and prepared to head north for work.  

收拾好一切，該打包的打包、該裝箱的裝箱、該留下的留下。  
I packed everything up — boxes for what to take, leave what to leave.  
暖暖送的筆筒安穩地躺在隨身的背袋裡。  
Nuannuan's pen holder lay safely in my backpack.  
昨天已約好了搬家公司，他們一個小時後會到。  
The movers were coming in an hour.  
電腦最後才裝箱，因為我打算再寫一封E-mail給暖暖。  
The computer was the last thing to pack — I wanted to send one more email to Nuannuan.  

我信上寫：  
I wrote:  
暖暖。  
Nuannuan.  
我找到工作了。  
I found a job.  
我得搬家，搬到新竹。（台灣只有新竹，沒有舊竹）  
I have to move — to Hsinchu. (Taiwan has New Bamboo, not Old Bamboo.)  
安頓好了，會把新的地址告訴妳。  
Once I'm settled, I'll send you my new address.  
涼涼在台灣。  
Liangliang, in Taiwan.
