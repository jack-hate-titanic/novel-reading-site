# 《暖暖》第十二章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

蘇州到北京約1379公里，晚上8點有班直達特快的火車，
Suzhou to Beijing is about 1,379 kilometers. There was a direct express train at 8 PM,
隔天早上7點20分到北京，要坐11個小時又20分鐘。
arriving at 7:20 AM — 11 hours and 20 minutes.
太久了。
Too long.
我決定先跟同事搭廠裡的車從蘇州到上海，再從上海飛北京。
I decided to take the factory car to Shanghai with my colleagues, then fly from Shanghai to Beijing.
機票貴了點，但時間快多了。
The ticket was more expensive, but much faster.
反正錢再賺就有，時間可是一去不回頭。
Money can always be earned again — time never comes back.

我退了上海飛香港再飛台灣的機票，改訂上海飛北京的機票。
I canceled my Shanghai-Hong Kong-Taiwan ticket and booked Shanghai-Beijing instead.
北京的飯店也訂好了，有個蘇州同事對北京很熟，我請他幫我訂個房間。
A Suzhou colleague who knew Beijing well helped me book a hotel.
同行的台灣工程師很訝異我不跟他們一道回台灣，紛紛問我發生什麼事？
The other Taiwan engineers were surprised I wasn't returning with them and asked what was going on.
我把自己想像成面對大海的夕陽武士，深沉地說：『為愛走天涯。』
I imagined myself as a sunset warrior facing the sea and said solemnly: "Traveling to the ends of the earth for love."
就差眼前沒大海了。
All that was missing was the ocean.

我拜託他們回台灣後先幫我請幾天假，然後他們飛台灣、我飛北京。
I asked them to cover a few days' leave for me after they got back; they flew to Taiwan, I flew to Beijing.
我打了通電話給徐馳，他一聽我要到北京，便說要來機場接我。
I called Xu Chi. The moment he heard I was coming to Beijing, he said he'd pick me up at the airport.
『這樣多不好意思。』我說。
"That's so embarrassing," I said.
「少來。」徐馳說，「你打電話給我，不就是希望我去機場接你嗎？」
"Come on," he said. "You called me hoping I'd pick you up, didn't you?"
『嘿嘿。』我笑了笑。
"Heh heh," I laughed.

然後我再打電話給暖暖。
Then I called Nuannuan.
『暖暖。』我說，『我離開蘇州了，現在人在上海機場。』
"Nuannuan," I said. "I've left Suzhou. I'm at the Shanghai airport now."
「是嗎？」暖暖說，「那祝你一路順風。」
"Really?" she said. "Well — safe travels."
『暖暖。』我試著讓自己的心跳和語調平穩，『這幾天忙嗎？』
"Nuannuan." I tried to keep my heartbeat and voice steady. "Busy these days?"
「挺忙的。」暖暖說。
"Pretty busy."
『喔。那妳大概每天都抽不出一點時間吧。』
"Oh. So you probably can't spare even a little time each day."
「是呀。我恨不得多生雙手呢。」
"Yeah. I wish I could grow extra hands."
『萬一這時候剛好有個老朋友想見妳一面，妳一定很為難。』
"What if an old friend wanted to see you right now? That'd be awkward."
「這沒法子。只好跟他說：不巧，正忙呢。」
"Nothing I can do about it. I'd just have to say: *Bad timing — I'm busy.*"

我的心瞬間墜落谷底，心摔得好痛，我說不出話來。
My heart fell to the bottom of a valley, shattering. I couldn't speak.
「快告訴我坐幾點的飛機唄。」暖暖說。
"Hurry up and tell me your flight time," she said.
『那已經沒意義了。』我說。
"It doesn't matter anymore," I said.
「說啥呀，你不說我咋去接你？」
"What are you talking about — if you don't tell me, how can I pick you up?"
『啊？』我楞了楞，『這……』
"Ah?" I froze. "This—"
「瞧你傻的，我當然去機場接你。」
"Look at you, being silly. Of course I'm picking you up."
『妳知道我要到北京？』
"You knew I was coming to Beijing?"
「就你那點心眼，還想矇我？」暖暖笑了。
"With your simple mind, you think you can fool me?" She laughed.

「剛剛是逗你玩的。」暖暖的笑聲還沒停止。
"I was just teasing you." She was still laughing.
『妳這人賊壞。』
"You're really *zei* mean."
「你才壞呢。要來北京也不早說。」
"You're the mean one — not telling me earlier you were coming."
心臟又重新跳動，我下意識拍了拍胸口。
My heart started beating again. I patted my chest instinctively.
我告訴暖暖坐幾點的飛機、幾點到北京，暖暖邊聽邊笑，很開心的樣子。
I told her my flight time. She listened and laughed, clearly delighted.
我也很開心，一下飛機就可以看見暖暖，比預期的幸福多了。
I was happy too — seeing her right after landing was more happiness than I'd expected.

『暖暖。』我說，『我要去北京找妳了。』
"Nuannuan," I said. "I'm coming to find you in Beijing."
「嗯。我等你。」暖暖說。
"Mm. I'll be waiting," she said.

拿著登機證，背上背袋，我要直奔暖暖身旁。
Boarding pass in hand, backpack on, I was heading straight to her.
排隊等候登機時，突然想起得跟徐馳說不用來接我了，匆忙拿出手機。
While waiting to board, I remembered I should tell Xu Chi not to pick me up, and hurriedly pulled out my phone.
我告訴徐馳，暖暖要來接我，不麻煩他了。
I told him Nuannuan was picking me up.
「我了解。」徐馳笑得很曖昧，「嘿嘿。」
"I understand." He gave an ambiguous laugh. "Heh heh."
『我要登機了。』我說。
"I'm boarding," I said.
「甭管多晚，記得給我打電話。」徐馳說。
"No matter how late, remember to call me," he said.
關掉手機，我登上飛機。
I turned off my phone and boarded.

想閉上眼休息，但情緒亢奮很難平靜。
I tried to close my eyes and rest, but I was too excited.
時間緩緩流逝，飛機持續向北，離台灣越來越遠，但離暖暖越來越近。
Time passed slowly as the plane flew north — farther from Taiwan, but closer to Nuannuan.
我的心跳與飛機距北京的距離成反比。
My heartbeat was inversely proportional to the plane's distance from Beijing.
傳來低沉的轟隆一聲，飛機降落了，緩緩在跑道滑行，心跳達到極限。
With a low rumble, the plane landed and taxied down the runway. My heart was pounding.
夕陽武士拿起劍，不，拿起背袋，呼出一口長長的氣，緩和心跳速率。
The sunset warrior picked up his sword — no, his backpack — and exhaled deeply, trying to calm his heartbeat.

拖著行李箱緩緩前進，右手不自覺顫抖，行李箱有些左右搖晃。
I dragged my suitcase forward slowly. My right hand trembled, making the suitcase wobble.
暖暖不知道變成什麼樣？還是擁有跟以前一樣的笑容嗎？
What did she look like now? Did she still have the same smile?
很想激動的四處張望尋找暖暖，但那不是夕陽武士的風格。
I wanted to frantically scan the crowd for her, but that wasn't a sunset warrior's style.
我只能假裝鎮定，利用眼角餘光掃射所有等候接機的人群的面孔。
I had to pretend to be calm, using my peripheral vision to scan the waiting faces.
然後我看到了暖暖。
And then I saw her.

感覺血液已沸騰，心臟也快從嘴裡跳出來了。
My blood felt like it was boiling; my heart was about to jump out of my mouth.
只剩幾步路而已，我得沉著、我得冷靜、我得堅強。
Just a few more steps. I had to stay composed, stay calm, stay strong.
我不能拋下行李箱，一面呼喊暖暖的名字一面張開雙臂向她飛奔，
I couldn't drop my suitcase and run toward her with open arms, calling her name —
因為我是夕陽武士。
because I was a sunset warrior.

暖暖臉上掛著淺淺的笑，雙手拿了張白紙板舉在胸前晃啊晃的，
Nuannuan wore a faint smile, holding a white cardboard sign in front of her chest, waving it gently.
上頭寫了兩個斗大的黑字：涼涼。
On it were two big black characters: *Liangliang.*
暖暖的頭髮也許長了些，但她的笑容跟相片或我記憶中的影像，
Her hair might have been a bit longer, but her smile was exactly as I remembered —
幾乎一模一樣。
almost exactly the same.
我甚至懷疑即使她的眉毛多長一根，我也能分辨出來。
I doubted I'd notice if she grew one extra eyebrow hair.

我維持既定的步伐，沉穩地走到暖暖面前，停下腳步。
I maintained my pace and walked steadily to her, stopping in front.
暖暖停止晃動手上的紙板。
She stopped waving the sign.
「嘿，涼涼。」暖暖說。
"Hey, Liangliang," she said.
『嗨，暖暖。』我說。
"Hi, Nuannuan," I said.
「走唄。」暖暖說。
"Let's go," she said.

我和暖暖並肩走著，雙腿因興奮而有些僵硬。
We walked side by side. My legs were stiff from excitement.
『幹嘛拿這牌子？』我問。
"Why the sign?" I asked.
「怕你認不得我。」
"In case you didn't recognize me."
『妳化成灰我都認得。』
"I'd recognize you even if you turned to ashes."
「這句不是這樣用的。」暖暖笑了。
"That's not how that phrase is used," she laughed.
『在台灣就這麼用。』我說。
"That's how we use it in Taiwan," I said.

「你也沒變。你剛出來，我就認得了。」暖暖說。
"You haven't changed. The moment you came out, I recognized you."
『我還是一樣瀟灑嗎？』我說。
"Still as dashing as ever?" I said.
「涼涼。」暖暖撲哧一笑，「記下來，這是你到北京講的第一個笑話。」
"Liangliang." She burst out laughing. "Note this down — your first joke in Beijing."
『這牌子好酷。』我指了指暖暖手中的紙板。
"That sign is cool." I pointed at the cardboard.
「是呀。」暖暖笑了笑，「好多人瞧著我呢。」
"Yeah." She smiled. "A lot of people were looking at me."
『那是因為妳漂亮。』
"That's because you're beautiful."
「這是你到北京講的第一句實話。」暖暖又笑了，「記下來。」
"That's your first truthful statement in Beijing." She laughed again. "Note this down."

一跨出機場大門，冷風一吹，我冷不防打了個噴嚏。
The moment I stepped out of the airport, a cold wind hit me and I sneezed.
中文字真有意思，因為冷才會冷不防，所以不會叫熱不防。
Chinese characters are interesting — *lengbufang* means *caught off guard by the cold*, so there's no such thing as *rebufang*.
「你穿這樣有些單薄。」暖暖說。
"You're a bit lightly dressed," she said.
『我想蘇州不會太冷，而且秋末冬初就回台灣，便沒帶厚一點的外套。』
"I didn't think Suzhou would be that cold, and I was supposed to be back in Taiwan by late autumn, so I didn't bring a heavier coat."
「北京冷多了。現在才二度。」
"Beijing is much colder. It's only two degrees."
『是梅開二度的二度嗎？』
"Two degrees, as in the *er* in *mei kai er du* — plum blossoms blooming twice?"
「是。」
"Yes."
『真巧。』我說，『我這次到北京，也算梅開二度。』
"What a coincidence," I said. "This trip to Beijing is my second bloom too."
「涼涼。」
"Liangliang."
『我知道。這是我到北京講的第一句渾話，我會記下來。』
"I know. That's my first nonsense remark in Beijing. I'll note it down."

走進停車場，暖暖先往左走了十幾步，停下來，再回頭往右走。
We entered the parking lot. She walked left a dozen steps, stopped, then turned and walked right.
但走了幾步後，又停下來，然後四處張望。
A few more steps, another stop. She looked around.
『怎麼了？』我問。
"What's wrong?" I asked.
「我忘了車停哪了。」暖暖說。
"I forgot where I parked," she said.
『啊？』我很驚訝，『忘了？』
"Ah?" I was shocked. "Forgot?"
「也不能說全忘，」暖暖右手在空中畫了一圈，「大約在這區。」
"Can't say I completely forgot." She drew a circle in the air with her right hand. "About this area."
暖暖的心胸很大，她所謂的「這區」，起碼兩百輛車。
She was very generous. Her "area" covered at least 200 cars.

『是什麼車型？車號多少？』我說，『我幫妳找。』
"What model? What's the plate number?" I said. "I'll help you find it."
「就四個輪子那種。」暖暖說。
"The kind with four wheels."
『喂。』
"Hey."
「是單位的車，不是我的。」暖暖說，「車型不知道、車號我沒記。」
"It's a company car, not mine. Don't know the model, didn't note the plate."
『那妳知道什麼？』
"What *do* you know?"
「是白色的車。」
"It's a white car."
我看了看四周，白色車的比例雖然不高，但也有不少輛啊。
I looked around. The proportion of white cars wasn't high, but there were still plenty.

「唉呀，我才不是犯迷糊，只是出門晚了，路上又堵車，我急呀，我怕你
"Oh, I wasn't being ditzy. I left late, traffic was jammed, I was worried —
下了飛機見不著我，你會慌呀。我停好了車，立馬衝進機場，只想早點
I was afraid you'd panic if you didn't see me when you landed. I parked and rushed straight in, just wanting to
看到你，哪還有心思記著車放哪。」
see you as soon as possible — how could I remember where I parked?"
暖暖劈里啪啦說完，語氣有些急，音調有些高。
She spoke rapidly, her tone anxious, her pitch rising.

從下飛機見到暖暖開始，總覺得這一切像是夢境，不太真實。
Ever since I'd seen her at the airport, it had all felt like a dream — unreal.
直到此刻，我才感受到暖暖的真實存在。
Only now did I feel her real presence.
暖暖還是一樣沒方向感，還是一樣總讓人覺得心頭暖暖的。
She still had no sense of direction. Still made my heart feel warm.
從台灣到蘇州、蘇州到北京，穿越了三千公里，我終於又看到暖暖了。
From Taiwan to Suzhou, Suzhou to Beijing — three thousand kilometers — I'd finally seen her again.
這不是作夢。
This wasn't a dream.

『嘿嘿。』我笑了笑。
"Heh heh," I laughed.
「你笑啥？」暖暖似乎有些臉紅。
"What are you laughing at?" She seemed to blush.
『沒事。』我說，『我們一起找吧。如果找不到，就一輩子待在這。』
"Nothing," I said. "Let's look together. If we can't find it, we'll stay here forever."
「別瞎說。」
"Don't talk nonsense."
我和暖暖一輛一輛找，20分鐘後，暖暖才從車窗上的識別證認出車來。
We searched car by car. Twenty minutes later, she finally recognized it by the sticker on the window.
但這輛白色車的位置，並不在暖暖剛剛用手畫的「這區」。
But its location wasn't in the "area" she'd drawn.

「我上個月才剛拿到駕照，拿你來試試，行不？」一上車，暖暖便說。
"I just got my license last month — want me to try it out on you?" she said as I got in.
『這是我的榮幸。』我說。
"It's my honor," I said.
離開首都機場，車子開上機場高速，兩旁樺樹的樹葉幾乎都已掉光。
Leaving the airport, we drove on the expressway. The birch trees on both sides had lost nearly all their leaves.
但樹幹潔白挺立，枝條柔軟，迎風搖曳時姿態柔媚，像是含羞的美人。
But their white trunks stood tall, their soft branches swaying gracefully in the wind like shy beauties.

「你住哪個飯店？」暖暖問。
"Which hotel are you staying at?" she asked.
『我忘了。』我說。
"I forgot," I said.
「忘了？」暖暖很驚訝。
"Forgot?" She was surprised.
『唉呀，我才不是犯迷糊，只是突然決定不回台灣，急著要來北京找妳，
"Oh, I wasn't being ditzy. I just suddenly decided not to go back to Taiwan and was in a rush to find you in Beijing.
但下了飛機妳找不到車，我又擔心妳會慌啊，哪還有心思記著住哪。』
But after I landed and you couldn't find the car, I was worried you'd panic — how could I remember where I was staying?"

暖暖笑個不停，好不容易止住笑，說：「涼涼。」
She laughed without stopping. Finally she managed, "Liangliang."
『是。』
"Yes."
「你住哪個飯店？」
"Which hotel?"
『王府井的台灣飯店。』我說。
"The Taiwan Hotel on Wangfujing."
「那地方我知道。」
"I know that place."
『真的知道？』
"Really?"
「別小看我。」暖暖說。
"Don't underestimate me."
『找不到也沒關係，頂多我就睡車上。』
"If we can't find it, I'll just sleep in the car."
「不會走丟的。」暖暖笑了笑。
"We won't get lost," she smiled.

天漸漸黑了，天空開始下起雨，不算大也不算小。
It was getting dark. Rain started falling — not heavy, not light.
外頭應該很冷，但車內有暖氣而且還有暖暖，暖活得很。
It must have been cold outside, but the car had heating and there was Nuannuan — plenty warm.
我和暖暖在車上閒聊，扯東扯西、天南地北，東西南北都說了。
We chatted in the car, talking about everything under the sun.
天完全黑了，在燈光照射下，我清楚看見雨的線條。
It was completely dark now. Under the streetlights, I could clearly see the lines of rain.
可能是錯覺，我發覺雨在高空較細，接近地面時變粗，速度也變慢。
Maybe it was an illusion, but the rain seemed finer up high, thicker near the ground, and slower.

「二環路又堵車了。」暖暖說。
"The Second Ring Road is jammed again," she said.
『反正我們已經見面了。』我說，『堵到天荒地老也沒關係。』
"Anyway, we've already met," I said. "I don't care if we're stuck here until the end of time."
車子完全停下來了，暖暖轉頭朝著我苦笑。
The car came to a complete stop. She turned and gave me a wry smile.
「如果你想到車輪碾著的，是元大都的古城牆，會有啥感覺？」暖暖說。
"If you think about how the wheels are rolling over the ancient city walls of Yuan Dadu — how does that feel?" she said.
我一時說不上來，有句成語叫滄海桑田，好像勉強可以形容。
I couldn't quite say. The idiom *seas change to mulberry fields* seemed to fit.

車子終於下了二環路，很快便抵達台灣飯店。
We finally exited the Second Ring Road and soon reached the Taiwan Hotel.
雨停了，我看見車窗上被雨刷掃過的邊緣有些閃亮，好奇便靠近細看。
The rain had stopped. I noticed something sparkling along the edges the wipers had swept, and leaned closer.
那似乎是凝結的小冰珠，我用手指輕輕刮起一塊，確實是碎冰沒錯。
They looked like tiny frozen beads. I scraped one off with my finger — definitely ice.
難道剛剛天空中下的，不完全是雨？
Was it not entirely rain falling from the sky?

「待會興許會下雪。」暖暖說。
"It might snow later," she said.
『妳是說寒冷的冬天時，下的那種東西？』
"You mean that stuff that falls in cold winter?"
「是呀。」
"Yes."
『從天空飄落的，白白的那種東西？』
"That white stuff that drifts from the sky?"
「是呀。」
"Yes."
『可以堆雪人、丟雪球的那種東西？』
"The stuff you can build snowmen and throw snowballs with?"
「是呀。」
"Yes."
『那是雪耶！』我幾乎失聲大叫。
"That's snow!" I nearly shouted.
暖暖不想理我，手指比了比飯店門口。
She ignored me and pointed at the hotel entrance.

我拖著行李箱、背著背袋，在飯店櫃台辦完check in手續。
I dragged my suitcase and backpack in and checked in at the front desk.
暖暖想看看房間長啥樣，便陪著我坐上電梯。
She wanted to see what the room looked like, so she came up with me.
「這房間還可以。」暖暖進房後，四處看了看後，說。
"The room is okay," she said, looking around.
