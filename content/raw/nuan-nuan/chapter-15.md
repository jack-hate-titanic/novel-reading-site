# 《暖暖》第十五章中英对照

说明：
- 本文件改编自蔡智恒（痞子蔡）的小说《暖暖》。
- 排版规则为"一句中文，一句英文"。

晚上8點32分的火車從哈爾濱出發，隔天早上7點7分到北京，
The train left Harbin at 8:32 PM and arrived in Beijing at 7:07 AM —
還是要坐10小時35分鐘。
still 10 hours and 35 minutes.
跟北京到哈爾濱的情況幾乎一樣，就差那兩分鐘。
Almost the same as the trip from Beijing, just two minutes' difference.
為什麼不同樣是8點半開而是8點32分開，我實在百思不解。
Why 8:32 instead of 8:30 — I couldn't figure it out.
但幸好多這兩分，因為我和暖暖貪玩，到月台時已是8點半了。
But those extra two minutes were lucky — we'd been playing and only reached the platform at 8:30.

回程的車票早已買好，仍然是軟臥下鋪的位置。
Return tickets already bought — soft sleeper, lower berth, same as before.
這次同包廂的是兩個來哈爾濱玩的北京女孩，像剛從大學畢業沒多久。
This time our compartment-mates were two Beijing girls visiting Harbin, fresh out of college.
就是那種穿上高跟鞋還不太會走路的年紀，通常這種年紀的女孩最迷人。
That age where they're not yet steady in heels — usually the most charming.
她們很熱情，主動跟暖暖閒聊兩句，暖暖還告訴她們我是從台灣來的。
They were enthusiastic and chatted with Nuannuan; she told them I was from Taiwan.

兩個女孩，一高一瘦，竟然同時從上鋪迅速爬下，來到我面前。
The two girls — one tall, one slender — both scrambled down from the upper berth and came to me.
「我還沒親眼見過台灣人呢，得仔細瞧瞧。」高的女孩說。
"I've never seen a Taiwanese person in the flesh — let me get a good look," the tall one said.
「說句話來聽聽。」瘦的女孩說。
"Say something," the slender one said.
『妳好。』我說。
"Hello," I said.
「講長一點的句子唄。」高的女孩說。
"A longer sentence," the tall one said.
『冷，好冷，哈爾濱實在是冷。』我說。
"Cold. So cold. Harbin is really cold," I said.
她們兩人哇哇一陣亂笑，車頂快被掀開了。
They burst into laughter — nearly lifting the roof off.
『別笑了。』我說，『人家會以為我們這裡發生凶殺案。』
"Stop laughing," I said. "People will think there's a murder going on."
她們兩人笑聲更大了，異口同聲說：「台灣人講話挺有趣的。」
They laughed even harder and said in unison: "Taiwanese people talk so funny!"

這兩個女孩應該剛度過一個愉快的哈爾濱之旅，情緒依然亢奮。
They must have had a great time in Harbin — still riding that high.
嘰嘰喳喳說個沒完，還拿出撲克牌邀我和暖暖一起玩。
They chattered endlessly and invited us to play cards.
暖暖將大列巴切片，四個人分著吃，才吃了三分之一就飽了。
Nuannuan sliced the *da lieba*; the four of us shared it. We were full after a third.
大列巴吃起來有些硬，口味微酸，但香味濃郁。
The bread was a bit hard, slightly sour, but richly fragrant.

好不容易她們終於安靜下來，我走出包廂外透透氣。
When they finally quieted down, I stepped out of the compartment for some air.
火車持續發出規律而低沉的咚隆聲，駛向北京。
The train rumbled steadily toward Beijing.
天一亮就到北京了，而我再待在北京一天後，就得回台灣。
Dawn would bring us to Beijing — and after one more day, I'd have to return to Taiwan.
突然襲來的現實讓我心一沉，凋謝了心裡盛開的花。
The sudden reality hit me, wilting the flowers blooming in my heart.
耽誤了幾天的工作可以救得回來，但回去後得面對無窮無盡的思念。
Lost workdays could be made up — but how to face the endless longing?

「在想啥？」暖暖也走出包廂。
"What are you thinking about?" She stepped out too.
『沒事。』我說。
"Nothing," I said.
暖暖看了我一眼，問：「啥時候的飛機？」
She looked at me. "When's your flight?"
『後天早上十點多。』我也看了暖暖一眼。
"Day after tomorrow, around 10 AM." I looked back at her.
然後我們便沉默了。
Then we fell silent.

『暖暖。』我打破沉默，『我想問妳一個深奧的問題。』
"Nuannuan," I broke the silence. "I want to ask you a deep question."
「問唄。」暖暖說。
"Go ahead."
『妳日子過得好嗎？』
"Are you doing well?"
「這問題確實深奧。」暖暖笑了笑，「日子過得還行。你呢？」
"That's indeed a deep question." She smiled. "Life's okay. And you?"
『我的日子過得一成不變，有些老套。』我說。
"My life is monotonous — a bit clichéd," I said.
「大部分人的人生都是老套呀，又有多少人的人生是新鮮呢？」暖暖說。
"Most people's lives are clichés. How many people have truly fresh lives?" she said.
『有道理。』我笑了笑。
"Good point." I smiled.

暖暖突然從包裡拿出一張紙，說：「你瞧。」
She suddenly pulled a piece of paper from her bag. "Look."
我看了一眼，便知道這是去年在蘇州街算字時所寫的字。
One glance told me it was the handwriting sample from Suzhou Street last year.
『怎麼會在妳這兒？』我問。
"How do you have this?" I asked.
「那時老先生給我後，一直想拿給你，卻忘了。」暖暖又拿出白紙和筆，
"The old man gave it to me back then. I kept meaning to give it to you, but forgot." She took out fresh paper and a pen.
「你再寫一次。老先生說了，興許字會變。」
"Write it again. The old man said handwriting can change."
我在車廂間找了個平整的地方，再寫了一次台南城隍廟的對聯。
I found a flat spot between carriages and wrote the Tainan City God Temple couplet again.

「你的字有些不一樣了。」暖暖對比兩張紙上的字，說：
"Your handwriting has changed." She compared the two sheets.
「比方這個『我』字，鉤筆劃不再尖銳，反而像條弧線。」
"Take this *wo* — the hook isn't sharp anymore; it's more like a curve."
我也看了看，發覺確實是如此。這大概意味著我世故了或是圓滑了。
I looked too — she was right. It probably meant I'd become more worldly, more polished.
進入職場一年半，我已經懂得要稱讚主管領帶的樣式和顏色了。
After a year and a half in the workforce, I'd learned to compliment my boss's tie patterns and colors.

暖暖也再寫一次成都武侯祠的對聯，我發覺暖暖的字幾乎沒變。
She rewrote the Chengdu Wuhou Shrine couplet. Her handwriting had barely changed.
至於排列與橫豎，我和暖暖橫豎的排列沒變，字的排列也直。
As for the alignment and horizontal-vertical patterns — they were the same, and the character alignment was still straight.
我依然有內在的束縛，暖暖始終缺乏勇氣。
I still had my internal constraints; she still lacked courage.
我和暖暖像是萬福閣，先讓邁達拉巨佛立好，然後遷就巨佛而建成；
We were like the Pavilion of Ten Thousand Blessings — built around the giant Maitreya after it was already in place,
從沒絞盡腦汁想過該如何改變環境、把巨佛擺進萬福閣裡。
never racking our brains to change the environment and fit the Buddha in differently.

『面對未來，妳有什麼打算？』我問。
"What are your plans for the future?" I asked.
「就過日子唄，要打算啥？」
"Just live. What plans?"
『說得也是。』我說，『但有時想想，這樣好像太過平凡。』
"True," I said. "But sometimes I think — it seems too ordinary."
「就讓別人去追逐不平凡。」暖暖笑說，「當多數人是不平凡時，不平凡
"Let others chase extraordinariness," she smiled. "When most people are extraordinary,
就成了平凡，而平凡就成了不平凡。」
extraordinariness becomes ordinary, and ordinariness becomes extraordinary."
『妳看得很開。』我說。
"You take things lightly," I said.
「只能如此了。」暖暖說。
"What else can we do?" she said.
關於分隔兩岸的現實，我和暖暖似乎都想做些什麼，但卻不能改變什麼。
Regarding the reality of the strait between us — we both wanted to do something, but couldn't change anything.

『我們好像小欣跟阿麗這兩個女孩的故事。』我說。
"We're like the story of Xiao Xin and A'li," I said.
「小欣跟阿麗？」暖暖很疑惑。
"Xiao Xin and A'li?" She looked puzzled.
『嗯。』我說，『小欣買了一條魚，但阿麗不想煮。』
"Mm." I nodded. "Xiao Xin bought a fish, but A'li didn't want to cook it."
「然後呢？」
"And then?"
『沒有然後了。』
"There is no *and then*."
「呀？」
"Huh?"
『這就是欣有魚而麗不煮。』
"That's — *Xin has fish, but Li won't cook.*"

暖暖睜大眼睛，臉上表情像是猶豫該生氣還是該笑，最後決定笑了。
Her eyes went wide, her expression caught between anger and laughter. She chose laughter.
「涼涼。」暖暖說，「沒想到我竟然能容忍你這麼久。」
"Liangliang," she said. "I can't believe I've put up with you this long."
『辛苦妳了。』我說。
"You've worked hard," I said.
「如果將來某天，我們再見面時，你一定要告訴我，你曾在哈爾濱往北京
"If someday we meet again, you have to tell me that on the train from Harbin to Beijing,
的火車上，說了一個五顆星的冷笑話。」
you told a five-star cold joke."
『我會的。』我說，『而且還會再奉上另一個五顆星冷笑話。』
"I will," I said. "And I'll throw in another five-star cold joke."
「這是約定哦。」暖暖笑了笑。
"That's a promise." She smiled.
『嗯。』我點點頭。
"Mm." I nodded.
我和暖暖對未來沒有規劃、沒有打算，但卻抱著某種期望。
We had no plans for the future, no intentions — but we held on to some kind of hope.

我和暖暖走回包廂，燈光已暗，那兩個北京女孩應該睡著了。
We went back to the compartment. The lights were dim; the two girls were probably asleep.
暖暖輕輕說聲晚安，我們便各自躺回屬於自己的下鋪。
She softly said goodnight, and we each lay down on our own lower berths.
我閉上眼睛，開始倒帶來北京後這幾天的情景。
I closed my eyes and began replaying the past few days in Beijing.
相聚總是短暫，而離別太長，我得用心記下這些場景，
Time together was always short, and parting too long. I had to memorize these scenes,
因為將來要回味的時間多著呢。
because I'd have plenty of time to savor them later.

時間一點一滴流逝，耳畔火車前進的聲響始終不斷，這是失眠的前兆。
Time ticked by. The sound of the train never stopped — a sign of insomnia.
我嘆口氣，慢慢摸索到門邊，輕輕拉開門，側身閃出去。
I sighed, fumbled to the door, slid it open, and slipped out.
遇見一個半夜上洗手間的中年漢子，我嚇了一跳。
I ran into a middle-aged man heading to the bathroom in the middle of the night and jumped.
因為他雙眼呆滯、表情木然，走路緩慢且隨著火車前進而左右搖晃。
His eyes were glazed, his expression blank; he walked slowly, swaying with the train.
如果你看過電影《禁入墳場》，你大概會跟我一樣，以為他是活死人。
If you've seen *Pet Sematary*, you'd think he was a living dead man too.

「咋出來了？」
"Why'd you come out?"
我轉過頭，暖暖揉了揉眼睛。
I turned. She was rubbing her eyes.
『因為睡不著。』我說。
"Can't sleep," I said.
「那我陪你。」暖暖說。
"Then I'll keep you company," she said.

當為了女朋友而戒煙的男人又開始抽煙時，通常大家都會驚訝地問：
When a man who quit smoking for his girlfriend starts again, everyone's surprised and asks:
「咦？你不是戒煙了嗎？」
"Hey — didn't you quit?"
但我和暖暖則是那種一句話都不說的人。
But Nuannuan and I were the kind who said nothing.
因為我們知道男人又抽煙的背後所代表的意義。
Because we both knew what it meant when a man started smoking again.
所以我和暖暖並不會互相詢問睡不著的理由。
So neither of us asked why the other couldn't sleep.

「輪到我問你一個深奧的問題。」過了許久，暖暖說。
"My turn to ask a deep question," she said after a long while.
『問吧。』我說。
"Go ahead."
「為何不從蘇州回台灣，而要來北京？」
"Why didn't you go back to Taiwan from Suzhou? Why come to Beijing?"
『因為心裡老想著去年夏天在北京的往事，所以我就來北京了。』我說。
"Because I kept thinking about last summer in Beijing. So I came," I said.
「北京魅力真大。」暖暖笑了。
"Beijing really is charming," she smiled.
『不是因為想念北京。』我說，『而是因為想念一個人。』
"It's not because I miss Beijing," I said. "It's because I miss someone."

「我可以繼續問嗎？」暖暖說。
"Can I keep asking?" she said.
『不可以。』我說。
"No."
「那我就不問。」
"Then I won't."
『可是我偏要回答。』我說，『因為想念暖暖，所以我到北京。』
"But I'll answer anyway," I said. "Because I miss Nuannuan — that's why I came to Beijing."
暖暖沒回話，靜靜靠躺著車身，臉上掛著淺淺的微笑。
She didn't answer, just leaned against the train wall, a faint smile on her face.

「我想睡了。」暖暖說。
"I want to sleep," she said.
『妳睡吧。』我說。
"Go ahead."
「你呢？」
"What about you?"
『我無法移動，因為思念的浪潮已經將我吞沒。』
"I can't move — the tide of longing has swallowed me."
「說啥呀。」
"What are you saying?"
『啊！淹到鼻子了，我快不能呼吸了。』
"Ah! It's up to my nose — I can't breathe!"
「你少無聊。」暖暖說。
"Stop being silly," she said.
『滅頂了。』我說，『救……命……啊……』
"I'm drowning," I said. "Hel…p…"
「別在這丟人了。」暖暖拉著我走回包廂，「快睡。」
"Don't embarrass yourself here." She pulled me back to the compartment. "Sleep."

在黑暗中躺回床鋪，閉上眼睛還是沒有睡意。
Back in bed in the dark, eyes closed, still no sleep.
「涼涼。」暖暖輕聲說。
"Liangliang," she whispered.
『嗯？』
"Mm?"
「伸出你右手。」
"Reach out your right hand."
雖然好奇，我還是伸出右手，暖暖左手小指勾住我右手小指。
Curious, I reached out. She hooked her left pinky around my right one.
『做什麼？』我問。
"What are you doing?" I asked.
「你不是說你滅頂了嗎？」暖暖輕輕笑著，「我只好勾你起來。」
"Didn't you say you were drowning?" she whispered with a laugh. "I had to hook you out."

我心裡又覺得暖暖的，全身逐漸放鬆，眼皮開始覺得重了。
My heart felt warm again. My whole body relaxed; my eyelids grew heavy.
「既然咱們勾勾手了，乾脆做個約定。」暖暖說。
"Since we've pinky-sworn, let's make a promise," she said.
『約定？』
"A promise?"
「如果以後你在台灣失眠時，要想起今夜。好嗎？」
"In the future, when you can't sleep in Taiwan, remember tonight. Okay?"
『嗯。』
"Mm."
「晚安。」暖暖說。
"Goodnight," she said.

我和暖暖雙手自然下垂，但依然保持著小指勾住的狀態。
Our hands hung naturally, but our pinkies stayed linked.
我知道醒來後小指一定會分開，但起碼入睡前小指是勾著的。
I knew they'd separate when we woke, but at least they were linked as we fell asleep.
這就夠了。
That was enough.

天亮了，火車抵達北京。
Dawn broke. The train arrived in Beijing.
用不著手機鬧鐘的呼叫，那兩位北京女孩的談笑聲，可以讓我醒十次。
No need for the alarm — the two girls' chatter could have woken me ten times.
「台灣小伙，得說再見了。」高的女孩說，「別哭哦。」
"Taiwan boy, time to say goodbye," the tall one said. "Don't cry."
「千萬別捨不得咱離開。」瘦的女孩說，「咱可是不回頭的花兒呢。」
"Don't be sad to see us go," the slender one said. "We're flowers that never look back."
『不是捨不得。』我說，『是求之不得。』
"I'm not sad," I said. "I'm relieved."
「說啥呀。」暖暖瞪我一眼。
"What are you saying?" She shot me a glare.
這兩個北京女孩邊笑邊走，人影都不見了，我卻還能聽見笑聲。
The two girls walked away laughing, disappearing from sight, but their laughter lingered.

剛走出車站，暖暖得回單位去交差，說了句忙完了再來找我，便走了。
Just outside the station, she had to go report to her office. She said she'd come find me when she was done, then left.
我看著暖暖的背影消失在人群，心裡有種說不出的孤單。
I watched her back disappear into the crowd — an indescribable loneliness filled me.
但我還是得堅強地站著，維持正常的呼吸、心跳和乾燥的眼角。
But I had to stand strong, maintain normal breathing and heartbeat and dry eyes.
因為我得先彩排一下，試著承受這種分離的力道，以免明天正式公演時，
I needed a rehearsal — to practice bearing the force of separation, so that when the real performance came tomorrow,
被這種力道擊倒。
I wouldn't be knocked down by it.

「嘿！」
"Hey!"
突然有人拍了拍我的肩膀，回過頭，暖暖笑吟吟地站在我身後。
Someone suddenly tapped my shoulder. I turned — she was standing behind me, smiling.
我張大嘴巴，又驚又喜。
My mouth fell open — shocked and delighted.
「坐過北京的地鐵嗎？」暖暖笑了笑，「咱們一起坐。」
"Ever ridden the Beijing subway?" she smiled. "Let's take it together."
『妳……』
"You—"
「想給你個驚喜而已。」暖暖很得意。
"Just wanted to surprise you," she said proudly.

暖暖帶著我走進地鐵站，坐2號線轉1號線，王府井站下車。
She led me into the subway — Line 2 to Line 1, getting off at Wangfujing.
離開地鐵站慢慢走回飯店，飯店斜對面有家永和豆漿，我們在那吃早點。
We walked slowly back to the hotel. There was a Yonghe Soy Milk across the street; we had breakfast there.
「永和豆漿在台灣很有名嗎？」暖暖問，「北京好多家分店呢。」
"Is Yonghe Soy Milk famous in Taiwan?" she asked. "There are so many branches in Beijing."
『在台灣，豆漿都叫永和、文旦都叫麻豆、貢丸都叫新竹。』
"In Taiwan, soy milk is always Yonghe, pomelos are always Madou, meatballs are always Hsinchu."
「說啥呀。」
"What are you talking about?"
『意思就是永和豆漿很有名。』我說。
"Meaning Yonghe Soy Milk is famous," I said.

想起去年喝豆汁的往事，同樣是豆字輩的，豆漿的味道就人性化許多，
I remembered last year's *douzhi* experience. Same *dou* family, but soy milk was much more humane —
起碼豆漿不用試煉你的味覺。
at least it didn't torture your taste buds.
『妳比較喜歡豆汁還是豆漿？』我問暖暖。
"Which do you like better — *douzhi* or soy milk?" I asked.
「豆汁。」暖暖回答。
"*Douzhi*," she answered.
『美女就是美女。』我說，『連舌頭都跟別人不一樣。』
"A beauty is a beauty," I said. "Even your tongue is different from everyone else's."
「你少無聊。」暖暖說。
"Stop being silly," she said.

吃完早點，我們走回台灣飯店，然後我上樓，暖暖坐計程車回單位。
After breakfast, we walked back to the Taiwan Hotel. I went upstairs; she took a taxi back to her office.

在前門的襯托下，北京的夜有種迷人的氣質。
Framed by Qianmen, Beijing's night had a captivating quality.
